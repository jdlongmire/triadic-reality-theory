# Expressive Actuality and the Unified Expressive Source

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001
**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Status:** formal synthesis candidate v0.1
**Type:** semantic/ontological bridge
**Controls:** TASR, ADF, ESR, no-smuggling

## 0. Purpose

Introduce **expressive actuality** E as a typed specialization of actuality and test whether it supplies the missing bridge between the thin co-primitive result and a Source-Mind ontology.

This artifact incorporates the proposed vocabulary while tightening five points:

1. E is defined neutrally enough not to presuppose Mind.
2. non-derivative sourcehood is required at the ultimate explanatory level, not for every derivative act.
3. already-actual physical processes may instantiate derivative expression but may not be smuggled into ultimate grounding.
4. algorithms are treated as formally specified transition procedures without assuming a prior personal specifier.
5. self-awareness/reflexivity remains an explicit downstream bridge rather than being inserted into the definition of E or sourcehood.

## 1. Vocabulary

Let:

D = an intelligible representational domain of well-formed contents, distinctions, and admissible consequence/transition relations.

L = logical order governing D.

I = informational content/differentiation within D.

X = generic determinate actuality.

E(c) = expressive actuality of content c.

A = act, the actuality-conferring role.

S = sourcehood.

U = unity/co-unity.

P = an already-actualized physical process.

G_E = a unified non-derivative expressive source.

M = Mind, to be reached only if further mind-specific conditions are established.

## 2. Representational domain

D specifies what counts as a differentiated content, consequence, relation, or distinction.

D does not, merely by being specified, entail that any content is expressively actual:

Spec(D) -/-> exists c E(c).

This preserves:

representability != actuality.

## 3. Expressive actuality

Define E neutrally:

E(c) iff c is determinately instantiated with its content-role preserved.

"Content-role preserved" means that the instantiation is governed by the identity/distinction/relations constitutive of c rather than being merely an unrelated token that an external observer happens to associate with c.

This definition does **not** yet assert:
- consciousness;
- aboutness for a subject;
- assertion by a person;
- semantic interpretation by a mind;
- self-awareness.

Those are downstream questions.

E is therefore stronger than bare occurrence but intentionally weaker than full intentional mentality.

Candidate typing:

E subseteq X.

Every expressive actuality is actual, but not every actuality is thereby established as expressive actuality.

## 4. Act

If E(c) obtains, there is an actuality-conferring role A with respect to c:

E(c) -> exists A Performs(A,c).

"Performs" is provisional notation for the relation by which c is not merely admissible/representable but obtains as E(c).

A must not be defined as merely:

"A = the fact that E occurs."

Otherwise A becomes synonymous with E and loses explanatory content.

## 5. Derivative and non-derivative act

A derivative act may genuinely explain a derivative expression within an already-actual domain.

Therefore reject:

Explains(A,E) -> NonDerivative(A).

Adopt the narrower ultimate form:

UltimateExplains(A,E) -> NonDerivativeSource(A).

A is derivative when its actuality-conferring role depends on an ontologically prior actuality/source.

A is non-derivative when its sourcehood does not depend on a prior E-producing act.

## 6. Explanatory sourcehood

S explains E at the ultimate level iff S originates the relevant A without merely renaming the occurrence of E.

Candidate:

UltimateExplains(A,E)
AND not Synonymous(A,E)
->
exists g Source(g,A).

This inherits GSP-A.

Source remains category-neutral:
- substance;
- process;
- event;
- unified structure;
- mind
are not prejudged.

## 7. No-smuggled actuality

An already-actualized physical process P may instantiate derivative E.

It may also be used analogically under ADF.

But if P ontically presupposes the conditions under investigation, it cannot serve as their ultimate ground:

P in X_phys
AND Presupposes(P, E-conditions)
->
not UltimateGround(P,E).

Thus:

physical expression is permitted downstream;

physical actuality is prohibited as circular first-principles sourcehood.

## 8. Algorithmic insufficiency

An algorithm is a formally specified rule/transition procedure over differentiated states.

No personal specifier is inferred merely from:

Algorithm(x).

Reject the overstrong principle:

Algorithm(x) -> exists y Specifies(y,x)

when "specifies" means a personal selecting agent.

Adopt instead:

Algorithm(x) -/-> E(x)

and:

Algorithm(x) -/-> S(x).

A formal transition relation does not by itself supply:
- actuality;
- semantic/content-role preservation;
- sourcehood;
- invocation;
- ultimate expression.

Algorithms may participate in derivative expression after those conditions are supplied.

## 9. Joint grounding

An ultimate ground of E must account for the conditions that E presupposes.

Candidate workload:

K_E := <D,L,I,A,U,E,S>.

Where discrimination among incompatible actualizable alternatives is required, add S_disc separately to avoid overloading sourcehood S.

Use:

SRC = sourcehood.

SEL = conditional discrimination/selection.

Then:

K_E := <D,L,I,A,U,E,SRC,SEL_cond>.

Functional Adequacy:

UltimateGround(g,E)
->
AdequatelyGrounds(g,K_E).

A posit accounting only for L, only for I, or only for A is incomplete.

## 10. Inexpressibility thesis

L and I can specify order and differentiated content.

They do not by themselves supply expressive actuality:

L AND I -/-> E.

This is the precise sense in which Logic and Information are **inexpressible without act**.

"Inexpressible" does not mean:
- meaningless;
- unrepresentable;
- nonexistent as a domain;
- impossible.

It means:

the pair lacks an actuality-conferring role sufficient for E.

## 11. Unified Expressive Source

The first major convergence target is not yet Mind.

Define:

G_E = a unified non-derivative source that intrinsically grounds D/L/I/A/U/E and any required SEL.

Candidate:

UltimateGround(g,E)
->
UnifiedExpressiveSource(g).

Expand:

UnifiedExpressiveSource(g) iff
SRC(g)
AND CON(g,L,I)
AND ACT(g,A)
AND EXP(g,E)
AND UNI(g,U).

This is an ontological profile.

## 12. Reflexivity and self-awareness

Do not define G_E as self-aware.

Introduce a separate candidate relation:

REF(g): the source's own source-act is available within its intrinsic content without requiring an ontologically external interpreter or a second source.

This is intentionally weaker and more formal than human phenomenal self-consciousness.

The proposed self-awareness bridge is:

UnifiedExpressiveSource(g)
AND REF(g)
?=> MindLike(g).

A stronger claim:

REF(g) -> SelfAware(g)

requires separate argument.

Thus self-awareness is not smuggled into the definition of Mind or E.

## 13. Mind hypothesis

Define the current hypothesis:

H_M:

There exists g such that:

UnifiedExpressiveSource(g)
AND REF(g)

and the resulting ontology warrants MindLike(g).

Formally:

G_E + REF ?=> M.

This remains a convergence hypothesis.

## 14. Personal source

PersonalSource(g) remains downstream.

Candidate additional features:
- intrinsic intentionality;
- self-relation/reflexivity;
- will/agency;
- reasons-as-reasons;
- interpersonal capacity.

Therefore:

MindLike(g) ?=> PersonalSource(g)

remains OPEN.

## 15. Two transcendental tracks

The architecture now distinguishes:

### Generic actuality track

X -> <L,I,A>.

This establishes the thin co-primitives.

### Expressive actuality track

E subseteq X,

with:

E -> <D,L,I,A,U,SRC,...>

subject to the bridge proofs in this artifact.

The expressive track is semantically stronger because its explanandum includes content-role preservation.

This explains why the generic X track may stop short of Mind while E may generate additional semantic/source constraints.

## 16. Transcendental-ontological synthesis

Transcendental side:

Given E, identify the conditions without which E cannot remain E.

Ontological side:

Ask what kind of ultimate ground can intrinsically satisfy and unify those conditions.

Consilient identification:

K_E -> CNS -> G_E

and then:

G_E + REF ?=> M.

Do not write:

Conditions(E) = Being(M)

as an established identity until REF/MindLike is independently discharged.

Use instead:

Conditions(E)
-> constrain UltimateGround(E)
-> G_E
->? M.

UltimateExplains(A,E) -> SRC,

## 19. Falsification / defeat conditions

The expressive-source programme is weakened if:

1. E collapses into bare X with no defensible content-role distinction;
2. content-role preservation can be fully explained without D/L/I;
3. E can obtain without A;
4. ultimate A can remain explanatory while genuinely source-less;
5. an already-actual P can ground E without dependency circularity;
6. algorithmic transition alone supplies E and SRC without borrowed actuality/interpretation;
7. joint grounding does not require U;
8. G_E adds no explanatory content beyond relabeling K_E;
9. REF cannot be defined without presupposing Mind.

## 21. Next bounded target

Formalize **content-role preservation** without importing intentionality.

This is the critical test of E.

If E cannot be defined independently of Mind, the expressive track becomes circular.

If content-role preservation can be specified through identity, truth-aptness, inferential role, reference constraints, or constitutive informational relations without assuming a subject, then E becomes a legitimate independent explanandum and the Source-Mind convergence materially strengthens.
