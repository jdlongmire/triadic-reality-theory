# Theorem Attempt: Necessary Logic and Infinite Transcendent Mind

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Track:** convergence / transcendental mind-ground  
**Status:** theorem attempt v0.1

## Target

Test whether the necessity and irreducibility of fundamental logical law can support the necessity of an **Infinite Transcendent Mind** without importing Kalam, PSR/CGP, or finite-observer idealism.

Notation:

M_T∞ := candidate Infinite Transcendent Mind.

"Transcendent" means ontologically prior to and non-derivative from derivative physical actuality, not spatially located "outside" the cosmos.

"Infinite" is provisional shorthand for unboundedness in the relevant fundamental respects. Infinity is not yet derived and must not be used as a premise to prove itself.

## 1. Corrected cognitive-foundation thesis

Reject the finite-observer formulations:

- no finite mind -> no identity;
- no finite mind -> no noncontradiction;
- no finite mind -> no excluded middle.

Those confuse apprehension with ontological ground.

The stronger thesis is:

> Finite minds apprehend logical law derivatively. The candidate ground of necessary logical law, if mental, must be a non-derivative transcendent Mind whose rational nature is intrinsic rather than acquired from derivative reality.

The direction is:

M_T∞ -> L3

not:

finite minds -> L3.

## 2. Three laws under the Mind-ground model

### Identity

A = A expresses determinate identity.

Mind-ground reading:
A rational Mind does not create identity by observing it. Determinate intelligibility and self-identity are intrinsic to rational actuality.

Candidate essence relation:

RationalNature(M_T∞) -> Identity-constraint.

### Non-Contradiction

not(A AND not-A) in the same respect/time/domain expresses coherent determinacy.

Mind-ground reading:
Non-Contradiction is not enforced by a later observer. Perfect rational coherence would be intrinsic to M_T∞'s nature.

Candidate:

RationalNature(M_T∞) -> NonContradiction-constraint.

### Excluded Middle

A OR not-A in the relevant bivalent domain expresses determinate obtaining/non-obtaining.

Mind-ground reading:
Mind does not impose arbitrary binary buckets on otherwise illogical reality. Rather, determinate content is apprehensible as obtaining or not obtaining under the relevant proposition/domain.

Candidate:

DeterminateRationalContent(M_T∞) -> ExcludedMiddle-constraint

subject to the repository's existing caution about the exact scope of LEM.

## 3. Logical necessity premise

TRT/LRT currently treats the denial of core logical constraint as self-defeating at least for Identity and Non-Contradiction.

## 4. Grounding premise

The key bridge is not "logic exists, therefore Mind."

It is:

G1. Necessary logical law has an ontological ground adequate to its necessity, intelligibility, and unity with I/A.

This is substantive.

H_S answers G1 with primitive necessary structure.
H_M answers G1 with necessary rational Mind.

Therefore G1 alone does not select Mind.

## 5. Essential-ground theorem schema

Suppose:

P1. Box L.
P2. M essentially grounds L.
P3. Grounding of L by M is essential to L: in every world in which L obtains, M is its ground.
P4. There is no alternative ground of L in worlds where M is absent.

Then:

Box L -> Box M.

Proof sketch:
- L obtains in every possible world.
- By P3/P4, wherever L obtains, M exists as its ground.
- Therefore M exists in every possible world.

This is valid conditional on P2-P4.

But P2-P4 carry the central burden. The theorem does not prove Mind from logic alone.

## 6. Contingent-ground problem

Could contingent M ground necessary L in the actual world while L exists ungrounded or differently grounded in other worlds?

Yes, unless grounding identity is essential.

Therefore:

ActualGround(M,L) + Box L

does not entail:

Box M.

The missing premise is **Necessary Ground Identity (NGI):**

NGI: the ontological ground of fundamental logical law is invariant across possible worlds.

NGI requires defense.

## 7. Can NGI be derived from fundamentality?

Candidate argument:

1. L is not a contingent feature of worlds; it is constitutive of world-possibility/actualizability.
2. Therefore L's ground, if it has one, cannot be an intra-world contingent item.
3. Any ground of L must stand prior to the modal domain L constrains.
4. A ground prior to the modal domain cannot vary as an ordinary world-relative contingent constituent.
5. Therefore the ground is trans-world/non-derivative.

This supports **transcendence** and modal priority more strongly than it supports Mind.

It yields candidate:

Ground(L,U) -> Transcendent(U).

To get Mind, the unity/intentionality/rationality arguments must converge.

## 8. Transcendence result

If L is constitutive of what counts as a possible/actualizable world, then no derivative physical system inside a world can ontologically ground L without circularity.

Thus:

Ground(L,U) -> U not-in derivative chi

and:

U ontologically prior to derivative chi.

This is a strong route to T in M_T∞: transcendence as ontological priority.

**Status:** MEDIUM-HIGH within TRT dependency order.

## 9. Mind identification

Why identify U as Mind rather than impersonal structure?

Not from L alone.

The Mind identification requires convergence:

- L: intrinsic rational/logical form;
- I: informational/content dimension;
- A: genuine act;
- C: unified locus;
- intentionality/sourcehood: if thick features survive;
- same-level explanatory unity.

Candidate:

UnifiedGround(U,L,I,A,C) + subject-level features -> Mind(U).

This remains abductive/constitutive rather than a completed deductive theorem.

## 10. Infinity/unlimitedness

"Transcendent Mind" does not yet entail infinite Mind.

Potential routes:
- U grounds the unrestricted representable/modal domain I_infinity;
- U is not externally limited by any prior ontology;
- U's rational/informational scope is not bounded by derivative structures.

But none yet proves absolute infinity.

Therefore the theorem target should temporarily distinguish:

M_T := Transcendent Mind

from:

M_T∞ := Infinite Transcendent Mind.

Current work may reach M_T before infinity.

Logic alone underdetermines its ground.

But the full necessary co-primitive package may constrain the ground more strongly:

plus:

Therefore the stronger theorem target should be:

If L/I/A are transcendentally necessary, necessarily co-unified, and cannot be grounded by derivative systems, then any adequate ultimate ground must intrinsically account for logical form, informational content/potential, act, and unity.

Then test whether M_T is the only currently substantive unified candidate or whether H_S can construct an equally unified impersonal essence.
