# Unity Test: Can One Essence Account for L, I, A, and C?

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Track:** convergence / abductive comparison  
**Status:** first-pass unity test

## Question

Can either principal ground-level hypothesis derive the co-primitives and their unity from one genuinely unified essence rather than nominally packaging independent primitives?

Targets:

- L: logical/admissibility role.
- I: differentiability/informational-potential role.
- A: actuality-conferring/dynamic role.
- C: co-unity/co-requirement of L/I/A in one reality-generating ontology.

Hypotheses:

H_S: primitive impersonal structural ground.
H_M∞: one ultimate, unlimited Mind whose intrinsic nature is logical, informational, and active.

## 1. Standard of derivation

A hypothesis earns unification only if:

1. its proposed essence E is independently intelligible;
2. L, I, A follow from E rather than being appended;
3. C follows from the unity of E rather than being an additional relation;
4. removing one of L/I/A would contradict or materially alter E;
5. E is not merely defined as "whatever has L/I/A/C."

This is an essence-to-aspect test, not a naming test.

## 2. H_S candidate essence

The strongest structuralist formulation is not four brute items. It is:

E_S := one primitive modal-actual structural order.

Candidate aspects:
- differentiation internal to structure -> I;
- consistency/identity constraints internal to structure -> L;
- obtaining/instantiation dimension internal to structure -> A;
- one structural order -> C.

This is stronger than the earlier bare T package and must be treated as the best version of H_S.

### Test S-I
Does "structure" entail differentiation?

Yes in a minimal sense. A structure with no distinguishable positions, relations, states, or identity conditions is difficult to distinguish from no structure.

**Result:** E_S -> I is plausible.

### Test S-L
Does structure entail logical constraint?

At least identity and noncontradiction appear constitutive of a determinate structure. Exact L3 remains stronger.

**Result:** E_S -> minimal L is plausible.

### Test S-A
Does structure entail actuality?

No. An abstract, possible, mathematical, or merely representable structure can be specified without asserting that it obtains as concrete/ontological actuality.

To force A into E_S, H_S must define E_S as an **actual** structural order.

But then "actual" is an additional essential characterization whose explanatory relation to structure remains to be shown.

**Result:** structure alone does not derive A.

### Test S-C
If E_S is genuinely one structure, C may be supplied by its unity for L and I. But because A is not derived from structure simpliciter, unity with A is not yet explained.

**Disposition:** H_S partially unifies L/I, but A remains a distinct burden.

## 3. H_M∞ candidate essence

Use a deliberately non-theological minimal conception:

E_M := an intrinsically contentful rational actuality capable of self-directed or intrinsic activity.

This is a candidate essence of Mind, not yet an established description of ultimate reality.

The test is whether L/I/A are analytic or constitutive aspects of such mentality rather than appended attributes.

### Test M-I
Can mind exist with no differentiation/content whatsoever?

A mind with literally no possible distinction between self/not-self, content/no-content, thought/no-thought, or any state appears indistinguishable from no mind.

Thus some informational/differentiable content-capacity appears constitutive of mind.

**Result:** E_M -> I is strong at the role level.

Caution: this does not yet entail I_infinity or omniscience.

### Test M-L
Can mind be mind while identity and noncontradiction fail absolutely?

A determinate mind requires identity sufficient to be that mind and distinguish its contents/states. Rational mind additionally requires consequence relations and standards of coherence.

**Result:** E_M -> minimal L is strong; E_M as rational mind -> richer L is plausible.

Caution: this does not yet establish that Mind is the ontological ground of logic rather than necessarily conforms to logic.

### Test M-A
Can mind be wholly passive such that no actuality/dynamism belongs to it?

A merely abstract specification of a mind is not a mind that exists. An actual mind necessarily has actuality. But whether **activity** is constitutive of mind is stronger.

If mind is defined as intrinsically active, A risks being inserted into E_M.

Need a non-circular argument.

Candidate: mentality minimally involves occurrence/actuality of awareness, apprehension, intentional state, or self-presence. A mind with no actual mental actuality whatsoever is indistinguishable from a merely possible mind.

This supports alpha (actuality), but not yet A as productive actuality-conferring action.

**Result:** E_M -> alpha is strong; E_M -> A productive/dynamic is plausible but not yet derived.

### Test M-C
If one mind necessarily has rational form, content, and actual mental actuality, their unity is not an external conjunction; they are aspects of one subject.

This gives H_M∞ a candidate explanation of C at the mind-instance level.

But applying that unity to the ultimate co-primitives requires showing that ultimate L/I/A are these mental aspects, not merely analogous to them.

**Disposition:** H_M∞ has a promising unity model, but the ground-level identity remains unproven.

## 4. Comparative result

### H_S
Strongest earned chain:

E_S -> I
E_S -> minimal L
E_S -/-> A without adding actuality/obtaining to the essence.

Therefore H_S currently has a **structure-to-actuality gap**.

### H_M∞
Strongest earned chain:

E_M -> I
E_M -> minimal L
E_M -> alpha
E_M ?-> productive A.

Therefore H_M∞ currently has an **actuality-to-actualizing-action gap**.

Both hypotheses have a remaining A problem, but they are not identical:
- H_S must explain why structure obtains at all.
- H_M∞, if actual mind is granted, must explain why its intrinsic actuality is actuality-conferring/productive beyond itself.

## 5. Important asymmetry

H_S's candidate essence "structure" naturally captures formal differentiation and constraint but not obtaining.

H_M∞'s candidate essence "actual mind" naturally captures a unified actual locus of differentiation and rational constraint, but productive actualization of derivative reality remains additional.

Thus H_M∞ currently has a potential advantage with respect to **C plus alpha**, not yet with respect to full A.

No overall winner is recorded because H_M∞ still owes:
- warrant for Mind at the ultimate level;
- unlimitedness;
- productive A;
- necessity.

## 6. Infinite/unlimited issue

Neither hypothesis gets infinity for free.

I_infinity as unbounded representable informational potential does not entail:
- an actually infinite number of represented items;
- an infinite mind;
- omniscience.

For H_M∞, a route to unlimitedness would need to show that an ultimate mind grounding the full modal/informational domain cannot be externally or intrinsically bounded in the relevant respect.

That is a separate limitation analysis.

## 7. Revised discriminator

The next strongest discriminator is A.

Question for H_S:
> How does merely formal/structural order account for obtaining actuality without adding a separate primitive actuality fact?

Question for H_M∞:
> Is intrinsic activity constitutive of mind in a way that can ground actuality beyond the mind itself, rather than merely describing an already actual subject?

The second question may connect to agency, but agency must not be assumed.
