## Purpose

Define the bridge between the TRT transcendental-ground programme and the classical/modal perfect-being ontological programme without importing maximal perfection into TRT's hard core.

The intended relation is convergence, not premise borrowing.

## 4. Immediate TRT proof obligation: necessary ground

Suppose U adequately grounds the constitutive co-primitive package G.

Question:

[
Could U fail to exist?
]

A merely contingent U raises three possibilities in a world without U:

1. G obtains without U.
2. G has a different ground U'.
3. no determinate actuality obtains.

Case 1 weakens/abandons U as essential ground.

Case 2 creates the Necessary Ground Identity problem.

Case 3 reconnects to the null-world/necessary-actuality analysis.

No case is dismissed by definition. They define the proof obligations.

## 6. From necessity to unlimitedness

If:

[
square M^T
]

is eventually established, the next question is limitation.

For candidate limitation B:

[
B(M^T)=?
]

Classify:

### External limitation
Some ontologically prior/external limiter constrains M^T.

This conflicts with ultimacy in the relevant respect.

### Logically necessary limitation
Example: inability to actualize contradiction as determinate actuality.

This is not a defect or imperfection under DAT because contradictory content is not an actualizable object of power.

### Intrinsic non-logical limitation
A restriction internal to M^T not imposed externally and not required by logic.

This requires explanation under the same explanatory-accounting discipline applied to rival grounds.

Therefore "unlimited" should mean:

> absence of non-logically-necessary limitation in the fundamental respects intrinsic to the ultimate ground.

It must not be defined as "can actualize contradictions."

## 7. Attribute recovery before maximal perfection

TRT must independently test:

- necessary existence;
- transcendence/non-derivation;
- rationality/logicality;
- informational scope;
- act/activity;
- unity;
- sourcehood/agency;
- selection/will;
- unlimitedness;
- uniqueness;
- knowledge scope;
- power scope;
- goodness/value.

No attribute is credited merely because classical theism traditionally assigns it to God.

## 8. Convergence target

Only after independent attribute recovery should the programme ask:

[
M_infty^Tstackrel{?}{=}G_{max}.
]

Shared attributes are necessary evidence for identity but do not by themselves prove numerical identity.

The convergence proof must establish that the referential roles cannot be satisfied by distinct ultimate beings/grounds.

## 9. Why this is not circular

TRT route does not begin:

"possibly a maximally perfect being exists."

It begins with determinate actuality and its typed transcendental conditions.

The perfect-being route begins with its own modal/conceptual premises.

If both independently constrain their referent toward the same unique necessary unlimited rational active ground, convergence gains evidential/theoretical significance.

If they diverge, the divergence is a research result.

## 11. Failure conditions

The convergence programme fails or must stop if:
- H_S supplies an equally adequate nonmental unified ground;
- Mind identification cannot be earned;
- the ultimate ground can coherently be contingent;
- unlimitedness cannot be derived;
- perfect-being premises fail independently;
- recovered TRT attributes conflict with maximal-perfection attributes;
- uniqueness/referential identity cannot be established.

No theological conclusion is protected from these outcomes.
