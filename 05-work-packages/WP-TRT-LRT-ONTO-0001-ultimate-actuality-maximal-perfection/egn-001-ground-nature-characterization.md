# EGN-001: Ground-Nature Characterization

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001
**Date:** 2026-09-19
**Status:** bridge theorem attempt v0.1
**Type:** B/D comparative analysis

## Target

Characterize what an adequate ultimate ground must be intrinsically, rather than merely listing what functions it must perform.

Target bridge:

[
AdequateGroundNature(E_G)
stackrel{?}{Longrightarrow}
MindLike(E_G).
]

MindLike must be independently characterized. It may not mean merely "satisfies the FAA workload."

## 1. Starting constraints

Across the actualizable determinate domain W_D:

[
square_Dexists U,Ground(U,mathcal G).
]

EGI-001 establishes an invariant ground-nature requirement:

[
square_D[
AdequateUltimateGround(U,mathcal G)
ightarrow
Conforms(U,E_G)
].
]

CPIT/FAP require E_G to account non-circularly for:
- L: logical/determinate constraint;
- I: differentiated representability/content;
- A: genuine act/actuality;
- S0 and stronger S where alternative plurality requires discrimination;
- C: co-unity.

## 2. Nontriviality rule

E_G cannot be defined as:

"whatever has L/I/A/S/C."

That would merely rename the workload.

The characterization must identify an independently intelligible kind of ground whose nature explains why the functions are unified.

Candidate kinds include:
- impersonal structure;
- impersonal active structure;
- powers/dispositional ground;
- primitive information-action ontology;
- subject/mind.

## 3. Minimum intrinsic properties

### E1. Determinate self-identity

An adequate ground must itself be determinate enough to be the ground rather than not the ground.

[
Ground(U,mathcal G)ightarrow Determinate(U).
]

This invokes L-role at the ground level.

### E2. Intrinsic differentiated capacity/content

The ground must be adequate to I_R. Merely standing in a relation to derivative information is insufficient if that relation itself leaves the representability/content role unexplained.

At minimum E_G must support determinate distinctions adequate to the representable domain it grounds.

### E3. Intrinsic actuality/act

An inert description or merely possible structure cannot ground X by description alone.

E_G must be actual and possess the A-role intrinsically rather than borrow actuality solely from derivative X.

### E4. Unity

The ground-nature must account for L/I/A as one adequate grounding order.

If distinct internal principles are invoked, CUB applies recursively unless their unity follows from E_G.

### E5. Discrimination where plurality obtains

If multiple incompatible D-states are available, the ground must account for the domain discrimination S required for determinate X.

This need not yet be intentional selection.

## 4. Strongest impersonal candidate

To avoid straw-manning H_S, define:

**IAS, Impersonal Active Structure:**

One non-derivative transcendent ground whose essence intrinsically includes:
- logical/determinate form;
- differentiated informational structure;
- actuality/activity;
- unified organization;
- discrimination among actualizable alternatives where required.

IAS is stronger than ordinary structural realism. It is constructed as the best nonmental competitor to Mind.

Question:

Does IAS remain substantively impersonal once all required capacities are specified?

## 5. IAS and action

If "active" means only:

IAS actually obtains,

then IAS has alpha but not yet an account of discrimination/actualization.

If "active" means:

IAS performs/constitutes acts that determine X,

then IAS contains genuine act.

No contradiction follows from calling such act impersonal.

Therefore:

A-role -/-> Mind

remains.

## 6. IAS and information

If information remains I-thin, differentiated structure, IAS can carry it impersonally.

If I-thick, intrinsic aboutness/reference, is required, IAS must add semantic relations.

But EGN does not yet establish I-thick at the ground level.

Therefore:

I_R -/-> Mind

remains.

## 7. IAS and logic

Constitutive logical determinacy can be modeled as primitive necessary structure.

L alone does not force rational apprehension.

Therefore:

L_D -/-> Mind

remains.

## 8. IAS and selection

Minimal S0 is simply differentiation internal to determinate actualization and does not entail agency.

Stronger S1 becomes relevant only if:
- alternatives are represented as alternatives;
- reasons/ends bear on them;
- selection is attributable to a source.

EGN does not yet establish those thick conditions from S0 alone.

Therefore:

S0 -/-> Mind.

## 9. IAS and co-unity

IAS can stipulate one essence unifying L/I/A/S.

NPR prohibits awarding it simplicity merely from the word "one."

But the same rule applies to Mind.

To defeat IAS, TRT must show either:
- IAS's unity is merely nominal while Mind's is constitutive for independently established reasons; or
- one of the required functions is intrinsically subject-involving.

This is the decisive bridge.

## 10. Independent MindLike markers

Define MindLike independently through the following candidate markers:

### M1. Intrinsic intentionality
Content is about/referential toward an object/state not merely by external assignment.

### M2. Aspectual apprehension
Content can be apprehended as evidence, reason, end, alternative, etc.

### M3. Rational apprehension
Logical/reason relations are not merely instantiated but apprehended as such.

### M4. Subject-sourcehood
Acts/selections are attributable to one locus as its acts.

### M5. Unified subject-locus
Content, rational relation, and act are unified in one subject rather than merely co-located structurally.

A ground need not be humanlike, embodied, temporal, or phenomenal in the human sense to satisfy MindLike.

## 11. Does FAA force M1-M5?

Current result:

FAA/CPIT force thin functional counterparts:
- differentiated content;
- logical constraint;
- act;
- discrimination;
- unity.

They do **not yet deductively force**:
- intrinsic aboutness;
- apprehension-as;
- reasons-as-reasons;
- subject-sourcehood.

Therefore the universal theorem:

[
AdequateGroundNature(E_G)ightarrow MindLike(E_G)
]

is NOT YET DERIVED from thin co-primitives alone.

## 12. But IAS incurs a characterization pressure

To remain impersonal, IAS must stop at thin functions.

If it is strengthened to include:
- intrinsic semantic content;
- reasons-as-reasons;
- intentional discrimination;
- source-attributable act;

then it begins satisfying M1-M5 regardless of whether it is called "structure."

This yields a conditional bridge:

[
AdequateGround(E_G)
land
Thick(I,L,A,S)
ightarrow
MindLike(E_G)
]

where Thick must be independently established.

This is the strongest current route.

## 13. Ground-level thickening question

The decisive issue is no longer whether Mind fits the workload.

It is:

> Does grounding the entire representable/actualizable/actualized order require the ground to contain the thick features intrinsically, or can thin impersonal structure be metaphysically sufficient?

IAS says thin structure is sufficient.

Mind says the unified order is intrinsically rational/contentful/active in the subject-involving sense.

This is the central live fork.

## 14. Possible route from selection to thickening

Where the ground accounts for one determinate actuality among incompatible actualizable alternatives, three models remain:

1. primitive impersonal discrimination;
2. rule/necessity-governed discrimination;
3. subject-originating intentional selection.

Models 1-2 remain coherent without Mind unless reasons/sourcehood are independently required.

Therefore selection alone does not close the bridge.

## 15. Possible route from semantic scope to thickening

If the ground must account not merely for differentiated representations but for **intrinsic semantic truth-bearing content**, then intentionality pressure rises sharply.

But DRT establishes representability, not semantics.

Therefore semantics cannot be imported into E_G without a separate theorem.

## 16. Possible route from rational normativity to thickening

If the ground must account not merely for L_D but for reasons-as-reasons/normative rationality, MindLike pressure rises.

But DAT establishes constitutive logical determinacy, not subject-level apprehension.

Again, a separate theorem is required.

## 17. EGN-001 result

The bridge:

[
AdequateGroundNature(E_G)ightarrow MindLike(E_G)
]

is **NOT YET DEDUCTIVELY ESTABLISHED** from the thin hard core.

A maximally strengthened nonmental rival, IAS, remains logically live:

[

	ext{one transcendent non-derivative active informational logical structure}.
]

However, IAS must pay for:
- intrinsic unity rather than nominal packaging;
- actuality/activity;
- discrimination;
- any semantic/normative/sourcehood thickening added later.

The current Mind advantage remains abductive/unificatory, not deductive.

## 18. Strong conditional theorem

**EGN-T:**

If an adequate ground must intrinsically instantiate:
- determinate semantic aboutness;
- rational relations as reasons;
- source-attributable act/selection;
- unified subject-locus;

then:

[
AdequateGround(E_G)ightarrow MindLike(E_G)
]

under the independent M1-M5 definition.

The antecedent is not yet established.

## 19. Research consequence

The project now has a precise choice.

### Route A: remain thin
Accept IAS as a permanent live rival and present Infinite Transcendent Mind as the best unification hypothesis under FAA.

### Route B: derive thick ground features
Attempt separate transcendental theorems showing that ultimate I/L/A/S cannot remain merely thin if they ground semantic truth, rational inference, and source-attributable actuality.

Only Route B can plausibly convert the Mind bridge from abductive to deductive.
