# Co-Primitive Irreducibility Theorem (CPIT)

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001
**Date:** 2026-09-19
**Status:** theorem candidate v0.1

## Thesis

Logic, Information, and Action are co-primitive relative to determinate actuality D iff each is necessary and no proper pair is sufficient to constitute D.

The theorem establishes functional/constitutive irreducibility. It does not establish that the co-primitives are substances, one substance, or Mind.

## 3. Lemma 2: L + A without I

Assume logical determinacy and actuality/act but remove differentiated informational content.

Without I there is no determinate what for the act to actualize.

Without L:

## 6. What CPIT does not prove

CPIT does not prove:
- L/I/A are substances;
- L/I/A are numerically distinct entities;
- one simple substance bears them;
- Mind;
- necessity of a bearer;
- infinity;
- perfection.

It proves a constitutive failure condition: remove one role and the remaining pair does not yield determinate actuality.

## 7. Selection distinction

CPIT does not establish strong selection S1.

Define:

### S0: minimal actualization differentiation
If act a actualizes i in domain/respect d, then nonidentical incompatible i' is not thereby actualized by that same act in that same respect.

S0 is internal to determinate actualization.

### S1: strong selection
A discriminator chooses/designates among apprehended or available alternatives in a stronger intentional, rule-governed, probabilistic, or volitional sense.

S1 requires the separate selection/sourcehood analysis.

Therefore:

CPIT -> S0 at most.

CPIT -/-> S1.

## 8. Co-unity burden

Suppose a ground theory distributes the roles:

[
g_Lightarrowmathcal L,
quad
g_Iightarrowmathcal I,
quad
g_Aightarrowmathcal A.
]

Because D requires the roles jointly, the theory must additionally account for:

[
C(g_L,g_I,g_A)
]

where C is sufficient co-unity/coordination for the roles to operate as one determinate-actuality constitution.

Thus plurality does not remove the unity problem. It adds a coordination relation.

**Co-Unity Burden (CUB):**

Any adequate grounding account that assigns the irreducible roles to distinct grounds must also non-circularly ground their necessary co-unity.

CUB does not entail a single simple bearer. It establishes an additional explanatory obligation.

## 9. Relation to FAA/CPGT

CPIT strengthens FAA by showing that the functional workload cannot be discharged by omitting one co-primitive.

FAA then asks whether a proposed ground:
- derives all three roles;
- accounts for S where required;
- accounts for C/co-unity.

CPGT may therefore use:

necessity + pair-failure + irreducibility + co-unity burden

before any Mind identification step.
