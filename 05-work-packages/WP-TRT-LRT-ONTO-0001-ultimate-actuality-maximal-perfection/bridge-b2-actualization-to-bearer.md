# Bridge B2: Does Actualization Require a Bearer or Ground?

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Track:** A, pure derivation  
**Target:** B2, A -> U  
**Status:** first pass complete

## Question

Given TRT's primitive actuality-conferring relation/operator A, does A require an ontological bearer or ground U?

The analysis must not infer agency from the word "action," and must not import PSR/CGP, Kalam, axiological premises, or perfect-being theology.

## 1. Neutralize the terminology

For this analysis, read A minimally as:

A = the actuality-conferring relation/operator by which admissible structure is instantiated as actual.

No intentionality, consciousness, temporality, efficient causation, or personal agency is included.

The target U is initially weak:

U = whatever ontological ground, relatum, structure, or bearer is minimally required for A to obtain.

U is not yet "God," a person, a substance, or a maximally perfect being.

## 2. Candidate ontologies of A

### M1: Brute relation
A is a primitive relation/fact. It has admissible structures and actual structures as relata but no further bearer.

Schema:

A(d,x), with d in D and alpha(x).

No U beyond the relation and relata.

### M2: Law/structure
A is grounded in a standing lawlike or structural principle S.

S constrains or constitutes the D-to-X relation.

Here U = S in a weak structural sense.

### M3: Power/disposition
A is the manifestation of an actuality-conferring power P borne by some B.

P(B) -> possible A-manifestation.

Here a bearer is explicit.

### M4: Agent action
A is intentional action of an agent U.

This is the strongest reading and is not licensed by TRT alone.

## 3. Does relationhood entail a bearer?

No.

Relations require relata under ordinary relational ontology, but they do not automatically require a third substance that "bears" the relation.

If A is genuinely primitive, A(d,x) may be ontologically basic.

Therefore:

A -> bearer U

does not follow merely from A being a relation.

**Result:** strong bearer thesis fails from relationhood alone.

## 4. Does operation/function language entail a bearer?

No.

The notation:

A: D -> X

is formal. A mathematical function need not correspond to an agent or substance performing an operation. Treating the arrow as an acting subject would be a category mistake.

**Result:** no bearer from map notation.

## 5. Does "action" entail an actor?

Ordinary grammar suggests actions are actions of something. But using that grammar as metaphysics would beg the question because TRT has not yet established that A is agent-causal action.

If A is defined thinly as actuality-conferring relation, the inference:

action -> actor

cannot be used until action is independently shown to be a manifestation of a power or agency.

**Result:** blocked.

## 6. Relata route

Although A does not require a third bearer, it cannot be a relation with no terms if it is genuinely relational.

At minimum:

A(d,x) -> exists d exists x.

But d is admissible structure and x is actual structure/status. These relata do not yet provide an ultimate actualizer.

A possible reduction is:

A(d,x) is simply the cross-level relation between d-as-admissible and x-as-actual.

If so, A may supervene on the pair without a further U.

This is a coherent countermodel to B2.

## 7. Grounding route

To require U, one needs an additional principle:

**GAP2: Grounded Actualization Principle**

An actuality-conferring relation cannot be an ungrounded brute relational fact; its obtaining must be grounded in an ontological basis adequate to confer/constitute actuality.

Then:

A + GAP2 -> U_ground.

But GAP2 is a grounding principle. It is narrower than unrestricted PSR, yet it is not derivable merely from the existence of A.

This mirrors B1:
- GAP rejects brute alpha.
- GAP2 rejects brute A.

A critic can stop at either level.

## 8. Regress test

Suppose A is grounded in U1, but U1's actuality requires A2 grounded in U2, and so on:

A1 <- U1 <- A2 <- U2 <- ...

Does an infinite regress fail?

Not by formal logic alone. To rule it out we need a well-foundedness principle for ontological grounding or an argument that derivative actualizers cannot collectively supply non-derivative actuality.

Candidate principle:

**WFG: Well-Founded Grounding**

Every instantiated actuality-conferring chain terminates in a non-derivative ground.

WFG would yield a terminal U, but WFG is not currently part of TRT's hard core.

**Result:** regress does not by itself derive U without a grounding/well-foundedness commitment.

## 9. Co-primitive route

Could L, I, and A jointly serve as the ultimate structure, eliminating the need for a bearer U?

Yes, as a coherent TRT ontology:

U_struct := <L, I, A>

where "U_struct" is simply a label for the irreducible co-primitive structure, not an entity standing behind it.

This yields an important alternative:

- **Bearer ontology:** the triad is grounded in U.
- **Structural ontology:** the triad itself is ultimate.

TRT currently appears compatible with the structural ontology.

Therefore any derivation of a distinct U must defeat the structural-ultimate model rather than assume it away.

## 10. Does co-requirement imply unity?

Not numerical unity.

Mutual co-requirement can establish that L, I, and A function jointly in actuality. It does not establish that they are properties or operations of one substance.

Thus:

CoRequire(L,I,A) does not entail exists U [Grounds(U,L,I,A)].

A unity-of-ground principle would be additional.

## 11. Strongest pure-track result

The pure track currently reaches:

Actuality
  -> within TRT, A
  -> A is at least relational/structural
  -> A has relata and a defined ontological role
  X distinct bearer U.

The best non-question-begging endpoint is therefore:

**Ultimate Structural Triad candidate**

T := <L, I, A>

as the irreducible co-required ontology of actualized reality.

Whether T itself requires a ground is not answered by TRT alone.

## 12. Consequence for the original maximal-perfection project

This is a significant negative/clarifying result.

TRT alone does not currently derive a being.

It derives or posits an irreducible structure of actuality.

To move from:

T = <L,I,A>

to:

exists U such that U grounds/instantiates L,I,A

requires at least one further metaphysical bridge.

Possible bridges:
- GAP2: actuality-conferring relations require ontological ground;
- WFG: grounding is well-founded;
- substance/property principle: fundamental operations/properties require a substance;
- agency argument: A is shown independently to be intentional selection;
- semantic/intellect argument: I/L require mind-like ground;
- convergence with contingency or transcendental tracks.

None may be imported into Track A without explicit status change.

## 13. B2 disposition

### Strong bearer claim
A -> U_bearer.

**Status:** FAILED AS STATED / HIGH confidence.

### Weak structural-ground claim
A has an ontological role/relata within T.

**Status:** HIGH.

### Ultimate structural triad
T = <L,I,A> can coherently be treated as ontologically primitive.

**Status:** LIVE COUNTERMODEL / MEDIUM-HIGH.

### Conditional bearer
A + GAP2 + appropriate grounding ontology -> U_ground.

**Status:** CONDITIONAL / MEDIUM.

### Personal/agent bearer
A -> personal U.

**Status:** NOT DERIVED / HIGH confidence.

## 14. Next research decision

The pure derivation track has reached a natural stopping point unless we can derive a bridge from the character of L, I, or A themselves.

The most promising pure-track attack is not generic grounding. It is:

> Does semantic/informational content plus logical normativity require a mind-like ground, thereby defeating the purely structural T model?

That would interrogate the internal character of the co-primitives rather than importing PSR.

Alternatively, freeze Track A here and move to Track B convergence, where contingency and other independent arguments may constrain T/U from outside.
