# Explanatory-Cost Ledger: Primitive Structure vs Unified Mind

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Track:** convergence / abductive comparison  
**Status:** baseline formalization

## Purpose

Prevent explanatory bookkeeping from being distorted by labels such as "brute," "emergent," "structure," or "Mind."

The comparison is between ontologies and their actual explanatory commitments, not the number of nouns used to name them.

## 1. Two controls

### Emergence Presupposition Rule (EPR)

Any emergence relation requires at minimum:

- differentiation between source and result: I-role;
- coherent identity/relation/transformation: L-role;
- if the emergence obtains rather than being merely represented, actuality/actualization: A-role.

Therefore:

Emergence(X,Y) -> (L AND I AND A)-roles.

Consequently, emergence cannot serve as an upstream ontological explanation of the co-primitives themselves without circularity:

(L,I,A) -> Emergence(X,(L,I,A)) -> (L,I,A).

This applies to proposed emergence from matter, energy, quantum fields, computation, complexity, evolution, or any derivative physical system insofar as those candidates already require the co-primitives to be differentiated, related, and actual.

"Emergence" remains legitimate downstream when a non-circular generative relation is specified.

### Bruteness Burden Rule (BBR)

"Brute" is not an explanatory mechanism and does not reduce primitive count.

Epistemic absence of a known explanation:

not KnownExplanation(B)

does not entail the metaphysical claim:

not exists G Explanation(G,B).

A brute terminus is admissible only as an explicit stopping claim after live deeper explanations have been evaluated. Calling a package brute does not convert multiple unexplained commitments into one ontologically simple primitive.

## 2. Structural hypothesis accounting

Let:

H_S: the ultimate ground is primitive impersonal structure T = <L,I,A>.

The hypothesis owes at least:

P1. L: logical/admissibility structure.
P2. I: differentiability/informational potential.
P3. A: actuality-conferring structure.
P4. C: co-requirement/unity relation explaining why L/I/A form one reality-generating ontology rather than three unrelated primitives.

Therefore the explanatory burden is not automatically:

Cost(H_S) = 1

merely because the package is named T.

A more faithful ledger is:

Cost(H_S) >= {L, I, A, C}

unless H_S independently demonstrates that T is ontologically simple and that L/I/A are necessary aspects of one primitive structural essence.

If it does so, that demonstration itself is substantive explanatory work and must be evaluated.

## 3. Unified-Mind hypothesis accounting

Let:

H_M∞: one ultimate, unlimited Mind M∞ has L/I/A as intrinsic unified aspects.

The hypothesis owes:

M1. M∞ exists or is warranted as ultimate ontology.
M2. Mind is substantive and not a relabeling of T.
M3. Nature(M∞) entails or explains L.
M4. Nature(M∞) entails or explains I.
M5. Nature(M∞) entails or explains A.
M6. Nature(M∞) explains C, the unity/co-requirement of L/I/A.
M7. Unlimitedness/infinity is independently warranted.
M8. Necessity, if claimed, is independently warranted.

Therefore:

Cost(H_M∞) != 1

merely because one noun, "Mind," is used.

The potential explanatory gain is conditional:

M∞ -> {L,I,A,C}.

If that implication is earned, H_M∞ unifies several explananda under one ontological ground. If it is merely stipulated, H_M∞ has renamed the package and gains no explanatory advantage.

## 4. Symmetric anti-packaging rule

**Nominal Packaging Rule (NPR):**

Naming a conjunction as one object does not establish ontological simplicity.

Thus neither move is allowed without proof:

<L,I,A,C> -> "one brute structure" -> primitive count 1.

or:

<L,I,A,C> -> "one Mind" -> primitive count 1.

Primitive accounting follows independently warranted ontological dependence, not grammar.

## 5. Explanatory termination

A legitimate terminus may arise in at least three ways:

1. **Necessary essence:** the explanandum follows from what the ultimate reality necessarily is.
2. **Self-explanatory structure:** further grounding would be incoherent or redundant and this is demonstrated, not asserted.
3. **Epistemic terminus:** no deeper warranted explanation is currently available.

Only (1) or (2) supports a strong metaphysical termination claim. (3) is an honest research stopping point but does not establish metaphysical bruteness.

## 10. First unity-test result — 2026-09-19

The best versions of both hypotheses were tested against the essence-to-aspect standard.

**H_S best form:** one primitive modal-actual structural order E_S.

E_S plausibly entails differentiation (I) and minimal logical constraint (L). Structure simpliciter does not entail that it obtains. Calling it "actual structure" adds actuality to the essence unless a non-circular structure-to-actuality bridge is supplied. H_S therefore retains a **structure-to-actuality gap**.

**H_M∞ best form:** an intrinsically contentful rational actual Mind E_M.

E_M strongly supports differentiability/content-capacity (I), minimal/rational logical constraint (L), and actuality status alpha in one subject. It does not yet derive productive actuality-conferring A beyond the mind. H_M∞ therefore retains an **actuality-to-productive-action gap**.

H_M∞ presently offers a promising unity model for L/I/alpha through one subject, but no overall verdict is authorized. The decisive next comparison is whether either candidate can derive productive A without importing GAP/GAP2 or equivalent hidden grounding assumptions.


## 11. Inertness Principle update — 2026-09-19

The comparison now adopts:

**IP, Inertness Principle:** L + I without A is ontologically inert with respect to actuality.

Formally:

I_adm = I_infinity | L,

but:

I_adm -/-> chi

without actuality-conferring efficacy.

This is functional, not rhetorical:
- L supplies constraint;
- I supplies differentiated potential/content;
- A supplies efficacy/actualization.

Accordingly H_S must not receive A by calling formal structure "actual structure." Its best form must independently account for an efficacy dimension in addition to formal/logical-informational order.

H_M∞ is correspondingly sharpened to **one intrinsically active rational informational actuality**. This gives a candidate intrinsic analogue of the three roles plus unity in one subject, but a new distinction is mandatory:

- A_int: intrinsic mental/occurrent activity;
- A_prod: productive actuality-conferring efficacy over derivative reality.

A_int -> A_prod is NOT YET DERIVED.

The next discriminator is whether an unlimited ultimate active Mind can warrant A_prod without inserting productive power by definition.
