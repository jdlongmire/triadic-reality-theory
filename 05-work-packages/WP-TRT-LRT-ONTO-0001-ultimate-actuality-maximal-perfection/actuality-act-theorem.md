# Actuality/Act Theorem (AAT)

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001
**Date:** 2026-09-19
**Status:** theorem candidate v0.1
**Role:** A_X leg for the Co-Primitive Ground Theorem

## Thesis

Actuality is categorically distinct from mere actualizability. If x is actualized, then the actuality role A_X is instantiated: x obtains rather than remaining merely representable or actualizable.

A_X is not defined as physical motion, efficient causation, external production, or temporal transition. Mental act is genuine act; physical instantiation is domain-specific.

## 1. Definitions

Let:
- R(x): x is representable.
- D(x): x is actualizable.
- X(x): x is actualized/obtains.
- alpha(x): actuality status of x.
- A_X: the ontological act/actuality role distinguishing X from D.

Canonical hierarchy:

X(x) -> D(x) -> R(x).

But:

D(x) -/-> X(x).

## 2. Categorical distinction

If actualizable and actualized were identical categories:

D = X,

then every actualizable alternative would be actual.

Where D contains mutually incompatible alternatives, that would collapse the distinction between admissibility and determinate actuality.

Therefore the D/X distinction is substantive.

## 3. A_X as role

A_X answers:

> What is actualized rather than merely actualizable?

At minimum:

X(x) -> alpha(x).

Within TRT, A_X names the act/actuality role by which the X category is not reducible to D.

This is a constitutive role claim. It does not yet prove a separate entity/operator standing behind alpha.

## 4. Brute-alpha countermodel

A reduced ontology can stipulate alpha as a primitive predicate over some members of D:

X = {x in D | alpha(x)}.

This shows:

alpha -/-> a further actuality-conferring bearer/operator

by logic alone.

Therefore AAT must not overclaim. It establishes the necessity of the actuality/act role for X, not an independent bearer of A.

TRT's stronger A primitive remains a hard-core model commitment and is separately defended by GAP/productive ontology arguments.

## 5. Inertness Principle

L + I can constrain and differentiate an admissible domain:

I_R --L_D--> D.

But absent the actuality role:

D -/-> X.

This is IP:

**Logical constraint plus differentiated potential is ontologically inert with respect to actuality unless actuality/act is instantiated.**

"Inert" means insufficient to yield X, not nonexistent.

## 6. Mental/physical action control

Do not define A_X through physical production.

An actual mental act concerning p can obtain while p is not physically instantiated.

Thus:

alpha(Think_M(p))

does not entail:

alpha_phys(p).

A_X is more general than physical-domain action.

## 7. Chance control

A probability measure over D does not entail an actual outcome:

P(x)>0 -/-> X(x).

An actual chance event still requires X/alpha/A_X.

Therefore chance cannot replace the actuality role.

## 8. Emergence control

An actual emergence process E already obtains as an actuality and has determinate source/result relations.

Therefore emergence cannot be the ontologically prior source of actuality as a category without presupposing actuality.

**Actuality Emergence Exclusion (AEE):**

No actual emergence process can generate the actuality-role upstream of the actuality required for that emergence process to obtain.

## 9. Formal schema

For all x:

X(x) -> D(x).

There exist conceivable/representable D-states not entailed to be X-states:

D(x) -/-> X(x).

For actualized x:

X(x) -> alpha(x) AND A_X-role.

Modal form:

**AAT:**

Box[ActualizedReality -> A_X].

This is role-level necessity, not yet Box exists a separate A-bearer.

## 10. Selection interface

Where multiple incompatible members of D exist, X requires discrimination S for domain instantiation.

AAT does not identify S with A_X.

S answers "which?"
A_X answers "actual rather than merely actualizable."

They may be aspects of one act in a Mind model, but that is not built into AAT.

## 11. Epistemic status

- D/X distinction: HIGH.
- Actualized -> actuality role: HIGH as constitutive category claim.
- L+I alone -> X: REJECTED under IP.
- A_X -> separate bearer/operator: NOT DERIVED.
- A_X = physical action: REJECTED.
- AAT Box[ActualizedReality -> A_X]: HIGH at role level.
- AAT -> Mind: NOT CLAIMED.
