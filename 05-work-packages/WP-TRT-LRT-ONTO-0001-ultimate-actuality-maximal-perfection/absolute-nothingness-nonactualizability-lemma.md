## 1. Representation

A rational subject can:
- formulate "absolute nothingness";
- encode existential negation;
- contrast nothing with something;
- reason about the purported content.

Therefore the representation/token/content-description is representable:

[

]

This says nothing yet about actualizability.

## 2. Content of N

Absolute nothingness means:

[

- empty physical space;
- vacuum;
- no matter;
- no spacetime;
- an empty set;
- an empty world-object;
- a null state;
- a boundary condition.

All of those are determinate somethings.

N means absence of all actuality.

## 5. Anti-reification control

Do not introduce:
- W0 = "the null world";
- a null state;
- a zero actuality;
- a nothingness object.

Those formulations risk assigning identity/status to what is defined as absence of all actuality.

Use only the represented content N and its actualizability status.

Thus:

"absolute nothingness is representable"

does not mean:

"there exists a nothingness object."

## 8. What ANNAL does not claim

ANNAL does not claim:
- nothingness is an entity;
- a mind must actualize something;
- PSR;
- Kalam;
- God;
- Mind as presupposition;
- one invariant ground across worlds.

It establishes only that the represented content of absolute non-actuality is not a member of the actualizable determinate domain.
