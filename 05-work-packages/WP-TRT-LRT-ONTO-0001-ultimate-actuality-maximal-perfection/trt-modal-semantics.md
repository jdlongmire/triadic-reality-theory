# TRT Modal Semantics: Representable, Actualizable, Actual

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Status:** working formalization, first pass

## Purpose

Formalize the modal structure already implicit in TRT's four-tier engine before importing any contingency, Kalam, PSR/CGP, axiological, or perfect-being premise.

This document supersedes loose language that identifies I_infinity with Shannon information. Shannon information is downstream of actualization and is not the primitive denoted by I_infinity.

## 1. Categories

### Representable

Let I_infinity denote the unrestricted domain of representable differentiation:

R := I_infinity.

A member of R is a well-formed distinction, relation, configuration, proposition, or structure that can be represented. Representability alone does not entail metaphysical possibility.

Contradiction-encoding structures may be representable:

p AND not-p in R.

### Actualizable / admissible

L3 supplies the admissibility constraint:

D := I_adm = I_infinity | L3.

D is the domain of structures that survive Identity, Non-Contradiction, and Excluded Middle as TRT presently specifies them.

Candidate modal interpretation:

Diamond p iff p in D.

This equivalence is a research target, not yet promoted to theorem. It requires showing that L3-admissibility is sufficient for metaphysical possibility rather than merely necessary for it.

For now the weaker result is:

Diamond p -> p in D.

### Actual

A maps actualizable structure into determinate actuality:

A: D -> X

where X := I_actual and chi is the total actualized reality generated through A.

Thus:

R --L3--> D --A--> X.

### Outcome-actual

Outcome-actuality is a proper specialization within actualized reality for determinate measurement outcomes. It is not needed for the present modal proof except as an example of determinate actualization.

## 2. Shannon information

Shannon information requires an instantiated probability-bearing structure, e.g.

H(X) = - sum_i p(x_i) log p(x_i).

The random variable X, its distinguishable outcomes, and its probability distribution are already specified within an actualized or physically instantiated informational structure.

Therefore Shannon information is downstream:

representable differentiation
  -> L3-admissible differentiation
  -> actualized differentiation
  -> probability-bearing actual structure
  -> Shannon information.

No inference from Shannon theory to the ontological primitive I_infinity is permitted.

## 3. Modal removal tests

### Remove I / representability

Without differentiability there is no representable alternative, no identity of a candidate state, and no modal domain over which alternatives can be distinguished.

Candidate result:

Modal discourse -> differentiability.

Status: STRONG CANDIDATE, not yet formal theorem.

### Remove L / admissibility

Without logical constraint there is no principled distinction between a contradiction that can be represented and a state that can coherently obtain.

Example:

p AND not-p may be representable,
but under L3 it is not actualizable.

Thus:

Representable != Actualizable.

Candidate result:

Metaphysical possibility -> L3-admissibility.

Status: STRONG CANDIDATE.

### Remove A / actualization

Without A, D can remain a domain of admissible possibilities, but nothing follows merely from admissibility about what obtains.

Thus:

D does not entail X.

This is already consonant with the TRT hard core: L3 governs the form of the actual but does not entail the fact of actuality.

## 4. Necessary conditions distinguished

The earlier provisional claim

Box(L AND I AND A)

was too quick.

The present analysis distinguishes:

### Conditions of coherent possibility

There is a strong candidate case for:

Box(ModalDomain -> I AND L).

If every possible world is itself a member or maximal structure in the actualizable domain, then L and I function as necessary conditions for possible-world representation and admissibility.

This does not yet prove that L and I are numerically identical entities instantiated in every world. It establishes necessary structural conditions.

### Condition of actuality

Actuality indisputably obtains:

exists chi.

Under TRT:

exists chi -> A.

So A is necessary for actual reality in the conditional sense:

Box(Actuality -> A).

This is weaker than:

Box A.

The pure derivation frontier is therefore:

Box(Actuality -> A) does not yet entail Box A.

## 5. Revised pure-track dependency

I / representability
  -> domain of differentiated representations

I + L3
  -> admissible / actualizable domain D

D + A
  -> actual domain X / chi

probability structure within X
  -> Shannon information

Modal reading:

Representable(p) does not imply Diamond p.

Diamond p -> Actualizable(p).

Actual(p) -> Actualizable(p).

Actualizable(p) does not imply Actual(p).

Candidate stronger equivalence requiring proof:

Diamond p iff Actualizable(p).

## 6. Key open question

Can A be shown necessary without CGP?

Three candidate routes remain inside the pure track:

1. **World actuality route:** If every metaphysically possible world must be internally determinate as a world, determine whether that requires A at each world or merely representation of that world from a metalanguage.
2. **Actualizer regress route:** If every actualizer were derivative, determine whether an infinite regress can yield actuality or whether a non-derivative actualizer is required.
3. **Co-primitive route:** Determine whether L, I, and A are genuinely co-primitives such that necessary L/I plus the actuality relation entails necessary A, rather than merely contingent A.

None is currently established.

## 7. Anti-equivocation controls

The following substitutions are prohibited without separate proof:

- representable = possible
- possible = actualizable, as biconditional, until sufficiency is established
- informational potential = Shannon information
- actualization = intentional agency
- necessary condition for actuality = necessarily existent entity
- necessary structural role = numerically identical necessary bearer
- co-requirement = ontological identity

## 8. Current disposition

**Established within TRT framework:**
- Representable and actualizable are distinct categories.
- L3 filters representable structure into admissible/actualizable structure.
- A distinguishes admissibility from actuality.
- Shannon information belongs downstream of actualized informational structure.
- Actuality requires A under TRT.

**Strong candidates requiring formal proof:**
- coherent modal possibility requires I/differentiability;
- metaphysical possibility requires L3-admissibility.

**Open:**
- whether L3-admissibility is sufficient for metaphysical possibility;
- whether A is necessary in every possible world;
- whether the three co-primitives require a unified necessary bearer.

**Confidence:** HIGH in the category correction; MEDIUM in the modal interpretation; UNCERTAIN on Box A and unified bearer.
