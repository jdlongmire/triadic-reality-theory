# Research Notes: Ultimate Actuality and Maximal Perfection

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001  
**Status:** first research pass, 2026-09-19

## 1. Historical baseline

### Anselm
In *Proslogion* II-III, Anselm reasons from "that than which nothing greater can be conceived" to existence in reality and then to an existence that cannot be conceived not to exist. The key perfection move is already modal: a being that cannot be conceived not to exist is greater than one that can.

**Primary-source status:** HIGH for the textual claim; accessible translation checked against *Proslogion* II-III.

### Leibniz
Leibniz makes the perfection machinery more explicit. *Monadology* §§38-44 moves from sufficient reason to a necessary substance, then argues that the necessary and independent ground must be unlimited and contain as much reality as possible. §41 identifies perfection with positive reality absent limitation. §§43-44 then make the necessary being the ground of essences, possibilities, and eternal truths.

This is especially relevant to the present project because Leibniz already attempts the direction we are testing: necessary ultimate ground -> unlimited reality -> perfection. The modern project therefore should not be described as wholly novel. The potential novelty is to reconstruct and test that move from TRT/LRT primitives with explicit dependency and circularity controls.

**Primary-source status:** HIGH for *Monadology* §§38-44, 48.

### Gödel
Gödel's 10 February 1970 note takes P(phi), "phi is positive", as primitive. God-likeness is possession of every positive property. Necessary existence is defined through necessary exemplification of essences, and necessary existence is itself axiomatized as positive. The formal route succeeds conditional on the axioms, but the positivity predicate remains load-bearing.

**Primary-source status:** HIGH for the 1970 note as reproduced in Gödel's collected material; manuscript/transcription checked.

### Plantinga
Plantinga's modal version defines maximal greatness through maximal excellence in every possible world and makes possible exemplification of unsurpassable/maximal greatness the load-bearing premise. Plantinga explicitly acknowledges that this premise is unlikely to persuade someone who does not already accept actual exemplification, while defending its rational acceptability.

**Primary-source status:** HIGH for the chapter-level claim from *The Nature of Necessity*, Ch. X, Oxford Academic abstract/metadata. Full text was not available in this pass, so exact page-level formulation remains TO VERIFY.

## 2. TRT/LRT starting point

TRT's hard core currently states:

chi = A(I_infinity | L3)

with:
- L3: finite logical constraint governing admissibility;
- I_infinity: unbounded informational potential;
- A: actualizing action mapping admissible possibility to determinate actuality;
- chi: actualized reality.

The core also explicitly says that L3 governs the form of actuality but does not entail the fact of actuality. A domain of pure unactualized possibility violates no logical law. This is important: the present argument cannot derive actuality from logic alone without contradicting the repository's own distinction.

## 3. Proposed definition

For this work package, define **ultimate actuality**, provisionally, as:

> U is ultimate actuality iff U is sufficient for the actuality of every derivative actual state and U's actuality is not derived from any ontologically prior actualizer or ground.

Formal sketch:

UA(U) := Actual(U)
         AND for every x, if DerivativeActual(x), Ground(U,x)
         AND there is no y such that OntologicallyPrior(y,U) AND Ground(y,U).

This definition deliberately does **not** include necessity, omniscience, goodness, personality, maximality, or aseity as separate hidden clauses. "Non-derivative" is included because ultimacy without it would be contradictory: an allegedly ultimate ground with a prior ground is not ultimate.

## 4. First derivation attempt

### D1. Non-derivative actuality
From the definition of ultimate actuality:

UA(U) -> not exists y [Prior(y,U) AND Ground(y,U)].

**Status:** DERIVED/DEFINITIONAL.  
**Confidence:** HIGH conditional on the definition.

This yields a thin form of aseity or ontological independence. It does not yet yield modal necessity.

### D2. Necessity
Candidate move:

1. U is the sufficient ultimate ground of every derivative actuality.
2. U has no ontologically prior ground.
3. Therefore U cannot fail to exist.

This does **not** follow as stated. "Ungrounded" or "non-derivative" does not by itself entail "necessary." A brute contingent terminus is logically available unless an additional principle excludes brute contingent actuality.

A stronger route is:

P1. Every contingent actuality has a sufficient ground/reason for its actuality.
P2. U is ungrounded by anything ontologically prior.
C1. U is not contingent.
P3. U is actual.
C2. U is necessary.

The load-bearing premise is P1, a Principle of Sufficient Reason or equivalent grounding principle.

**Disposition:** necessity is NOT YET DERIVED from TRT's current hard core alone. The derivation requires an additional metaphysical principle unless TRT's notion of A can independently rule out brute contingent actualization.

**Confidence:** HIGH in this negative finding.

### D3. Unity / uniqueness
If two ultimate actualities U1 and U2 existed, neither could be ontologically prior to the other by definition. Mere plurality is not contradictory. Uniqueness therefore does not follow merely from non-derivative ultimacy.

Possible routes require further premises, e.g.:
- ultimate grounding is total and non-overlapping;
- two unrestricted ultimate grounds would either limit each other or be indiscernible;
- necessary beings with identical unrestricted grounding roles collapse by identity conditions.

Each requires argument.

**Disposition:** NOT YET DERIVED.

### D4. Rational / logical adequacy
L3 is a necessary constraint on actuality in TRT. If U is sufficient for all derivative actuality, U cannot actualize contradictions as outcome-actual states without violating the hard core. Thus:

UA(U) AND TRT -> U's actualizing efficacy is L3-conforming.

This establishes **logical adequacy/conformity**, not intellect. A lawlike or structural ground could satisfy logical constraint without being a mind.

**Disposition:** logical adequacy DERIVED conditional on TRT; intellect NOT DERIVED.

### D5. Informational adequacy
If U is sufficient for the actualization of every derivative informational distinction, then U must be adequate to the informational content actualized through A. This supports a relation of informational sufficiency.

It does not yet establish that U "knows" propositions. Moving from informational adequacy to knowledge requires intentionality, representation, truth-directed cognition, or another semantic bridge.

**Disposition:** informational sufficiency plausibly DERIVED; omniscience NOT DERIVED.

### D6. Action and agency
TRT treats A as primitive actualizing action. Action in the minimal TRT sense is sufficient to distinguish actuality from mere possibility. However, "action" does not automatically mean intentional personal agency.

To derive agency requires showing that selection among admissible possibilities cannot be exhaustively characterized as impersonal transition, law, stochastic selection, or brute actualization.

**Disposition:** actualizing efficacy DERIVED from TRT; personal agency NOT DERIVED.

### D7. Goodness
Nothing in L3, I_infinity, A, or chi as currently specified contains an axiology. Objective moral value may require an ultimate ground, but that premise is external to the present TRT hard core.

**Disposition:** goodness and moral perfection NOT DERIVED.

### D8. Unlimitedness / maximal perfection
Leibniz's move is instructive: a unique, universal, necessary substance with nothing independent outside it is argued to be incapable of limitation and to contain as much reality as possible. The weak point for our reconstruction is the inference from ultimacy/necessity to absence of all privative limitation.

TRT presently gives no theorem that every limitation of U would require an external limiter. Some limitations may be essential or constitutive rather than externally imposed. We therefore cannot yet infer "maximal reality" from "ultimate actuality."

**Disposition:** maximal perfection NOT YET DERIVED.

## 5. Interim result

The first pass does not derive a maximally perfect being from TRT/LRT. It does something more useful at this stage: it identifies the exact bridges that would have to be justified.

Current dependency frontier:

TRT triad
  -> determinate actuality
  -> candidate ultimate actuality
      -> non-derivative actuality [DERIVED by definition]
      -> L3-conforming actualization [DERIVED conditional on TRT]
      -> informational sufficiency [PLAUSIBLE]
      -> necessity [BLOCKED: needs PSR/equivalent]
      -> uniqueness [BLOCKED]
      -> intellect/omniscience [BLOCKED: semantic/intentional bridge]
      -> agency/personhood [BLOCKED: action-to-agency bridge]
      -> goodness [BLOCKED: axiological bridge]
      -> unlimitedness/maximal perfection [BLOCKED: limitation principle]

The important finding is that the Gödel/Plantinga burden has not simply disappeared. It has been decomposed. Instead of one primitive "positivity" or one large "possibly maximally great" premise, the burden appears as several smaller metaphysical bridge principles that can be examined independently.

## 6. Strongest current research direction

The next step should focus on the **necessity bridge**, because necessity gates the classical perfect-being route.

Research question:

> Does TRT's distinction between possibility and actuality, together with A as the primitive actualizing principle, entail that the ultimate source of A cannot itself be contingently actual, or is a PSR-like principle genuinely additional?

If necessity survives without importing PSR, the programme has a distinctive result. If it requires PSR, that should be stated explicitly and the argument becomes a hybrid transcendental/cosmological-perfect-being argument rather than a pure derivation from TRT.
