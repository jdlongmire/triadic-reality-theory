# Consilient Source-Mind Argument

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001
**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Status:** convergence argument v0.1
**Type:** abductive/consilient identification, downstream of transcendental and ontological constraints
**Controls:** TASR, ADF, ESR, no-smuggling

## 1. Claim type

This argument does not claim:

L -> Mind

I -> Mind

A -> Mind

or:

L AND I AND A -> Mind

as a simple deductive theorem.

Its force is consilient: independently established requirements converge on a candidate ontology capable of satisfying them jointly with minimal unexplained remainder.

The argument therefore has three levels:

1. transcendental necessity;
2. ontological constraint;
3. consilient identification.

These levels must not be collapsed.

## 2. Level T: transcendental necessity

From determinate actuality X:

X -> (L AND I AND A).

Where:

L = logical determinacy/admissibility.

I = differentiated representability/informational content-role.

A = act/actuality rather than mere admissibility.

This establishes role-level necessity relative to X.

## 3. Level O: ontological constraint

Role necessity creates a grounding burden.

The source/bearer and co-unity analyses add:

C = intrinsic unity/co-ordination of the roles.

SRC = non-derivative sourcehood for A if A is genuinely explanatory rather than synonymous with X.

EXP = determinate expression/actualization of informational differentiation under logical order.

S = discrimination where mutually incompatible actualizable alternatives obtain for one actuality domain.

Thus the accumulated constraint set is:

K := <L,I,A,C,S_cond,SRC,EXP>.

No member of K is defined as Mind.

## 4. Inexpressibility thesis

Logic and Information can specify an intelligible representational domain, but they possess no intrinsic actuality-producing efficacy merely by being logical and informational.

Therefore:

L AND I -/-> X.

More precisely:

L AND I AND not-A

contains no actuality-producing role internal to the pair.

This is the sense in which L and I are "inexpressible" absent act: they may be representable as capacity/order, but they do not by themselves express themselves as actuality.

Do not interpret "inexpressible" as "unrepresentable."

## 5. Sourcehood consequence

If A is retained as an explanatory co-primitive rather than a synonym for X, GSP-A supplies:

NonDerivative(A)
AND Explanatory(A,X)
AND not Synonymous(A,X)
->
exists g Source(g,A).

Functional Adequacy then requires the ultimate source to ground L and I as well.

Therefore the candidate source must satisfy:

Source(g,A)
AND Grounds(g,L)
AND Grounds(g,I)
AND Grounds(g,C).

Where S applies, it must also account for discrimination.

## 6. Containment and expression

Define ontological containment:

CON(g): L and I are intrinsic/non-external to g's grounding adequacy.

Define expression:

EXP(g): g is capable of determinate actualization of informational differentiation under logical order through intrinsic act.

Then:

UltimateGround(g,X)
->
CON(g) AND SRC(g) AND EXP(g) AND UNI(g).

This remains a first-principles ontological constraint.

## 7. Consilience

The question now changes from:

"Does any one role entail Mind?"

to:

"What ontology jointly satisfies the independently derived constraint set K?"

Candidate impersonal structural ground H_S must supply, without hidden additions:

- intrinsic logical order;
- intrinsic informational differentiation/content;
- genuine non-derivative act;
- intrinsic unity;
- determinate expression;
- conditional discrimination;
- sourcehood.

A bare tuple <L,I,A> does not satisfy this burden merely by notation.

If H_S introduces separate primitives for each function plus a coordination relation, its explanatory cost must be counted.

## 8. Source-Mind candidate

Define a minimal source-mind candidate M_s:

M_s is one intrinsically unified source whose nature is:
- rational/logically ordered;
- informational/contentful;
- active;
- capable of determinate expression;
- capable of discrimination where alternatives require it.

Formally:

M_s := UnifiedSource<L,I,A,C,EXP,S_cond>.

The notation is a candidate ontological model, not an identity theorem.

## 9. Why Mind is a candidate

Mind is proposed because the source profile requires one ontology in which rational order, content, act, unity, and expression are intrinsic rather than externally assembled.

The argument is not:

"human minds exhibit these features, therefore the ultimate ground is Mind."

That inference is prohibited by ADF.

The argument is:

1. first-principles analysis independently generates K;
2. candidate ontologies are tested against K;
3. M_s offers a unified realization of K;
4. rival ontologies remain live if they can satisfy K with equal or greater explanatory adequacy and without hidden primitives.

Thus Mind enters at the identification stage, not the transcendental premise stage.

## 10. Personal source qualification

"Personal" is not yet derived merely from M_s.

PersonalSource(g) requires additional warrant, potentially involving:
- intentionality;
- self-relation;
- will/agency;
- reasons-as-reasons;
- interpersonal capacity.

Therefore:

M_s ?=> PersonalSource(g)

remains OPEN.

This prevents the phrase "personal source mind" from importing downstream attributes into the consilience argument.

## 11. Consilience criterion

The source-mind hypothesis gains warrant to the extent that independently derived constraints converge without ad hoc auxiliary primitives.

Evaluation criteria:

1. coverage of K;
2. intrinsic rather than borrowed satisfaction of K;
3. unity of the roles;
4. primitive count;
5. absence of circular grounding;
6. no collapse of A into brute X;
7. no external selector for S;
8. no reification of representable I into an unexplained Platonic realm;
9. compatibility with the transcendental results;
10. explanatory remainder.

No numerical score is authorized at this stage.

## 12. Rival burden

H_S remains a legitimate rival.

But "primitive impersonal structure" is not itself a completed rival explanation.

It must specify what the structure is such that:
- information is intrinsic;
- logic is intrinsic;
- act is genuine;
- sourcehood is internal;
- expression is possible;
- unity is non-external;
- discrimination is accounted for where required.

If the answer merely repeats those functions as primitive labels, H_S has restated K rather than unified it.

## 13. Consilient inference

The present inference is:

X
->T
<L,I,A>
->O
K
->CNS
M_s as candidate best-unified source ontology.

Where:

T = transcendental derivation.

O = ontological constraint derivation.

CNS = consilient/abductive identification.

Only T and the established portions of O carry deductive/transcendental force.

CNS remains defeasible.

## 14. Defeat conditions

The Source-Mind consilience is weakened or defeated if:

1. a nonmental ontology intrinsically unifies K with no greater unexplained primitive burden;
2. A can be explanatory while genuinely source-less;
3. L/I can be actuality-producing without A;
4. C requires no grounding or unity relation;
5. EXP reduces completely to brute obtaining without explanatory loss;
6. S is shown unnecessary where incompatible alternatives obtain;
7. "Mind" adds no content beyond relabeling K.

## 15. Analogy policy

GenAI, computers, human cognition, language, organisms, and other physical actualities may illustrate capacity/activation/expression distinctions.

They have zero first-principles weight.

No downstream IS may be used to derive K or M_s.

## 16. Current disposition

Transcendental L/I/A: established/canonical within current WP framework.

Source/unity/expression burden: strong ontological candidate, with GSP-A explicitly qualified.

Mind as unified ontology of convergence: live consilient candidate.

Personal Mind: not yet derived.

Christian God: not inferred at this stage.

## 17. Canonical summary

> Determinate actuality transcendentally requires Logic, Information, and Action. Those roles do not become a self-subsisting ontology merely by being named or represented as a tuple. If Action is genuinely explanatory rather than synonymous with the brute fact of actuality, it carries a source burden; an ultimate source must also intrinsically ground the logical and informational conditions of the actuality it explains, together with their unity and any required discrimination. Logic and Information without act remain capacity/order rather than self-expression as actuality. The independently derived constraints therefore converge on the requirement for an intrinsically unified, rationally ordered, informationally contentful, active source capable of determinate expression. Source-Mind is proposed as a unified ontology of that convergence. This is a consilient identification, not a deduction from any single co-primitive, and remains defeasible by an impersonal ontology that can satisfy the same constraint set without circularity, borrowed actuality, or equivalent unexplained primitives.
