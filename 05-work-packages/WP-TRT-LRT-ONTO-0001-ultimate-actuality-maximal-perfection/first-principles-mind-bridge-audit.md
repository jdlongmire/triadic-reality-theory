# First-Principles Mind Bridge: Functional Derivation Audit

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001
**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Status:** first-principles bridge audit v0.1
**Controls:** TASR, DSIR, ADF, ESR

## 1. Target

Test:

AdequateUltimateGround(g,G) ?=> MindLike(g)

where:

G = <I_R, L_D, A_X, S, C>.

No physically actualized mind, brain, computer, organism, language system, or other member of X_phys is permitted as a first-principles premise. Such systems may be used only as analogy after a relation is independently derived.

## 2. Starting obligations

An adequate ultimate ground g must account non-circularly for:

I_R: differentiated representability/content-role.

L_D: logical determinacy and admissibility.

A_X: actuality/act rather than mere admissibility.

S: discrimination where mutually incompatible actualizable alternatives are relevant to one determinate actuality domain.

C: co-unity of the roles in the actuality-generating order.

These are functional obligations. None is initially labeled mental.

## 3. Candidate minimal MindLike profile

To avoid defining Mind into the conclusion, introduce candidate features separately:

U: unified locus/source.

RAT: intrinsic rational/logical adequacy.

CONT: intrinsic content-bearing capacity.

ACT: intrinsic act/sourcehood.

DISC: intrinsic discrimination among alternatives where S applies.

INT: intentional aboutness.

SUBJ: subjecthood.

AG: agency.

The bridge succeeds only to the extent these features are independently derived.

## 4. Co-unity to unified locus

C requires the reality-generating roles to be jointly coordinated in one adequate ground-order.

This does not entail one substance or subject.

Therefore:

C ?=> U

is plausible only in the weak sense that the ground must provide one non-circular explanatory unity. It does not yet yield numerical simplicity or personal unity.

**Status:** U_weak DERIVED/STRONG; U_subject NOT DERIVED.

## 5. L_D to rational adequacy

If g grounds L_D, g cannot violate the logical conditions it grounds.

Therefore:

Ground(g,L_D) -> Conforms(g,L_D)

is a strong candidate.

But:

Conforms(g,L_D) != Apprehends(g,L_D).

Thus intrinsic logical adequacy follows more readily than intellect.

**Status:** RAT_thin STRONG; intellect NOT DERIVED.

## 6. I_R to content-bearing adequacy

If g adequately grounds differentiated representability, the relevant distinctions cannot be wholly absent from the ground-account.

Functional Adequacy yields:

Ground(g,I_R) -> AdequateFor(g,I_R).

This supports intrinsic informational/differentiational adequacy.

It does not yet establish:

- semantic aboutness;
- knowledge;
- representation by a subject;
- propositional attitudes.

**Status:** CONT_thin STRONG; INT/knowledge NOT DERIVED.

## 7. A_X to intrinsic act/sourcehood

An ultimate ground of A_X cannot be wholly derivative with respect to actuality.

Thus:

Ground(g,A_X) -> NonDerivativeActRole(g).

This is stronger than inert abstract structure.

It still does not establish intentional agency.

**Status:** ACT_thin STRONG; AG NOT DERIVED.

## 8. S to discrimination

Where the target actuality domain contains mutually incompatible actualizable alternatives, S requires discrimination sufficient for one determinate actuality rather than an incompatible plurality.

Therefore an adequate ground must account for:

DISC(g,D_alt,X).

But discrimination does not by itself entail conscious choice, will, reasons, or libertarian freedom.

**Status:** DISC STRONG conditionally; will NOT DERIVED.

## 9. Joint integration test

The strongest first-principles result presently available is not Mind.

It is an integrated profile:

P_g := <U_weak, RAT_thin, CONT_thin, ACT_thin, DISC_conditional>.

An adequate ground must be capable of unifying:
- logical adequacy;
- informational differentiation/content capacity;
- non-derivative act;
- conditional discrimination;
- co-unity.

This profile is more specific than bare impersonal structure but still does not contain intrinsic semantics or subjecthood.

## 10. Can subjecthood be derived from integration alone?

Candidate:

U + CONT + RAT + ACT + DISC -> SUBJ.

This implication is not presently valid.

An impersonal unified structure could be stipulated to instantiate all five thin roles unless contradiction or explanatory inadequacy is shown.

Calling their unity a "subject" would be terminological smuggling.

**Result:** SUBJ NOT DERIVED.

## 11. Can intentionality be derived?

Candidate:

CONT + DISC -> INT.

No. Differentiated content and discrimination do not by themselves establish aboutness.

Candidate:

CONT + RAT -> INT.

No. Logical relations among informational distinctions do not by themselves establish reference or intentional directedness.

**Result:** INT NOT DERIVED from thin profile.

## 12. Can agency be derived?

Candidate:

ACT + DISC -> AG.

No. Actualization plus discrimination can remain an impersonal actuality relation unless source-attribution, intentionality, or reasons are independently established.

**Result:** AG NOT DERIVED.

## 13. First-principles stopping point

The pure bridge currently reaches:

AdequateUltimateGround(g,G)
->
IntegratedRationalInformationalActiveDiscriminatingGround(g)

in a thin functional sense.

It does not yet reach:

MindLike(g)

if MindLike includes subjecthood, intentionality, intellect, or agency.

This is a valid stopping point.

## 14. Rival structural hypothesis

H_S remains live:

One non-derivative unified impersonal ground intrinsically instantiates the thin functional profile P_g.

To defeat H_S deductively, one must show that at least one required role is irreducibly subject-involving or semantic/agentive.

No such result follows from the current thin co-primitives alone.

## 15. What would move the bridge

Only same-level first-principles results are admissible. Candidate routes:

1. show that I_R at the ultimate level requires intrinsic semantic determination rather than bare differentiation;
2. show that L_D includes irreducible normativity/intelligibility requiring apprehension;
3. show that S cannot be adequately grounded by impersonal discrimination;
4. show that C requires subject-unity rather than structural co-unity;
5. show that A_X requires sourcehood with agentive rather than merely actualizing character.

Each is a separate proof obligation.

## 16. ADF compliance

No downstream physical example is used as a premise in sections 4-15.

Any later analogy to human cognition, computation, software, biological systems, or physical causation is illustrative only and must be explicitly labeled ANALOGY.

## 17. No-smuggling audit

Prohibited and not used:

- Information = knowledge.
- Content = intentionality.
- Logic = intellect.
- Logical conformity = rational apprehension.
- Action = agency.
- Discrimination = choice.
- Co-unity = subject.
- Ultimate = necessary.
- Non-derivative = personal.

**Audit:** PASS.

## 18. Disposition

Derived/strong from functional obligations:
- weak explanatory unity;
- logical/rational adequacy in the thin sense;
- informational/differentiational adequacy;
- non-derivative act-role;
- conditional discrimination.

Not derived:
- subjecthood;
- intrinsic intentionality;
- intellect/knowledge;
- will;
- agency;
- consciousness;
- personhood.

Therefore the canonical Mind bridge must be weakened to:

AdequateUltimateGround(g,G)
->
P_g

and:

P_g ?=> MindLike(g)

remains OPEN.

## 19. Next bounded target

The next investigation should not enumerate examples. It should test one class-level proposition:

> Does objective logical normativity belong to L_D itself, or only to rational agents reasoning about L_D?

If normativity is intrinsic to L_D and cannot be reduced to truth-preserving structural relation, that may thicken RAT toward intellect. If normativity belongs only to downstream reasoners, it cannot support the first-principles Mind bridge.

This is the next bounded bridge because it directly tests a load-bearing transition and satisfies ESR.
