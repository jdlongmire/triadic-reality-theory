# Canonical Category Backbone: Representable, Actualizable, Actualized

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Status:** canonical category model v0.1

## Purpose

Fix the categorical backbone for subsequent co-primitive theorem work and prevent equivocation among information, possibility, admissibility, and actuality.

## 1. Three categories

### R: Representable

R is the domain of differentiated structures that can be represented.

Primitive role:

I_role := differentiation / representational informational potential.

I_role answers:

> What can be represented as a distinguishable structure?

Representability does not imply metaphysical possibility or actualizability.

A contradiction-encoding structure may be representable:

Representable(P AND not-P).

Therefore:

Representable(x) -/-> Actualizable(x).

### D: Actualizable

D is the domain of representable structures that satisfy all conditions required for possible actualization.

L3 supplies at least necessary logical admissibility constraints.

Therefore:

Actualizable(x) -> Representable(x) AND L3(x).

Do **not** yet assert the converse:

Representable(x) AND L3(x) -> Actualizable(x)

because logical consistency may be necessary without being sufficient. Additional modal, mathematical, relational, or domain constraints may apply.

Thus:

D subseteq (R | L3).

The exact criterion for D remains a formalization target.

### X: Actualized

X is the domain of structures that actually obtain.

Primitive role:

A_role := act / actualization.

A_role answers:

> What is actualized rather than merely actualizable?

Therefore:

Actualized(x) -> Actualizable(x).

But:

Actualizable(x) -/-> Actualized(x).

The actualized domain is chi:

X = chi.

## 2. Canonical hierarchy

The canonical inclusion/dependency hierarchy is:

R ⊇ D ⊇ X

with:

I_role -> R.

L3 constrains R toward D.

A_role distinguishes/actualizes X from D.

Schematic:

I_infinity
  --L3 + any further admissibility constraints-->
I_actualizable
  --A-->
chi.

For compact conceptual use:

R --L--> D --A--> X,

with the explicit caveat that L3 may not be the complete sufficiency criterion for D.

## 3. Three governing questions

I-role:
**What can be represented?**

L-role:
**What can be actualized?**

A-role:
**What is actualized?**

These questions are categorical and must remain distinct.

## 4. Necessary implications

Adopt:

Actualized(x) -> Actualizable(x).

Actualizable(x) -> Representable(x).

Therefore:

Actualized(x) -> Representable(x).

Also adopt from DAT:

Actualized(x) -> L3(x)

under exact same-respect predication.

Candidate:

Actualizable(x) -> L3(x).

This is stronger upstream use of L3 and should be reviewed against the exact definition of actualizability, but it is consistent with the DAT conception of logical determinacy as necessary for any determinate actuality.

## 5. Prohibited collapses

Do not infer:

Representable = Actualizable.

Actualizable = Actualized.

I_infinity = Shannon information.

Representable = semantically meaningful.

L3-admissible = physically realizable.

Mental apprehension = physical instantiation.

Possible = actual.

Actualizable = selected.

Selected = physically instantiated without a domain bridge.

## 6. Relation to selection

Where D contains mutually incompatible alternatives, selection S is downstream of representability and admissibility:

R --L--> D --S/A_d--> X_d.

S does not create the alternatives or their logical admissibility.

S discriminates among already actualizable alternatives for a target actuality domain.

The exact relation between S and A remains domain/model dependent.

## 7. Mind correspondence

The Mind hypothesis maps naturally but provisionally:

I_role -> content/differentiation available to apprehension.

L_role -> rational/logical constraint on coherent content.

A_role -> genuine mental act.

C -> unity of content, rational form, and act in one subject.

Physical instantiation remains downstream through PIB/S and is not the definition of A.

## 8. DAT interface

DAT governs X:

Actualized(x) -> L3(x).

The category backbone adds the upstream target:

Actualizable(x) -> L3(x).

DAT does not claim every L3-consistent representation is actualizable.

Thus:

Actualizable(x) -> L3(x)

but not yet:

L3(x) -> Actualizable(x).

## 9. Co-primitive theorem interface

The co-primitive theorem should no longer be written vaguely as "L/I/A make reality."

Use:

1. I_role is necessary for representability/differentiation.
2. L_role is necessary for actualizability/determinate admissibility.
3. A_role is necessary for actuality rather than mere actualizability.

Target schema:

Actualized(x)
->
[Representable(x)
 AND Actualizable(x)
 AND L3(x)
 AND A_role].

At the role level:

Box[ActualizedReality -> (I_role AND L_role AND A_role)].

## 10. Research obligations

### R theorem
Formalize why any representable/actualized x requires differentiation sufficient for identity/reference.

### D theorem
Formalize why any actualizable x must satisfy L3 and determine what additional constraints, if any, are required for sufficiency.

### X theorem
Formalize A_role without reducing act to physical production or defining actuality circularly.

### Ground theorem
Determine what can non-stipulatively unify the three necessary roles and their co-requirement.
