# Co-Primitive Source-Bearer Bridge

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001
**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Status:** first-principles bridge candidate v0.1
**Controls:** TASR, ADF, ESR, no-smuggling

## 1. Motivation

The co-primitives L, I, and A have been established as irreducible functional/ontic roles relative to determinate actuality. That result alone does not supply an ontology.

A tuple:

T = <L,I,A>

is a representation of required roles. Tuple notation does not itself establish a self-subsisting bearer, source, or actual ground.

The present bridge asks:

> What must be true for L, I, and A to be more than explanatory role-descriptions?

## 2. Role/ontology distinction

Adopt:

NecessaryRole(r,X) != SelfSubsistingOntology(r).

A necessary condition of actuality is not thereby an independently actual entity.

Therefore the move:

L, I, A are necessary roles
therefore
<L,I,A> exists as primitive impersonal structure

is invalid without an additional ontological account.

This applies equally to a Mind hypothesis. Mind may not be inserted merely because the tuple needs a label.

## 3. Bearer/source burden

If determinate actuality X obtains, the roles are not merely described. They are instantiated or grounded in the actuality-generating order.

Introduce:

Grounds(g,r)

for g adequately grounding role r.

Functional Adequacy requires:

UltimateGround(g,X)
->
Grounds(g,L) AND Grounds(g,I) AND Grounds(g,A).

The co-unity burden additionally requires that their joint operation not be externally coordinated by a more ultimate unexplained relation.

Thus:

UltimateGround(g,X)
->
GroundsUnified(g,<L,I,A>).

This is a bearer/source requirement at the explanatory level. It does not yet specify substance, subject, person, or mind.

## 4. Logic without source/bearer

L by itself is constraint/order.

Even if logical relations are objective, L alone supplies no differentiated content and no actuality-conferring act.

Therefore:

L alone -/-> X.

Treating L as an abstract object does not solve A.

Treating L as a brute law does not explain what instantiates or expresses the law in actuality.

## 5. Information without source/bearer

I_R supplies differentiated representability.

An effectively unbounded representational domain must not be reified merely from its representability.

Rep(x) -/-> Exists(x).

Likewise:

DomainOfRepresentability(I_R) -/-> IndependentPlatonicRealm(I_R).

If I is proposed as ontologically fundamental, the proposal owes an account of how differentiated informational content is actual rather than merely a represented domain.

## 6. Action without source

A is especially resistant to bearerless treatment.

If A is genuine act/actualization rather than a symbol for the fact that X obtains, then the theory owes a source of act.

Otherwise:

A := "actuality happens"

risks becoming a restatement rather than an explanation.

Candidate Source Principle:

SP-A:
Genuine non-derivative act requires a non-derivative source of act.

This is a bridge candidate, not yet promoted theorem.

It must be tested against event ontologies and process ontologies that deny enduring bearers.

## 7. Containment

Define containment minimally:

Contain(g,L,I)

means that the logical order and informational resources required for the actuality grounded by g are intrinsic to g's grounding adequacy and are not supplied by an ontologically prior external source.

Containment does not mean spatial containment.

It means ontological non-externality.

For an ultimate ground:

UltimateGround(g,X)
->
not ExistsPrior(h, Supplies(h,L_or_I,g)).

Thus L and I cannot be borrowed from a more ultimate external realm without g ceasing to be ultimate in the relevant respect.

## 8. Expression

Define expression minimally:

Express(g,i,L,A,X)

means that g is the source through which informational content i, under logical constraint L and act A, obtains as X.

Expression does not initially mean linguistic expression, conscious thought, intention, or voluntary choice.

Candidate schema:

Contain(g,L,I)
AND Source(g,A)
AND Express(g,I|L,A)
-> X.

The arrow is explanatory/grounding, not temporal.

## 9. Intrinsic unity requirement

If separate grounds are proposed:

g_L -> L
g_I -> I
g_A -> A,

then their coordinated production of one determinate actuality requires C.

If C is external to all three, the alleged grounds are not jointly ultimate without grounding C.

If C is primitive in addition to them, primitive count rises and the theory owes an account of why the four primitives form one actuality-generating order.

The unified-ground hypothesis therefore has a genuine explanatory target:

One g intrinsically grounds <L,I,A,C>.

This does not prove one substance, but it sharpens the unity burden on pluralist structural rivals.

## 10. Source profile

The first-principles profile is now:

SRC(g): g is non-derivative source of act.

CON(g): L and I are intrinsic/non-external to g's grounding adequacy.

EXP(g): g can express/actualize informational differentiation under logical order.

UNI(g): the roles are intrinsically unified rather than externally coordinated.

Then:

AdequateUltimateGround(g,G)
->
SRC(g) AND CON(g) AND EXP(g) AND UNI(g).

This is the strongest current source-bearer profile.

## 11. Does this entail minimal agency?

Define minimal agency neutrally:

Agent_min(g) := g is a unified source to which an act expressing determinate content is attributable.

Then:

SRC + CON + EXP + UNI ?=> Agent_min.

This implication is a serious candidate.

It does not yet entail:
- consciousness;
- phenomenology;
- deliberation;
- libertarian freedom;
- moral responsibility;
- personhood;
- human-style cognition.

The remaining question is whether an impersonal process/source can satisfy SRC+CON+EXP+UNI while genuinely being the source of content-expressive act.

## 12. Impersonal structural rival

H_S must now do more than posit the tuple <L,I,A>.

It must specify an ontology h such that:

1. h is actual/non-derivative;
2. L is intrinsic to h;
3. I is intrinsic to h;
4. A is intrinsic to h;
5. h accounts for their unity;
6. h is genuinely sourceful rather than merely a description of X;
7. h can ground determinate expression/actualization without importing a further selector/source.

If H_S satisfies all seven, it remains live.

If it cannot, "primitive impersonal structure" is incomplete rather than an equal explanatory rival.

## 13. Mind-like bridge

The source profile now approaches MindLike from first principles more directly:

MindLike_min(g) :=
UnifiedSource(g)
AND IntrinsicLogicalOrder(g)
AND IntrinsicInformationalContent(g)
AND ContentExpressiveAct(g).

Candidate:

AdequateUltimateGround(g,G)
?=>
MindLike_min(g).

This definition is intentionally minimal.

The no-smuggling test asks whether "content-expressive act" has already imported intentionality. If expression means only actualization of informationally differentiated content, the definition remains pre-intentional. If it means expression *about* something, intentionality has been smuggled.

Therefore MindLike_min remains a candidate label for the source profile, not yet a completed Mind theorem.

## 14. "Effectively nothing" clarification

The phrase "without a source agentic mind able to contain and express them, you effectively have nothing" is interpreted technically as:

> Without an actual grounding source, L/I/A remain descriptions of necessary ontological roles and do not by themselves constitute an explanatory ontology capable of grounding X.

This does not mean L/I/A are literally nothing.

It means role descriptions alone do not discharge the source/bearer burden.

## 15. No-smuggling audit

No physical actualized system is used as a first-principles premise.

No inference uses:
- computer execution;
- brains;
- biological information;
- human cognition;
- language;
- physical causation;
- physical law as instantiated law.

Analogies remain permitted under ADF but carry zero derivational weight.

No equation is made between:
- information and knowledge;
- expression and intentionality;
- action and agency;
- unity and subjecthood;
- source and personhood.

**Audit:** PASS.

## 16. Main result

The prior stopping point:

AdequateUltimateGround(g,G) -> P_g

is strengthened by an explicit source/bearer obligation:

AdequateUltimateGround(g,G)
->
UnifiedIntrinsicSourceOf(L,I,A)(g).

The open bridge is narrowed to:

UnifiedIntrinsicSourceOf(L,I,A)(g)
?=>
Agent_min(g)
?=>
MindLike(g).

This is preferable to attempting to derive Mind independently from thickened L, I, or A.

## 17. Next bounded target

Test the Source Principle SP-A and the minimal-agency implication at the ontology-class level:

> Can genuine non-derivative act be bearerless/source-less, or does "act" require something to which the act is attributable?

Only event ontology, process ontology, substance ontology, and structural ontology need to be compared as classes. ESR prohibits unbounded instance enumeration.

The result should determine whether Agent_min is derivable or whether sourcehood remains an additional primitive.


## 18. ANALOGY: Dormant Generative AI and intrinsic sourcehood

**Status:** ANALOGY ONLY under ADF.  
**Derivational weight:** ZERO.

A dormant generative-AI system provides a useful downstream illustration of the already-derived distinction among informational capacity, logical/relational constraint, activation, and actual output.

Schematically:

\[
I_{model}+L_{architecture}\nRightarrow A
\]

and therefore:

\[
I_{model}+L_{architecture}\nRightarrow X_{output}.
\]

Model weights do not invoke themselves. Representational capacity does not entail an actual inference event.

A derivative execution chain can be represented approximately as:

\[
I_{model}
+
L_{architecture}
+
A_{runtime}
+
I_{prompt}
+
S_{invocation}
\rightarrow
X_{output}.
\]

This is not a model of ultimate ontology. Every term in the example is already physically actualized and externally dependent.

Its legitimate use is narrower: it illustrates the categorical distinction:

\[
\text{capacity}\neq\text{activation}\neq\text{actualized result}.
\]

### Distributed-role lesson

The example also illustrates what distributed grounding looks like.

The model does not intrinsically supply:
- its hardware;
- electrical power;
- runtime;
- invocation;
- prompt;
- training history;
- architecture;
- external causal environment.

Its logic/information capacity is therefore inert with respect to a new output until derivative activation conditions obtain.

Again, this carries no first-principles proof weight.

### Return to first principles

The independently derived ultimate-ground requirement differs categorically.

If \(g\) is ultimate, the explanation cannot terminate with:

\[
Contain(g,L,I)
\]

plus an external:

\[
h\rightarrow Activate(g).
\]

If \(h\) is ontologically prior and supplies the actuality-producing act, then \(g\) is not ultimate with respect to \(A\).

Therefore, from the source/bearer argument rather than from the GenAI analogy:

\[
UltimateGround(g,X)
\rightarrow
IntrinsicSource(g,A).
\]

Combined with containment:

\[
UltimateGround(g,X)
\rightarrow
Intrinsic_g(L,I,A,C).
\]

The analogy may then be used to make this result intuitive:

> Logic and information describe capacity. Capacity does not become an actualized result merely by being capacity. Derivative systems receive activation from outside themselves. An ultimate ground cannot outsource the actuality-producing source without ceasing to be ultimate in that respect.

### ADF audit

The following inference is prohibited:

> GenAI requires external activation; therefore ultimate reality requires an intrinsic agent.

The permitted order is:

1. first-principles source/bearer analysis establishes the intrinsic-source requirement;
2. the GenAI case illustrates the difference between capacity and actualization;
3. no property of GenAI is transferred upstream as an ontological premise.

**ADF status:** PASS.
