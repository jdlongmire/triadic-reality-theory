# Action-Scope Correction: Mental Act and Physical Instantiation

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Status:** corrective ontology note

## Core correction

Earlier analysis implicitly privileged physically external production as the fuller sense of action. That hierarchy is not warranted by TRT.

A mental act is fully an act. Its actuality does not depend on physically instantiating the intentional content of the act.

Example schema:

alpha(Think_M(p)) = true

does not entail:

alpha_phys(p) = true.

A thought about p can be actual while p is not physically actual.

## 1. Three distinctions

### Act actuality
The occurrence/obtaining of an act itself.

Example: M actually thinks p.

### Intentional/content actuality
The content p is actually entertained, apprehended, represented, judged, intended, etc. as content of the act.

This still does not imply that p obtains as its referent.

### Referential/domain instantiation
The represented content p obtains in some target ontological domain, e.g. physical reality.

Thus:

actual mental act concerning p
  -/-> physical instantiation of p.

## 2. Revised reading of A

A should not be defined by physical production.

At the most general level:

A = primitive act / actuality-bearing operation or occurrence under logical constraint over informational content.

Domain-specific actualization is a specialization of A, not the definition of A.

Candidate schematic:

chi_d = A_d(I_adm,d)

where d denotes an actuality domain or mode.

Examples:
- d = mental: actual apprehension/thought of content;
- d = physical: physical instantiation;
- other domains are not assumed, merely left formally open.

This is provisional and requires later formalization.

## 3. Representation is not referent instantiation

For a mind M:

Represent_M(p) AND Act_M(p)

does not entail:

p obtains in the physical domain.

Therefore an ultimate mind can, in principle, actively apprehend an admissible possibility without that possibility becoming a physically actual world/state.

This blocks an important modal-collapse error.

## 4. Necessary Mind and contingent physical reality

Candidate model:

Box M∞.

Box [M∞ is rational, informational, and active].

M∞ actively apprehends a domain D of admissible possibilities.

Only a selected/subset relation S determines which admissible contents are physically instantiated:

S subseteq D.

Therefore:

Box M∞

does not entail:

Box chi_phys.

The existence and intrinsic activity of a necessary Mind can be necessary while a derivative physical actuality remains contingent, provided the physical-instantiation relation is not itself necessitating all admissible content.

No claim about free will is required yet. The point is purely modal: actual mental content does not entail actual physical referent.

## 5. Consequence for the co-primitives

The correspondence with Mind is strengthened:

- L: rational/logical form of thought;
- I: content/differentiation available to thought;
- A: thinking/apprehending/judging/intending as genuine act;
- C: unity of form, content, and act in one subject.

Physical action is downstream and domain-specific. It may instantiate the same primitive action category under physical constraints, but it does not define A.

## 6. Consequence for H_S vs H_M∞

H_S must still account for:
- constraint;
- differentiated content/potential;
- act/actuality;
- their unity.

It may not reduce A to physical transition because physical transition already occurs downstream in chi_phys.

H_M∞ has a stronger intrinsic-unity model:

Mind-in-act is simultaneously:
- rationally formed;
- contentful;
- active;
- unified as one subject.

This does not yet prove M∞ is ultimate, infinite, necessary, or the ground of physical reality. It establishes that Mind is a genuine same-category model of the triad without privileging physical manifestation.

## 7. Terminology cleanup

Withdraw or deprecate where misleading:
- "productive A" as though non-physical mental action were non-productive or deficient;
- "external action" as the criterion of genuine action;
- "A_prod" as the privileged completion of A.

Retain instead:
- A_M: mental act;
- A_phys: physical-domain actualization/action;
- A_d: domain-indexed act/actualization where needed;
- alpha_d: actuality status in domain d, if formalization requires it.

No ontological hierarchy among these is assumed merely from the indexing.

## 8. Open bridge

The remaining question is not:

Does mental action become real action?

It is:

> What relation, if any, connects the acts/content of M∞ to derivative physical instantiation?

Call this provisional bridge:

**PIB: Physical Instantiation Bridge**

PIB(M∞, p, chi_phys).

PIB must be separately established. It may eventually involve will, selection, decree, grounding, causation, or another relation. None is assumed here.

## 9. Anti-collapse controls

Prohibited inferences:

- M thinks p -> p physically obtains.
- p is admissible -> p is mentally entertained.
- p is mentally entertained -> p is physically instantiated.
- necessary Mind -> necessary physical creation.
- unlimited informational content -> all content physically actualized.
- action -> physical action.
- mental action -> lesser or derivative action by definition.

## 10. Disposition

- Mental act is genuine action: ADOPTED as category correction.
- Mental-act actuality != physical-referent actuality: HIGH.
- A should not be defined by physical production: ADOPTED provisionally.
- Domain-indexed A_d: FORMALIZATION CANDIDATE.
- Necessary active Mind -> necessary physical world: REJECTED as invalid without PIB/necessitation premise.
- PIB: OPEN.
