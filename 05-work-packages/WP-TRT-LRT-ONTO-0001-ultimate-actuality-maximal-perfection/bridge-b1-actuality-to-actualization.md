# Bridge B1: Does Actuality Require Actualization?

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Track:** A, pure derivation  
**Target:** B1, alpha -> A  
**Status:** first pass complete

## Question

Given an admissible structure d in D = I_adm and the fact alpha(d) that d actually obtains, does the distinction between merely admissible and actual require an actuality-conferring relation A?

The analysis must not import PSR/CGP. The issue is constitutive, not explanatory in the unrestricted sufficient-reason sense.

## 1. Minimal formal setting

Let:

- R(x): x is representable.
- D(x): x is admissible/actualizable under L.
- alpha(x): x is actual / obtains.
- A(d,x): d stands in the actuality-conferring relation to actual x.

TRT requires:

alpha(x) -> D(x)

for actual structures, and denies:

D(x) -> alpha(x).

Therefore admissibility and actuality are distinct statuses.

## 2. The brute-status model

A critic may propose:

BSA: Some d is admissible and simply has alpha. There is no further actuality-conferring relation A. Actuality is a primitive monadic status.

Formally:

exists d [D(d) AND alpha(d) AND not exists a A(a,d)].

Is BSA contradictory?

No contradiction with Identity or Non-Contradiction follows merely from the statement. Therefore B1 cannot be established by elenchus alone.

## 3. Definitional danger

If A is defined as "whatever relation makes alpha true," then:

alpha -> A

is true by definition and carries no metaphysical content.

Reject this as a proof.

The research target is whether TRT independently needs A as an ontological primitive in addition to alpha.

## 4. TRT's existing hard-core commitment

TRT currently includes both:

- actuality is a genuine status distinct from possibility;
- A is a primitive actualizing action by which admissible possibility becomes determinate actuality.

Therefore B1 is already part of the TRT hard core at the model level. But the present WP asks whether that commitment is independently warranted or whether alpha alone could replace A.

This is an internal parsimony test on TRT.

## 5. Removal test: eliminate A, retain alpha

Construct TRT-minus-A:

R = representable domain.
L filters R into D.
alpha is a primitive predicate over some members of D.
chi = {x in D | alpha(x)}.

This model preserves:
- representable/admissible distinction;
- admissible/actual distinction;
- determinate actual states;
- the four-tier architecture, with alpha selecting the actual subset.

It does not explain or model why alpha attaches to some admissible structures rather than others.

But lack of explanation is not formal incoherence.

**Result:** A is not logically required merely to represent actuality. A can be eliminated in favor of primitive alpha at the cost of explanatory/ontological structure.

## 6. What A contributes

A contributes more than alpha only if TRT asserts at least one of:

A1. Actuality is grounded rather than brute.
A2. The admissible-to-actual relation has structure that constrains which admissible states are actual.
A3. Actualization is dynamically or ontologically productive.
A4. The same actuality-conferring principle connects quantum outcome selection, cosmological actuality, and other actualization phenomena.

These are substantive commitments. None follows from alpha alone.

Thus B1 depends on a **Grounded Actuality Principle (GAP)**:

GAP: If an admissible structure is actual, its actuality is not an unexplained primitive predicate; actuality is grounded/conferrred through an ontological relation or principle.

Then:

alpha + GAP -> A.

GAP is narrower than CGP:
- CGP concerns every contingent actuality and sufficient reason.
- GAP concerns the ontological distinction between admissible and actual inside TRT.
- GAP does not yet require a reason why this actual state rather than another obtains.
- GAP does not require determinism or necessitation.

## 7. Is GAP already implicit in TRT?

Yes, descriptively. TRT's central identity:

chi ≡ A(I_infinity | L3)

does not merely classify actual members of D. It makes A constitutive of chi.

Therefore removing GAP/A would change TRT's ontology from:

constrained information in action

to something closer to:

constrained information bearing primitive actuality labels.

That is a materially different theory.

So within TRT:

B1 is hard-core by model commitment.

But as a metaphysical derivation:

B1 remains conditional on GAP.

This distinction must be preserved.

## 8. Does GAP collapse into PSR?

No, unless strengthened.

GAP says actuality is ontologically grounded/conferrred rather than a primitive label.

It does not say:
- every fact has an explanation;
- every contingent fact has a sufficient reason;
- A determines uniquely which outcome occurs;
- A itself has a ground;
- A is necessary.

Therefore GAP can be evaluated independently of unrestricted PSR and CGP.

## 9. Quantum indeterminacy check

Suppose two admissible outcomes d1 and d2 have probabilities p1 and p2, and d1 becomes outcome-actual.

GAP requires that outcome actuality be an instance of A.

It does not require A to contain a sufficient reason that necessitates d1 rather than d2.

Thus stochastic/indeterministic actualization remains compatible:

A(D) -> one determinate actual outcome according to non-necessitating structure.

No immediate conflict with quantum indeterminacy follows.

## 10. Ontological economy comparison

### Model M_alpha
Primitives: I, L, alpha.
Actuality is a primitive predicate.

Advantages:
- fewer moving parts;
- sufficient to classify actual vs admissible.

Cost:
- no ontological account of the admissible/actual crossing;
- central TRT claim "information in action" disappears;
- no common actualization structure available for downstream LRM/measurement work.

### Model M_A
Primitives: I, L, A.
alpha is status of A's output.

Advantages:
- preserves TRT's productive ontology;
- actual/admissible distinction has an explicit bridge;
- provides a locus for formalization and empirical descendants.

Cost:
- A is an additional substantive primitive whose warrant must be defended.

Parsimony alone does not decide between them because explanatory role and primitive count trade off.

## 11. B1 disposition

Two senses must be distinguished.

### Internal TRT theorem
Given TRT's hard core:

TRT AND alpha(x) -> A-role(x).

**Status:** DERIVED FROM MODEL COMMITMENT / HIGH.

### Independent metaphysical theorem
alpha(x) -> A-role(x).

**Status:** NOT DERIVED WITHOUT GAP.

Conditional:

alpha(x) AND GAP -> A-role(x).

**Status:** VALID BY THE STATED ONTOLOGY / MEDIUM-HIGH.

## 12. Consequence for the ontological-argument WP

The pure track may use A because A is an explicit TRT primitive. It may not claim that A has been independently proven from the bare existence of actuality.

This matters for later convergence claims. A critic can reject TRT's productive actualization primitive and adopt brute alpha without formal contradiction.

Therefore the next bridge B2 must be framed:

Given TRT's primitive A, does an actuality-conferring relation/action require an ontological bearer or ground U?

That question remains pure-track because it interrogates the ontology of an existing TRT primitive rather than importing an external sufficient-reason principle.

## 13. Confidence

- TRT internally entails A as constitutive of chi: HIGH.
- alpha alone logically entails A: LOW / rejected.
- GAP accurately isolates the additional metaphysical burden: HIGH.
- GAP is true independently of TRT: UNCERTAIN.
- A is preferable to brute alpha as the productive TRT research ontology: MEDIUM-HIGH, methodological rather than deductive.
