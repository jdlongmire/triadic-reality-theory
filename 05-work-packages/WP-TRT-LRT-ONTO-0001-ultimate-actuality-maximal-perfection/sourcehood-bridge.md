# Sourcehood Bridge: From Selection Event to Act of the Mind

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Track:** convergence / mind-ground model  
**Target:** source attribution for S  
**Status:** first-pass analysis complete

## Question

What makes a selection S an act of M∞ rather than an event merely occurring in, alongside, or because of conditions external to M∞?

The target is minimal sourcehood sufficient for agency, not libertarian freedom, moral responsibility, or a full theory of agent causation.

## 1. Distinguish location, causation, and sourcehood

Three claims differ:

1. **Location:** selection occurs in/at M.
2. **Event causation:** prior state r causes selection s.
3. **Sourcehood:** M is the subject to whom s is attributable as M's act.

Location does not entail sourcehood.

A neural event can occur in a finite mind/brain without being an intentional act of the person.

Likewise event causation does not by itself settle sourcehood. A selection may have causal antecedents while still being attributable to an agent, depending on the theory of agency.

## 2. Minimal sourcehood conditions

Candidate conditions for Source(M,S):

### SC1. Subject integration
S belongs to the same unified subject that apprehends the alternatives and relevant reasons.

### SC2. Intentional directedness
S is directed toward an apprehended content/end rather than merely correlated with an outcome.

### SC3. Reason responsiveness
S is intelligibly related to reasons, ends, or considerations apprehended by M.

### SC4. Non-external determination
No ontologically prior external selector is sufficient to make S occur independently of M's own rational activity.

### SC5. Act attribution
S is properly predicable as "M selects p" rather than only "selection p occurs at M."

Together these constitute a candidate minimal sourcehood profile.

## 3. Is sourcehood definitional?

Partly.

"Act of M" analytically includes attribution to M. But the research burden is whether the proposed ultimate Mind model has the metaphysical resources to satisfy SC1-SC5 without adding another primitive selector.

Thus sourcehood cannot be proven merely by changing grammar from "selection occurs" to "M selects."

## 4. External selector test

Suppose an external R fully determines S independently of M.

R -> S.

If R is ontologically prior and sufficient while M is merely the location of the result, SC4 fails.

For an ultimate M∞ this creates a direct problem:

ExternalPrior(R,M∞) -> not Ultimate(M∞)

in the relevant respect.

Therefore a genuinely ultimate Mind cannot have its selection wholly sourced in an ontologically prior external determinant.

This does not exclude intrinsic reasons, intrinsic nature, or non-prior relations.

## 5. Internal deterministic rule

Suppose M∞'s own nature N necessarily entails S:

N(M∞) -> necessarily S(p).

Then S is internal and attributable to M∞ in one sense. Sourcehood may survive.

But if N uniquely necessitates every selection, contingency of derivative physical reality is threatened.

Thus sourcehood and freedom are distinct:
- internal necessity may establish sourcehood;
- it may fail to establish alternative possibility or contingent creation.

## 6. Reasons as determinants

Reasons can relate to selection in several ways:

R1. Reasons necessitate one unique selection.
R2. Reasons constrain a set of rationally permissible selections.
R3. Reasons incline or favor without necessitating.
R4. Reasons are explanatory but not causally efficacious.

Only R1 immediately threatens modal collapse.

R2 is especially important. If multiple alternatives are equally or sufficiently consistent with perfect rationality, then:

PerfectRationality(M∞) does not entail unique S.

This would permit:

Box M∞ AND Box Rational(M∞)

without:

Box chi_phys.

The key question is whether multiple rationally permissible world-level selections are coherent.

## 7. Principle of sufficient reason pressure

A critic may argue:

If p and q are equally rationally permissible and M selects p, why p rather than q?

If the answer requires a sufficient differentiating reason, then either:
- there is such a reason, potentially restoring unique necessitation; or
- selection contains an irreducible agent-originating determination.

This is the classic pressure point between strong PSR and robust volitional contingency.

The current WP must not silently assume either resolution.

## 8. Agent-originating determination

Candidate:

**AOD:** A rational subject can be the non-derivative source of a selection among multiple rationally permissible alternatives, where reasons constrain/permitted the act without uniquely necessitating it.

If coherent, AOD supplies:
- sourcehood;
- contingency;
- reason responsiveness;
- no external selector.

This is close to a minimal agent-causal/libertarian structure, but the label is withheld until objections are tested.

AOD is not derived from TRT. It is a candidate solution to the sourcehood + modal-collapse problem.

## 9. Does AOD become "brute choice"?

Potential objection:

If no reason uniquely determines p over q, selection of p is brute.

Response to test:

"Not uniquely necessitated by reasons" does not entail "unreasoned" or "uncaused." The agent may be the source, with reasons providing intelligible sufficient permissibility rather than a unique sufficient determinant.

But this requires a substantive ontology of agent causation/sourcehood. Otherwise "the agent chose" could become another gap-accounting label.

Therefore AOD must earn explanatory content by showing how agent-sourcehood differs from S0 brute selection.

## 10. Distinguishing AOD from brute S0

S0:
- no subject-level reason relation required;
- no intentional directedness required;
- no source attribution beyond the event;
- no rational constraint required.

AOD:
- unified subject apprehends alternatives;
- alternatives are intentionally represented;
- reasons constrain/rationalize the selection;
- subject is source of the determination;
- outcome is attributable as the subject's act.

Thus AOD is structurally richer than brute selection even if reasons do not uniquely necessitate the outcome.

Whether that richer structure is metaphysically coherent remains open.

## 11. Ultimate Mind sourcehood candidate

For M∞:

If M∞ is:
- one unified subject;
- rationally apprehensive of alternatives;
- intrinsically active;
- not governed by an ontologically prior external selector;

then SC1, SC2, and SC4 fit naturally.

SC3 follows if selection is reason-responsive.

SC5 follows only if sourcehood is established rather than asserted.

This gives H_M∞ a natural locus for sourcehood but not yet a proof.

## 12. Impersonal H_S comparison

H_S can implement S via:
- brute outcome;
- deterministic rule;
- stochastic measure;
- optimization criterion.

But "sourcehood" in the agentive sense is not required by H_S.

Therefore sourcehood is not a universal explanatory obligation unless independent evidence establishes agency/will at the ground level.

H_M∞ incurs the sourcehood burden because it proposes Mind as explanatory ground. Its potential payoff is unifying selection with the same subject that apprehends alternatives and reasons.

## 13. Disposition

- Location -> sourcehood: NO.
- External prior sufficient selector compatible with ultimate M∞: NO in the relevant respect.
- Internal necessary determination compatible with sourcehood: YES.
- Internal necessary determination compatible with contingent creation: potentially NO.
- Multiple rationally permissible selections: COHERENT CANDIDATE / not yet proven.
- AOD as solution: LIVE / UNCERTAIN.
- AOD = brute selection: NOT BY DEFINITION, but explanatory distinction must be defended.
- Minimal will/agency route through sourcehood: PROMISING.

## 14. Next research target

Run the **Rational Permissibility Test**:

> Can a perfect rational agent face two or more alternatives such that each is fully rationally permissible and no defect follows from selecting either?

If YES:
- necessary perfect rationality need not uniquely determine derivative actuality;
- AOD/volitional contingency remains coherent;
- modal collapse can be blocked without irrationality.

If NO:
- perfect rationality uniquely determines selection;
- a necessary perfect Mind risks necessitating physical reality unless some other contingency source exists.

This is the next critical bridge.
