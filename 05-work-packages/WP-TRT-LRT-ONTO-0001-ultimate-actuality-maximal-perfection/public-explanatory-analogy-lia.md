## Purpose

Govern the public explanation of the Logic-Information-Action framework without weakening the category distinctions established in the formal TRT/LRT corpus.

The public-facing narrative is maintained separately in `lia-storyline.md`. This artifact supplies the precision layer, formal mapping, theological boundary, and anti-overreach guardrails. Where the public narrative compresses distinctions, this artifact and the formal corpus govern.

## 1. Logic: constraint role

An ontological constraint is a constraint on what can obtain in reality rather than merely a rule for how an observer should think.

Under the classical qualification, the same subject cannot both possess and lack the same predicate at the same time and in the same respect.

The public “physics engine” analogy captures the constraint relation only. Logic is not literally software, code, a physical mechanism, or a contingent rule set.

**Public shorthand:** Logic constrains what can be.

## 2. Information: representation, coherence, and realizability

Information supplies differentiated content: the determinate “what” under consideration.

Linguistic expressibility must remain distinct from coherent representability. One can utter “square circle” without thereby specifying a coherent state capable of realization.

The musical analogy supplies three pedagogical stages:

1. **Score:** specified or represented content.
2. **Performable score:** content satisfying the relevant logical and realization constraints.
3. **Performance:** content actually instantiated through action.

The forward sequence

**Rep(x) → Coh(x) → Real(x | Y) → X(x)**

is permitted only as a sequence of questions. It is not an unconditional implication chain.

The formally warranted direction begins with actuality:

**X(x) → Real(x | Y_actual) → Compat(x, Y_actual) → Coh(x) → Rep(x)**

This actuality-first direction is philosophically important. It prevents representability or conceivability from being promoted into metaphysical possibility without the intervening burdens.

**Public shorthand:** Information specifies what it is.

## 3. Action: actuality role

Action supplies the act/actuality role. It must not be reduced to physical motion, external production, or temporal change.

A thought can be actual as a mental act without producing physical motion.

The relevant asymmetry is:

**specification ⇏ obtaining**

Logic plus Information can characterize admissible or differentiated content. They do not entail, merely by doing so, that the content is actual.

**Public shorthand:** Action accounts for its actuality.

## 4. Governed public formulation

The preferred public formulation is:

> Logic constrains what can be. Information specifies what it is. Action accounts for its actuality.

Musical shorthand:

> Score → performable score → performance.

The public derivative should preserve the actuality-first insight in ordinary language even when formal notation is omitted.

## 5. Theological boundary: the Logos

The L-I-A triad must never be identified with the Logos.

The Logos is the uncreated Son of God. He is not Logic, Information, or Action treated as an abstract primitive; not an operator; not a vector, state, field, law, or member of a mathematical ontology; and not contained within the created explanatory framework.

Within the Christian theological extension, the claim is grounding and dependence: the L-I-A structure describes necessary features of created actuality; Christianity identifies their ultimate ground with the uncreated Logos, through whom all things were made and in whom all things hold together.

### Deprecated wording

The following formulation is deprecated and must not be used as a canonical summary:

> “That threefold order, personal and active, is what Scripture calls the Logos.”

Reason: it reads as an identity claim between the L-I-A structure and the Logos, crossing the established created/uncreated and model/ground boundary.

Preferred replacement:

> The L-I-A structure describes necessary features of created actuality. Christianity identifies their ultimate ground with the uncreated Logos, through whom all things were made and in whom all things hold together.

## 6. Guardrails

Do not use the public analogy to assert any of the following without the relevant independent argument:

- that coherence is sufficient for metaphysical realizability;
- that representability entails coherence;
- that specification entails actuality;
- that every instance of Action is temporal change;
- that the L-I-A triad itself is God or the Logos;
- that the analogy proves the personal-ground or Christian-identification bridge;
- that “square circle” names a coherent possible state merely because the phrase is linguistically expressible.
