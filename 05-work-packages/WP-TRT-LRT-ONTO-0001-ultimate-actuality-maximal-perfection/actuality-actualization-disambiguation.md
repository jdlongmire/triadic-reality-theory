# Disambiguating A: Actuality and Actualization

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Track:** A, pure derivation  
**Status:** first-pass ontology clarification

## Problem

TRT currently uses A in several related senses:

1. actualizing action;
2. a principle by which admissible possibility becomes actual;
3. a map A: I_adm -> I_actual;
4. implicitly, the actuality-conferring distinction separating admissible from actual.

These senses cannot be treated as interchangeable in modal proofs.

## Proposed normalization

Separate **actuality** from **actualization**.

### alpha: actuality status/category

Let alpha(x) mean: x obtains; x is actual rather than merely admissible.

alpha is not an event, agent, force, substance, or temporal process. It is the primitive ontological status distinction required by TRT's explicit posit that actuality is genuinely distinct from possibility.

This is the minimal hard-core commitment already present in substance, though not separately symbolized.

### A: actualization operator/relation

Reserve A for the actuality-conferring operation/relation:

A: I_adm -> I_actual.

A expresses the crossing from admissible to actual. Whether A is fundamentally:
- a relation,
- an operation,
- an action,
- a process,
- a capacity,
- or ultimately agency

is a further ontological question.

### chi: actualized reality

chi is the total actual domain/result:

chi = image(A) or, retaining the existing notation, chi ≡ A(I_infinity | L3).

Every member of chi satisfies alpha.

## Revised architecture

R = I_infinity: representable differentiation.

D = I_adm = R | L3: admissible/actualizable differentiation.

A: D -> X: actualization relation/operator.

X = chi: actualized domain.

alpha(x): x has actuality status.

Thus:

R --L3--> D --A--> X
                     |
                     v
                 alpha(x)

This prevents the modal category of actuality from being conflated with the mechanism/relation of actualization.

## Hard-core implications

TRT's existing explicit posit:

"actuality is a genuine status distinct from possibility"

maps naturally to alpha, not to A.

Therefore the strongest current modal claim is:

Box(alpha-role)

in the limited sense that metaphysical modal discourse necessarily distinguishes obtaining from merely admissible/non-obtaining.

This does NOT immediately yield:

Box A

because the necessary availability/category of actuality does not by itself prove that an actualization operator is instantiated or acts in every possible world.

## Co-primitive revision

The phrase "L, I, A are necessary co-primitives" must now be split.

### Necessary modal/ontological roles

Candidate:

Box(L_role AND I_role AND alpha_role).

- L_role: coherent admissibility.
- I_role: differentiability/representability.
- alpha_role: actuality/non-actuality distinction.

### Necessary structure of any actualized reality

Established within TRT:

Box(Actuality -> (L_role AND I_role AND A_role)).

Here A_role is required because actual reality, as distinct from merely admissible structure, requires an actuality-conferring relation in the TRT ontology.

The stronger:

Box A_role

remains open unless alpha and A can be shown inseparable.

## Is alpha a fourth primitive?

Not necessarily.

alpha may be the **codomain status** generated/instantiated by A rather than an additional productive primitive.

Analogy:

- I supplies the candidate structures.
- L constrains admissibility.
- A actualizes.
- alpha names the status of the result.

On this reading, alpha is not a fourth co-primitive alongside L/I/A. It is a formally explicit predicate needed to avoid using A for both the operator and its result/status.

Recommended ontology:

Primitives: L, I, A.
Status predicate: alpha.
Result: chi.

## Does A entail agency?

No.

A remains ontologically thin:

A = whatever actuality-conferring relation is required to account for the difference between an admissible structure and an actual one.

This is deliberately neutral among:
- impersonal primitive transition,
- lawlike actualization,
- stochastic actualization,
- agent causation,
- personal intentional action.

Agency remains a later proof obligation.

## Does A entail temporality?

No.

The arrow D -> X is ontological, not necessarily temporal. "Becomes actual" is potentially misleading if read as a time-indexed transition from a previously existing possibility.

Preferred language:

"A confers/grounds actuality on admissible structure"

or

"A is the actuality-conferring relation from admissible to actual."

Temporal actualization may be a special physical realization of the more basic ontological relation.

## Does A require a bearer?

Open.

Three options remain:

A1. A is primitive relation with no further bearer.
A2. A is grounded in a standing actuality-conferring principle.
A3. A is an operation/action of a bearer U.

TRT's current hard core does not establish A3.

The ontological-argument WP must not move from A to U without a separate bearer argument.

## Modal consequence

The previous NACT result should now be written:

**Necessary Actuality-Distinction Thesis (NADT):**

Box(alpha_role).

This is clearer than Box A and avoids claiming that actualizing action occurs in every possible world.

Candidate co-necessity:

Box(L_role AND I_role AND alpha_role).

Then, for actualized reality:

Box(alpha-instantiated -> A_role)

within TRT, subject to precise formalization of what counts as alpha-instantiation.

## Research frontier

The next question is now sharper:

> Does the necessary actuality distinction alpha require an actuality-conferring ground A, and does A in turn require a bearer U?

This gives two explicit bridges:

B1: alpha -> A.
B2: A -> U.

Neither may be assumed.

If both can be derived, the pure track could move:

necessary modal roles
  -> necessary actuality distinction
  -> actuality-conferring ground
  -> bearer
  -> candidate Ultimate Actuality.

If either fails, the stopping point is recorded.

## Disposition

- A overloading identified: HIGH.
- alpha/A separation: HIGH as a formal clarification.
- alpha as fourth productive primitive: REJECTED provisionally.
- A as non-temporal actuality-conferring relation: MEDIUM-HIGH.
- A -> agency: NOT DERIVED.
- A -> bearer U: NOT DERIVED.
- Box alpha_role: MEDIUM candidate pending formal modal treatment.
- Box A: SUSPENDED as an ambiguous formulation.

## Repository impact

This clarification should first remain local to the WP. If it survives review, a separate change package should audit the TRT hard core, position paper, formalization, and traceability for overloaded uses of A before any global terminology change.
