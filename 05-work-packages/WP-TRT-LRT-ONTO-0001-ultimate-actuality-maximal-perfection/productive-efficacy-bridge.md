# Productive Efficacy Bridge: Intrinsic Activity to Actualization

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Track:** convergence / abductive comparison  
**Target:** A_int -> A_prod  
**Status:** first-pass analysis complete

## Question

If the ultimate candidate is one intrinsically active rational informational actuality M∞, does its intrinsic activity entail productive actuality-conferring efficacy over derivative reality?

Definitions:

- A_int: intrinsic/occurrent activity belonging to M∞.
- A_prod: efficacy sufficient to actualize admissible derivative structures into chi.
- U-ground role: ultimate ontological ground of derivative actuality.

No creation doctrine, omnipotence, will, personhood, or divine perfection is assumed.

## 1. Direct entailment test

A_int alone does not entail A_prod.

Countermodel:

M is an actual, intrinsically active mind whose activity is entirely immanent to M and has no capacity to actualize anything ontologically distinct from itself.

This is coherent at the conceptual level.

Therefore:

A_int -/-> A_prod.

**Status:** FAILED AS A BARE ENTAILMENT.

## 2. Add the ultimate-ground role

The relevant H_M∞ is not merely "an active mind exists." It is proposed as the ultimate ground of the co-primitives and derivative actuality.

Let:

UG(M) := M is ontologically sufficient as the ultimate ground of chi.

Then if chi contains derivative actuality not identical to M, UG(M) requires some grounding relation between M and chi.

If that relation is actuality-conferring, then M has A_prod by role.

Thus:

M + A_int + UG(M) + derivative chi -> A_prod.

But this is conditional on UG(M). It does not independently prove that M is the ultimate ground.

Important: A_prod here is not derived from mentality alone. It follows from mentality plus the ground-role hypothesis.

## 3. Can unlimitedness supply the bridge?

Candidate:

If M∞ is unlimited in actuality-conferring efficacy, then A_prod follows.

This is trivial and circular if "unlimited" already includes productive power.

Therefore unlimitedness must be defined dimensionally.

Let Unlimited_r(M) mean M has no external or intrinsic limitation in respect r.

We would need first to establish that productive efficacy is an essential respect of M before unlimitedness in that respect can be invoked.

So:

Unlimited(M) -/-> A_prod

without specifying and defending the relevant dimension.

## 4. Rational content and action

Could rational informational content plus intrinsic activity yield productive action?

A mind may entertain content and act internally without external causal efficacy. Therefore:

L + I + A_int -/-> A_prod

as a bare conceptual entailment.

However, if A in TRT is not ordinary efficient causation but the ontological actuality-conferring relation, then a candidate ultimate ground must be capable of grounding actuality by definition of the explanatory role.

Again, this supports a **role-fit argument**, not a derivation from mind's essence alone.

## 5. Role-fit versus essence derivation

Two distinct claims must be tracked:

### Essence claim
Nature(M∞) -> A_prod.

Current status: NOT DERIVED.

### Role-fit claim
If M∞ is the ultimate ground of derivative actuality, M∞ must satisfy A_prod.

Current status: HIGH, conditional on the ultimate-ground identification.

This distinction prevents the programme from saying "mind acts, therefore mind creates."

## 6. Could derivative reality be internal to M∞?

A third model avoids "external productive causation":

**Internal-content model:**

Derivative chi is grounded as content/state/expression within M∞ rather than as ontologically external product.

Then A_prod may be an immanent actualization relation rather than efficient causation across an ontological boundary.

This could unify A_int and A_prod more tightly.

But it raises major questions:
- Does this collapse creation into divine mental content?
- Are derivative entities genuinely distinct?
- Does it imply idealism/panentheism?
- Can physical independence and causal structure be recovered?

No such model is adopted. It is recorded because "external production" is not the only possible grounding relation.

## 7. Could productive efficacy be primitive in H_M∞?

Yes, but then H_M∞ would carry:

{Mind, intrinsic activity, productive efficacy}

unless productive efficacy is shown to follow from Mind or ground-role necessity.

That weakens the claimed unification advantage.

The explanatory-cost ledger must count A_prod separately until a bridge is earned.

## 8. Comparison with H_S

H_S must include productive efficacy A as one of its primitive roles because L + I is inert.

H_M∞ naturally contains A_int but still owes A_prod.

Therefore the current asymmetry is:

H_S:
L + I + A_prod + C.

H_M∞:
Mind -> L + I + A_int + C,
plus A_prod debt.

If H_M∞ can derive A_prod from its ground role or essence without circularity, it gains a genuine unification advantage. If not, both hypotheses retain an independent productive-efficacy primitive.

## 9. Ground-role bridge

A cleaner formulation is:

**GRB, Ground-Role Bridge:**

For any U, if U is the ontologically sufficient ultimate ground of derivative actuality chi, then U possesses or instantiates whatever actuality-conferring efficacy is sufficient for chi.

Formally:

UG(U,chi) -> A_prod(U,chi).

GRB is close to analytic in the meaning of "ontologically sufficient ground." It does not establish that any U satisfies UG.

Therefore the research sequence must be:

1. independently warrant M∞ as the best/necessary candidate ultimate ground;
2. then GRB attributes A_prod as required by that role.

Using GRB to prove M∞ is the ground would be circular.

## 10. Does A_prod imply omnipotence?

No.

A_prod over actual chi establishes efficacy sufficient for derivative actuality actually grounded.

It does not establish:
- ability to actualize every logically possible state;
- absence of all power limitations;
- maximal power;
- freedom to actualize otherwise.

Omnipotence remains a later scope argument.

## 11. Does A_prod imply will?

No.

Productive efficacy can be conceived as:
- necessary emanation;
- lawlike grounding;
- spontaneous actualization;
- intentional willing;
- another relation.

Will requires a separate selection/agency argument.

## 12. Disposition

- A_int -> A_prod: **FAILED AS BARE ENTAILMENT / HIGH**.
- UG(M∞,chi) -> A_prod: **HIGH conditional role entailment**.
- Unlimitedness -> A_prod: **NOT WITHOUT DIMENSIONAL WARRANT**.
- A_prod -> omnipotence: **NO**.
- A_prod -> will/personhood: **NO**.
- H_M∞ unification advantage on full A: **NOT YET EARNED**.
- GRB: **ADOPT AS ROLE-DEFINITION CONTROL, not proof of M∞**.

## 13. Next research target

The central problem moves upstream:

> Can M∞ be independently warranted as the ultimate ground of T/chi rather than merely a candidate that would fit the role if assumed?

This returns to the same-level H_S vs H_M∞ comparison.

The most promising discriminator is now C, unity:

- H_S must explain why constraint, informational potential, and productive efficacy are one co-required ontology.
- H_M∞ proposes one subject whose rationality, content, and intrinsic activity are constitutively unified, but still owes the identity between that activity and productive grounding.

A second discriminator is necessity:
- if the co-primitives are transcendentally necessary, which candidate can ground their necessity non-stipulatively?
