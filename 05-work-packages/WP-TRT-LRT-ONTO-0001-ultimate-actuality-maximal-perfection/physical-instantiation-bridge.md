# Physical Instantiation Bridge (PIB)

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Track:** convergence / mind-ground model  
**Status:** first-pass bridge analysis

## Question

Assuming for analysis an ultimate rational informational active Mind M∞, what relation could connect a mental act/content p to derivative physical instantiation without conflating thought with physical actuality or making every thought physically actual?

Target:

PIB(M∞, p, chi_phys).

No doctrine of creation, will, decree, omnipotence, or freedom is assumed at opening.

## 1. Required distinctions

For content p:

1. Representable_M(p): p is available as content.
2. Admissible_M(p): p is logically coherent/admissible.
3. Act_M(p): M∞ actually apprehends/thinks p.
4. Select_phys(p): p is selected/designated for physical instantiation.
5. alpha_phys(p): p obtains in physical actuality.

The critical non-entailments are:

Representable_M(p) -/-> Act_M(p).

Act_M(p) -/-> Select_phys(p).

Select_phys(p) -/-> alpha_phys(p) unless PIB supplies an actuality relation.

Therefore physical reality requires a bridge distinct from mere mental representation.

## 2. Minimal PIB schema

A minimal non-theological schema is:

Act_M(p) AND S_M(p) AND B(M,p) -> alpha_phys(p),

where:
- S_M is a selection/designation relation;
- B is the domain bridge sufficient for physical instantiation.

This does not yet say selection is libertarian will, decree, command, efficient causation, or temporal intervention.

## 3. Why selection is required

If every actively apprehended admissible content were physically instantiated, then all mutually incompatible admissible alternatives could become physically actual together.

For example, if p and q are individually admissible but mutually exclusive in the same physical respect:

Diamond p.
Diamond q.
not Diamond_phys(p AND q).

If:

Act_M(p) -> alpha_phys(p)

and

Act_M(q) -> alpha_phys(q),

then an unlimited Mind apprehending both would generate incompatible physical actuality.

Therefore any Mind-ground model that includes multiple admissible alternatives needs a discriminator between:
- content apprehended;
- content physically instantiated.

Call that discriminator S.

**Selection requirement:** physical instantiation is not identical to mental apprehension.

## 4. Does S imply will?

Not yet.

S could be modeled as:
- intentional choice;
- necessary optimization;
- rule-governed designation;
- value-sensitive selection;
- stochastic selection;
- another primitive discriminator.

However, once S is attributed to a rational Mind rather than an impersonal mechanism, intentional/will-like interpretations become live and should be tested rather than assumed.

## 5. Does PIB imply temporal creation?

No.

The relation between M∞ and chi_phys may be ontological rather than temporally sequential.

"p is selected, then becomes physical" is explanatory ordering, not necessarily time ordering.

Thus:

M∞ ontologically prior to chi_phys

does not entail:

there is a time before chi_phys at which M∞ waits.

## 6. Does physical instantiation require new A?

Not necessarily.

The action category may be one, while its intentional object/scope differs.

Candidate:

A_M(p): mental act with content p.

A_M+S+B(p): same ultimate agent/action grounds physical instantiation of selected content.

This could preserve one A-category rather than adding a second primitive physical-action category.

But this is a model candidate, not established.

## 7. Modal-collapse control

Suppose:

Box M∞.

Box [M∞ is rational, informational, active].

Suppose M∞ necessarily apprehends the full admissible domain D.

It still does not follow:

for every p in D, Box alpha_phys(p).

If S is not necessitated uniquely for every p, physical instantiation may remain contingent.

Even if S is necessary for some p, only those selected contents inherit the relevant necessity.

Thus the collapse question shifts to S:

> Is the selection relation itself necessary, contingent, reason-responsive, or free?

This is where any future doctrine of will would enter.

## 8. Physical law as bridge structure

If physical laws are downstream constraints on instantiated physical reality, they cannot explain why there is physical instantiation at all.

They may govern:

alpha_phys(p) -> lawful evolution of p

but do not by themselves supply:

mental/admissible content -> alpha_phys(p).

Therefore physical law is not PIB unless an argument shows law itself has actuality-conferring status rather than merely constraining already-actual physical states.

This preserves the Inertness Principle at the physical-domain level.

## 9. Relation to TRT

A possible refinement of TRT is:

chi_phys = A_M/S/B(I_adm | L)

but this notation is premature.

The current central identity:

chi ≡ A(I_infinity | L3)

should not be globally rewritten from this WP until:
- the ontology of A is normalized;
- domain distinctions are formalized;
- PIB survives review;
- the Mind hypothesis is independently warranted.

For now PIB remains a model extension, not hard core.

### H_S
Must supply:

### H_M∞
Potentially supplies:

- unity in one subject;
- plus owes S/PIB for physical instantiation.

This reveals a common problem:

**Actuality discrimination:** why this physically actual state rather than another admissible state?

TRT's A marks the admissible/actual boundary but does not yet solve selection among admissible alternatives.

The Mind model locates a natural candidate locus for selection, but does not yet establish its nature.

## 11. New research bridge: S

The next target is therefore not generic action but **selection**.

Questions:

1. Does determinate physical actuality require selection among mutually incompatible admissible alternatives?
2. Can selection be impersonal without becoming another primitive?
3. Does rational selection require reasons/normativity?
4. Can reasons determine selection without modal collapse?
5. Does selection by Mind warrant the category of will?
6. Can physical indeterminacy coexist with ultimate rational selection?
7. Is S global (world-level) or local (event/outcome-level), or both?

## 12. Disposition

- Mental act != physical instantiation: HIGH.
- Multiple incompatible admissible contents require a discriminator if one physical actuality is determinate: MEDIUM-HIGH.
- S as provisional selection/designation relation: ADOPTED FOR ANALYSIS.
- S = will: NOT YET DERIVED.
- PIB temporal: NO requirement.
- Physical law alone as PIB: NOT ESTABLISHED and structurally insufficient if merely constraining.
- Necessary Mind -> necessary physical world: BLOCKED.
- Selection analysis: NEXT TARGET.
