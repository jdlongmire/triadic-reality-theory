# Metaphysical Admissibility Constraint Audit

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001
**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Status:** research formalization v0.1

## Question

Does logical coherence under L3 suffice for actualizability?

Coh_L3(x) ?=> Actualizable(x)

The answer cannot presently be affirmed without adding a premise. The task is to identify what an additional constraint M(x) could mean without defining it circularly as "metaphysically possible."

## 1. Baseline

Canonical TRT licenses:

X subset D subset R

and:

x in D => Coh_L3(x).

It does not license:

Coh_L3(x) => x in D.

Define provisionally:

x in D iff Rep(x) AND Coh_L3(x) AND M(x).

M is a placeholder for any non-logical condition required for actualizability.

## 2. No-free-lunch test for M

An acceptable M must satisfy:

1. Non-circularity: M cannot mean "possible," "can exist," or "actualizable."
2. Independence: its content must be stateable without presupposing D.
3. Necessity: every actualizable x must satisfy M.
4. Discrimination: M must exclude at least one L3-coherent representation.
5. Non-smuggling: M cannot silently import physical laws, divine decree, actuality, agency, or world-specific conditions into unrestricted metaphysical possibility.
6. Typing: intrinsic constraints on x must be separated from relational constraints indexed to Y.

## 3. Candidate classes

### Mathematical consistency

Mathematical constraints constitutive of x appear to refine coherence once included in the complete specification. A Euclidean square-circle is excluded by relations already encoded in its description.

**Status:** likely reducible to sufficiently rich logical/informational coherence.

### Essential or constitutive constraints

If x fails constitutive conditions of the kind it purports to instantiate, the complete specification is incoherent. Treating essence as an additional layer requires an independently defended theory of essence.

**Status:** candidate collapse into full coherence.

### Relational compossibility

Coh(x) AND Coh(y) can hold while NOT Coh(x AND y).

This establishes failure of joint actualizability, not intrinsic unactualizability of either term. Compossibility belongs to Compat(x,Y), not intrinsic M(x).

**Status:** genuine relational constraint.

### Physical-law compatibility

Compatibility with instantiated laws is relevant to physical realizability in a specified world. It does not establish unrestricted metaphysical impossibility unless those laws are independently necessary.

**Status:** domain-relative only.

### Causal production conditions

Requiring an available cause or production pathway risks importing PSR/CGP into possibility and confuses what a state is with how it becomes actual.

**Status:** rejected absent independent argument.

### Divine decree or will

G wills x cannot define actualizability because it collapses unactualized possibilities toward actuality.

**Status:** rejected as D criterion.

### Semantic conceivability

Conceivability adds nothing beyond representability and is epistemically limited.

**Status:** rejected.

## 4. Strongest current result

No independently established intrinsic M(x) survives the audit.

Candidates divide into:

1. constraints apparently reducible to sufficiently complete coherence;
2. relational/world-indexed compatibility constraints;
3. illicit additions importing causation, actuality, decree, or modal vocabulary.

This motivates:

D_intrinsic ?= {x in R | Coh*(x)}

where Coh*(x) means maximally specified coherence, including constitutive logical, mathematical, definitional, and relational constraints internal to the representation.

This remains a conjecture.

## 5. L3 versus Coh*

The prior question was underspecified. L3 is the invariant logical constraint. Coh* is the result of applying logical constraint to the complete informational specification.

Proposed structure:

Rep(x) -> Coh*(x) -> Compat*(x,Y) -> X(x).

Here:

- L3 supplies invariant logical constraints;
- I supplies the full informational specification;
- Coh* is the result of applying L3 to that specification;
- Compat* applies the same test to x together with Y;
- A distinguishes what actually obtains.

This avoids introducing an unexplained fourth primitive M.

## 6. Candidate refinement of D

Research hypothesis:

D(x) iff Rep(x) AND Coh*(x).

Define:

Coh*(x) := the complete informational specification of x contains no contradiction under L3.

For conditional actualizability:

Real(x | Y) iff Coh*(x AND Y),

provided Y is sufficiently specified for the domain under analysis.

These are hypotheses, not promoted theorems.

## 7. No modal collapse

The candidate does not assert D => X.

Nor does it assert that all members of D can jointly obtain.

D(x) AND D(y) may coexist with NOT Coh*(x AND y).

Action remains necessary:

D --A--> X.

## 8. Counterfactual consequence

The four-way distinction becomes:

IF != CAN != WOULD != IS

where:

IF := Rep(Y -> x)

CAN := Coh*(x AND Y)

WOULD := TrueCF(Y -> x)

IS := X(x).

The unresolved burden moves to CAN-to-WOULD. This is where Molinist middle knowledge introduces substantive counterfactual truth claims concerning libertarian agents. TRT/LRT need not do so.

## 9. Adversarial targets

Before promotion, test Coh* sufficiency against:

- impossible objects alleged to be logically consistent;
- essentialist modal objections;
- Kripkean necessary a posteriori cases;
- mathematical independence and undecidability;
- dispositional and causal modality;
- laws-of-nature accounts of possibility;
- Aristotelian potentiality;
- Thomistic act/potency distinctions;
- Molinist counterfactuals of creaturely freedom;
- Reformed accounts of divine decree and counterfactual knowledge;
- impossible-world semantics.

For each objection ask:

> Does this identify a genuine constraint beyond complete informational coherence, or reveal that the original representation was incomplete?

## 10. Disposition

**Result:** no justified independent M(x) has yet been found.

**Candidate simplification:**

D ?= Coh*(R).

**Interpretation:** actualizability may be exhausted by complete informational coherence under logical constraint, while conditional/world-relative realizability is complete joint coherence under the specified condition set.

**Confidence:** MEDIUM as a research hypothesis; LOW as a promoted metaphysical theorem.

**No-smuggling status:** PASS at this stage. No additional modal primitive has been inserted.

## 11. Next proof burden

Formalize Coh* without making "complete specification" omniscience-dependent by definition.

Immediate target:

Coh*(x) ?<=> D(x).

The decisive falsifier would be a well-specified x that is completely non-contradictory under all constitutive constraints yet demonstrably metaphysically unactualizable for a reason not reducible to its specification or to a condition set Y.
