# Functional Adequacy Argument (FAA)

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Track:** convergence / transcendental adequacy  
**Status:** canonical comparative argument v0.1

## Thesis

Any proposed ultimate ground of actualized reality must account non-circularly for the functions required by the canonical Representable -> Actualizable -> Actualized architecture, including discrimination among mutually exclusive actualizable alternatives and the unity of the whole order.

The argument is comparative. It does not assume that every rival explicitly presupposes Mind. It asks whether a rival can discharge the complete explanatory workload without leaving functions unexplained, distributing them across unrelated primitives, or reintroducing mind-like capacities under different names.

## 1. Canonical explanandum

Define:

G := <I_R, L_D, A_X, S, C>.

Where:

### I_R: Representability / differentiated content
The capacity/domain by which distinct structures, states, relations, alternatives, or contents are representable.

Question:
What can be represented?

### L_D: Logical constraint on actualizability
The constitutive logical constraints, at minimum DAT/L3, required for a representable structure to qualify for determinate actualizability.

Question:
What can be actualized?

### A_X: Act / actuality
The primitive role distinguishing what actually obtains from what is merely actualizable.

Question:
What is actualized?

### S: Selection / actuality discrimination
Where multiple mutually incompatible alternatives are actualizable, the discriminator by which one derivative actuality rather than incompatible alternatives is instantiated in the relevant domain.

Question:
Which actualizable content is instantiated?

### C: Co-unity
The relation/essence by which I_R, L_D, A_X, and S form one coherent reality-generating order rather than an accidental plurality of unrelated primitives.

Question:
Why do these functions operate together as one ontology?

Canonical schematic:

R --L_D--> D --S/A_X--> X.

## 2. Functional Adequacy Principle

**FAP:**

UltimateGround(g,chi)
->
GroundsAdequately(g,I_R,L_D,A_X,S,C).

A candidate that explains only a proper subset is a partial ground, not yet an adequate ultimate ground.

"Grounds adequately" requires:
- non-circularity;
- no appeal to downstream systems as upstream generators;
- no nominal packaging of independent primitives as one essence;
- no unexplained functional substitution hidden by vocabulary;
- explicit treatment of co-unity C.

## 3. No-free-lunch rule

No candidate receives a functional capacity for free merely by changing terminology.

Examples:

- "law" does not automatically supply act or selection;
- "structure" does not automatically supply actuality;
- "information" does not automatically supply semantics;
- "necessity" does not automatically supply unity;
- "chance" does not automatically supply sourcehood;
- "emergence" cannot generate conditions it presupposes;
- "brute" terminates explanation but does not reduce primitive count;
- "Mind" does not automatically receive infinity, necessity, goodness, or physical-instantiation power.

Every candidate pays the ontological cost of the functions it uses.

## 4. Dependency controls

FAA inherits:

### DSIR
Derivative-System Independence Rule:
No derivative system within chi may serve as an independent ground-level counterexample to the conditions required for chi.

### EPR
Emergence Presupposition Rule:
Any actual emergence relation presupposes differentiation, coherent relation, and actuality; emergence cannot non-circularly generate the co-primitive roles upstream.

### BBR
Bruteness Burden Rule:
"Brute" supplies no positive explanation and does not collapse multiple primitive commitments into one.

### NPR
Nominal Packaging Rule:
Naming a conjunction as one entity does not establish ontological simplicity.

### CPP
Chance Presupposition Principle:
Actual chance structures presuppose differentiated alternatives, coherent modal/probability relations, and actuality.

### DAT / LEE
Determinate Actuality Theorem and Logical Emergence Exclusion secure the L_D dependency against downstream physical generation.

## 5. Rival adequacy audit

### Material physicalism

Potential strengths:
- concrete physical processes;
- embodiment;
- causal/functional correlations;
- derivative state transitions.

Ground-level debts:
- derivative physical systems already presuppose I_R, L_D, and A_X;
- physical laws/processes therefore cannot generate those conditions upstream without dependency circularity;
- semantic content and reasons-as-reasons require separate account;
- selection of an initial/domain actuality remains a separate problem if laws govern only already-actual states.

FAA disposition:
material physicalism may explain derivative organization within X but is not yet an adequate upstream ground of G.

### Abstracta / Platonism

Potential strengths:
- necessary structure;
- mathematical/logical relations;
- possible account of L_D and representable formal content.

Ground-level debts:
- standard abstracta are commonly characterized as causally/efficaciously inert;
- inert structure does not by itself supply A_X;
- selection S is not supplied merely by necessary abstract relations;
- relation between abstract structure and concrete actuality requires an additional bridge.

FAA disposition:
strong partial candidate for formal structure; incomplete on act/selection unless supplemented.

### Primitive/brute necessity

Potential strengths:
- can terminate regress;
- can stipulate necessary order.

Ground-level debts:
- "necessary" does not explain why the necessary terminus has I_R/L_D/A_X/S/C;
- "brute" supplies no unification;
- multiple primitives remain multiple unless simplicity is independently shown.

FAA disposition:
admissible stopping claim, not explanatory unification by itself.

### Impersonal laws

Potential strengths:
- constraint;
- regularity;
- transition rules.

Ground-level debts:
- a proposition describing a law is inert;
- a governing law endowed with efficacy must account for that efficacy;
- laws over states presuppose differentiated states and logical form;
- general constraint does not automatically explain selection of one concrete actuality.

FAA disposition:
partial unless law ontology independently derives act, selection, and unity.

### Structural realism / primitive structure

Potential strengths:
- relations;
- patterns;
- formal organization;
- candidate unified L/I structure.

Ground-level debts:
- structure simpliciter does not entail obtaining actuality;
- adding actuality/efficacy creates additional burden unless derived from one essence;
- selection remains separate where alternatives are mutually incompatible;
- co-unity C must be demonstrated rather than nominally packaged.

FAA disposition:
live H_S rival; adequacy depends on a genuine single structural essence deriving all functions.

### Information-as-physics

Potential strengths:
- correlation;
- encoded states;
- physical information measures;
- derivative information processing.

Ground-level debts:
- Shannon/physical information is downstream of actualized state structure;
- correlation does not automatically supply intrinsic semantics;
- information alone is inert with respect to actuality under IP;
- selection and co-unity remain.

FAA disposition:
useful derivative account; not yet an ultimate ground.

### Nonmental necessary ground

Potential strengths:
- can posit necessary order;
- can avoid derivative physical circularity.

Ground-level debts:
- must independently derive rational/logical constraint;
- differentiated/intelligible content;
- genuine act;
- actuality discrimination/selection;
- co-unity.

If the candidate adds primitive intentionality, reasons, sourcehood, or selection-like powers, FAA asks whether these are genuinely nonmental capacities or functional equivalents of the Mind model under alternate labels.

FAA disposition:
live in principle; must pass full functional audit.

## 6. Infinite Transcendent Mind candidate

Define provisionally:

H_M_Tinf:
one non-derivative transcendent Mind whose intrinsic nature is rational, informational/contentful, active, and unified.

Candidate correspondence:

I_R <-> content/representation.

L_D <-> intrinsic rational/logical form.

A_X <-> genuine act.

S <-> intentional selection among apprehended permissible alternatives.

C <-> unity of one subject.

This is a model-fit map, not yet a completed derivation.

The Mind candidate receives no free credit for:
- necessity;
- infinity/unlimitedness;
- uniqueness;
- omniscience;
- omnipotence;
- goodness;
- physical instantiation;
- Christian identification.

Those remain independent proof obligations.

## 7. Comparative thesis

The decisive comparative claim is:

> Rival grounds tend either to be inadequate to one or more required functions, split the workload across additional primitives, or introduce capacities functionally resembling rationality, content, act, selection, and unity. Infinite Transcendent Mind is the current candidate that most directly presents those functions as intrinsic aspects of one subject, but that explanatory unification must be earned rather than stipulated.

This is not yet:

forall g [AdequateGround(g,G) -> Mind(g)].

That universal premise remains to be defended.

Current warranted form:

For each serious rival g:
Audit(g,G).

If no nonmental rival can satisfy FAP without independent added primitives or mind-like functional equivalents, the comparative case for H_M_Tinf strengthens.

## 9. "Mind-like" definition control

MindLike(g) must not be defined as "whatever satisfies G," or the theorem becomes trivial.

Candidate independent markers include:
- intrinsic content/intentionality;
- rational apprehension;
- genuine act;
- source-attributable selection;
- unified subject-locus.

These markers require independent analysis.

## 10. Explanatory economy

Primitive accounting is functional rather than nominal.

H_S cannot claim one primitive merely by naming <I,L,A,S,C> "Structure."

H_M_Tinf cannot claim one primitive merely by naming the same package "Mind."

A unification advantage exists only if one independently intelligible essence entails/explains the functions without separate additions.

## 11. Current strongest conclusion

> Any proposed ultimate ground must account non-circularly for representability, logical actualizability, genuine act, determinate selection, and their unity. Derivative physical systems cannot ground these conditions because their existence already presupposes them. Emergence cannot generate them upstream because emergence itself presupposes differentiation, logical relation, and actuality. Bruteness does not reduce their explanatory cost. An impersonal ground remains viable only if it can derive these functions from a genuinely unified nonmental essence. Infinite Transcendent Mind is presently the candidate whose intrinsic character most directly corresponds to the complete functional requirement, but its necessity, infinity, uniqueness, and ultimate identification remain independent proof obligations.
