# Ontological-Transcendental Synthesis Architecture

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001
**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Status:** canonical architecture candidate v0.1
**Controls:** TASR, ADF, ESR, no-smuggling

## 1. Purpose

Define the architectural relationship between the transcendental and ontological stages of TRT/LRT without allowing claims from one stage to be smuggled into the other.

The programme is an ontological-transcendental synthesis:

> Transcendental analysis identifies the irreducible conditions presupposed by determinate actuality. Ontological analysis then asks what kind of ultimate ground can intrinsically satisfy and unify those conditions without circularity, borrowed actuality, or unexplained external coordination.

## 2. Stage T: Transcendental derivation

Start with determinate actuality:

X.

Ask what cannot be removed while retaining determinate actuality as determinate actuality.

Current role-level result:

X -> (L AND I AND A).

Where:

- L = logical determinacy/admissibility role;
- I = differentiated representability/informational role;
- A = act/actuality role.

Canonical theorem form:

Box[X -> (L AND I AND A)].

This stage establishes necessary roles relative to X.

It does not establish:
- a substance;
- a bearer;
- a mind;
- a person;
- God;
- one numerically identical entity across worlds;
- necessity of a bearer;
- agency.

## 3. Role/Ontology Firewall

Adopt:

NecessaryRole(r,X) != SelfSubsistingOntology(r).

Therefore:

X -> (L AND I AND A)

does not license:

Exists(T=<L,I,A>) as an independently self-subsisting impersonal entity.

Tuple notation is representational bookkeeping, not ontology.

The same firewall prohibits inserting Mind merely because Mind could bear the roles.

## 4. Stage O: Ontological grounding

Given the transcendental role result, ask:

What ontology can adequately ground the roles and their unity?

Let:

G_req := <L,I,A,C,S_cond>,

where:
- C = co-unity/coordination burden;
- S_cond = discrimination/selection only where mutually incompatible actualizable alternatives obtain for one actuality domain.

Functional Adequacy requires:

UltimateGround(g,X) -> AdequatelyGrounds(g,G_req).

The ontological stage therefore begins only after Stage T has identified the workload.

## 5. Source-bearer constraint

The ontological ground cannot merely redescribe the roles.

Candidate result:

AdequateUltimateGround(g,G_req)
->
UnifiedIntrinsicSourceOf(L,I,A)(g).

Expand:

SRC(g): non-derivative source of act.

CON(g): L and I are intrinsic/non-external to the grounding adequacy of g.

EXP(g): g is capable of content-determinate actualization under L through A.

UNI(g): L/I/A are intrinsically unified rather than externally coordinated by a more ultimate unexplained relation.

Thus:

AdequateUltimateGround(g,G_req)
->
SRC(g) AND CON(g) AND EXP(g) AND UNI(g).

This bridge remains a candidate until the Source Principle is tested.

## 6. Stage A_min: Minimal agency target

Define minimally:

Agent_min(g) :=
g is a unified intrinsic source to which a determinate content-expressive act is attributable.

Target:

SRC(g) AND CON(g) AND EXP(g) AND UNI(g)
?=> Agent_min(g).

Agent_min does not entail:
- consciousness;
- phenomenology;
- libertarian freedom;
- deliberation;
- moral responsibility;
- personhood;
- human cognition.

## 7. Stage M_min: Minimal Mind target

Only after Agent_min is established may the programme ask whether the source profile warrants MindLike_min.

Candidate:

Agent_min(g)
AND IntrinsicLogicalOrder(g)
AND IntrinsicInformationalContent(g)
AND UNI(g)
?=> MindLike_min(g).

MindLike_min must add real ontological content. If it merely renames the source profile, the bridge fails.

## 9. ADF control

Derivative physical actualities may illustrate a relation but may not ground Stage T or Stage O.

Permitted:

first-principles result -> downstream analogy.

Prohibited:

downstream physical IS -> first-principles ontology.

This applies to:
- human minds;
- brains;
- GenAI;
- computers;
- organisms;
- language;
- physical laws as instantiated;
- physical causation;
- biological information.

## 10. ESR control

Representational infinity is not an invitation to enumerate cases.

New cases are admitted only if they:
1. threaten a load-bearing arrow;
2. identify a genuinely new primitive;
3. falsify an adopted distinction;
4. supply a counterexample outside an already tested class.

## 11. No-smuggling controls

Prohibited substitutions:

- role = ontology;
- information = knowledge;
- logic = intellect;
- action = agency;
- unity = subject;
- expression = intentionality;
- source = person;
- actualizable = actual;
- IF = CAN;
- CAN = WOULD;
- WOULD = IS;
- ultimate = necessary;
- non-derivative = divine.

## 12. Programme boundary

Necessity, uniqueness, unlimitedness/infinity, omniscience, omnipotence, goodness, perfection, personhood, and Christian identification remain downstream proof obligations.

They may not be used to establish Stage O, A_min, or M_min.

## 13. Immediate proof target

The next bounded theorem target is the Source Principle:

SP-A:
Genuine non-derivative act requires a non-derivative source of act.

Formally:

NonDerivativeAct(A) ?=> exists g Source(g,A).

The audit must compare ontology classes rather than examples:
- event ontology;
- process ontology;
- structural ontology;
- substance/source ontology.

The question is whether "bearerless act" is coherent and explanatorily sufficient, or whether act without source merely restates that actuality obtains.

## 14. Success criterion

The synthesis succeeds methodologically even if the Mind bridge fails, provided it identifies exactly where the derivation stops.

A successful Mind result requires every bridge to survive independently:

X -> L/I/A -> unified intrinsic source -> Agent_min -> MindLike_min.

No later theological conclusion may repair a failed earlier bridge.
