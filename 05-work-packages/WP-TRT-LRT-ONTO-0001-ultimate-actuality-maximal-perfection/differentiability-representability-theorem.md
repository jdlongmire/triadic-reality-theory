# Differentiability and Representability Theorem (DRT)

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001
**Date:** 2026-09-19
**Status:** theorem candidate v0.1
**Role:** I_R leg for the Co-Primitive Ground Theorem

## Thesis

Any determinate actualized reality must be differentiable enough to be representable as the actuality it is. An actuality with no identity-condition, distinction, relation, boundary, state, or difference from non-obtaining would fail to be a determinate actuality.

This theorem concerns ontological differentiation/representability, not Shannon information and not intrinsic semantics.

## 1. Definitions

Let:
- Actualized(x): x obtains.
- Determinate(x): x is actualized as something definite.
- Diff(x): x participates in at least the distinctions required for determinate identity/predication.
- Representable(x): x can be picked out under a determinate informational distinction.
- I_R: the role/domain of differentiated representability.

## 2. Minimal differentiation

DAT gives:

Actualized(x) -> Determinate(x).

Determinate x requires at minimum:
- x rather than not-x;
- identity of x across the predication being made;
- distinguishability of properties/relations attributed to x;
- distinction between obtaining and non-obtaining for exact applicable conditions.

Therefore:

Determinate(x) -> Diff(x).

Hence:

Actualized(x) -> Diff(x).

## 3. Representability

"Representable" does not mean "actually represented by a finite observer."

It means that x is sufficiently determinate/differentiated to be a possible object of exact reference, predication, relation, or informational distinction.

Thus:

Diff(x) -> Representable(x)

under the minimal ontological sense used by TRT.

Therefore:

Actualized(x) -> Representable(x).

## 4. Featureless-simple objection

A critic may propose a perfectly simple actuality with no internal parts or differentiated properties.

DRT does not require internal complexity.

Even a simple actuality must be:
- itself rather than not itself;
- actual rather than non-actual;
- sufficiently determinate to be the actuality being posited.

Therefore I_R means differentiability/determinate distinguishability, not compositional plurality.

## 5. Observer-independence

DRT does not infer:

no finite observer -> no differentiation.

Finite cognition may apprehend distinctions derivatively. The theorem concerns the ontological determinacy required for actuality, whether or not a finite observer exists.

## 6. Shannon firewall

Do not infer:

Diff(x) -> ShannonInformation(x).

Shannon information requires a probability-bearing state structure and quantitative measure.

DRT establishes only I_R, the representability/differentiability role upstream of Shannon information.

## 7. Semantic firewall

Do not infer:

Representable(x) -> IntrinsicSemanticContent(x).

Aboutness, reference in the intentional sense, truth conditions, and meaning remain separate thickening questions.

## 8. Emergence control

Any claim that differentiation itself emerges from a prior X must distinguish:
- X from what emerges;
- before/after or ground/result;
- relation E from not-E.

Thus the proposed emergence already uses differentiation.

**Differentiation Emergence Exclusion (DEE):**

No emergence process can be the ontologically prior generator of the minimal differentiation required to identify the source, result, and emergence relation.

Derivative differentiated structures can emerge within an already differentiated ontology.

## 9. Formal schema

For all x:

Actualized(x) -> Determinate(x).

Determinate(x) -> Diff(x).

Diff(x) -> Representable(x).

Therefore:

Actualized(x) -> Representable(x).

Modal form:

**DRT:**

Box[ActualizedReality -> I_R].

## 10. Category-backbone interface

DRT secures the R dependency:

X -> R.

The canonical backbone remains:

R ⊇ D ⊇ X.

DRT does not claim every representable structure is actualizable.

## 11. Epistemic status

- Actualized -> determinate: inherited from DAT.
- Determinate -> minimal differentiation: HIGH.
- Minimal differentiation -> ontological representability: MEDIUM-HIGH, definition-sensitive.
- DRT Box[ActualizedReality -> I_R]: MEDIUM-HIGH.
- DRT -> semantics: NOT CLAIMED.
- DRT -> Mind: NOT CLAIMED.
