# Semantic Bridge: From Differentiability to Intrinsic Information

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Track:** A-adjacent evidential sub-track  
**Target:** I-thin -> I-thick -> Mind?  
**Status:** first-pass analysis complete

## Question

Does TRT's primitive I, presently defined minimally as differentiability / representable informational potential, entail intrinsic semantic content at the ultimate level?

If not, can semantic information enter the argument by an independently warranted bridge?

## 1. Definitions

### I-thin
Differentiability sufficient for representable alternatives, identity conditions, relations, configurations, and state distinctions.

I-thin does not include:
- aboutness;
- reference;
- truth conditions;
- interpretation;
- intentionality;
- knowledge.

### I-syntactic
Structured symbol/state relations governed by transformation rules.

This adds formal organization but still need not include intrinsic meaning.

### I-semantic / I-thick
Information that is about something, can refer, misrepresent, or bear truth conditions in a non-merely-ascribed sense.

### I-pragmatic
Information situated in use, goal, function, action, or interpretation.

These categories must not be collapsed.

## 2. Direct entailment test

Question:

I-thin -> I-semantic?

Countermodel:

A cellular automaton or abstract state graph contains distinguishable states and lawful transitions. An external interpreter may assign meanings to configurations, but nothing in the bare graph entails that one state intrinsically means "predator," "true," "electron," or any other content.

Therefore:

I-thin does not entail I-semantic.

**Status:** FAILED / HIGH confidence.

The symbol I_infinity must not carry semantic content merely because it is called "information."

## 3. Syntactic escalation

Could sufficiently rich formal structure generate semantics?

I-syntactic supplies:
- tokens/states;
- relations;
- rules;
- transformations.

But a permutation or reinterpretation of token assignments can preserve syntax while changing assigned meaning. Formal role alone underdetermines reference.

This is a version of the familiar syntax/semantics gap. It does not prove that naturalized semantics is impossible, but it blocks a definitional inference from formal structure to intrinsic aboutness.

**Result:** I-syntactic does not by itself entail I-semantic.

## 4. Functional/causal semantics

A stronger naturalistic proposal grounds content in causal covariance, biological function, teleosemantics, inferential role, predictive use, or system-environment relations.

These approaches may supply non-arbitrary content without a conscious interpreter.

Therefore the claim:

"semantic information exists only in mind"

cannot be treated as established.

The relevant question is narrower:

> Can intrinsic truth-conditional/reference-bearing content be fully grounded in non-mental causal/functional structure without presupposing normativity, representation, or goal-directedness?

This remains an open philosophical dispute and requires primary-source research before O2 can be promoted.

## 5. Error/misrepresentation test

Semantic content becomes more demanding when a state can be **wrong about** its object rather than merely fail to correlate.

Pure covariance handles:
- state s covaries with condition c.

Semantics requires at least the possibility:
- s represents c;
- c is absent;
- s is therefore mistaken/misrepresenting.

The distinction between malfunction and misrepresentation introduces normative conditions: what the state is supposed to indicate, track, or mean.

This creates a possible bridge:

intrinsic semantics
  -> correctness conditions
  -> normativity
  -> ?

But normativity does not yet entail mind. Biological-function theories explicitly attempt to naturalize such norms.

## 6. Truth-bearing content

If a content is propositionally truth-apt, it has conditions under which it is true or false.

Candidate chain:

propositional semantic content
  -> truth conditions
  -> logical relations among contents
  -> normative standards of inference.

This produces an important interaction between I-thick and L-thick.

The abductive case for Mind may therefore depend less on I alone than on the **joint irreducibility** of:

I_semantic + L_normative + A_goal/reason-responsive.

That is stronger than any single-feature argument.

## 7. Ultimate-level question

Even if derivative biological or computational systems can instantiate naturalized semantics, the ultimate-ground question remains:

Can an ontologically primitive T = <L,I,A> possess intrinsic semantic content without a subject for whom or in whom content is meaningful?

There are at least three live answers:

### S1. Structural semantics
Semantic relations are primitive objective relations. No subject is required.

### S2. Functional semantics
Content emerges from causal/functional organization once sufficiently structured actual systems exist.

### M1. Mental semantics
Intrinsic content is fundamentally intentional and ultimately grounded in mind.

TRT presently does not choose among these.

## 8. Observation from derivative systems

Computers/AI:
- clearly instantiate syntax, encoded information, and functional outputs;
- semantic status is contested and often depends on human-defined tasks, corpora, labels, interfaces, or use;
- no conclusion about intrinsic semantics is authorized without further argument.

Biology:
- genetic and signaling systems exhibit strong functional/informational organization;
- whether this is genuinely semantic or metaphorical/teleonomic is contested;
- biology is therefore a serious counterexample candidate, not something to dismiss.

Human mind:
- provides the clearest ordinary case of intentional aboutness, truth-apt thought, reasons, and purposive action;
- whether mental content is itself reducible to physical/functional structure remains contested.

## 9. Result for H_M∞

The direct route fails:

I-thin -> Mind.

A stronger abductive route remains live:

(I-semantic + L-normative + A-reason-responsive)
  -> Mind-like ground?

This is not yet established, but it is a much better discriminator between H_S and H_M∞.

H_S can easily absorb thin I/L/A. Its burden rises only if thick features are independently established as fundamental rather than derivative.

## 10. Revised research strategy

Do not attempt to derive Mind from I alone.

Instead test a **Triadic Thickening Hypothesis (TTH):**

The ultimate co-primitives, when examined jointly, may entail or best explain:
- intrinsic semantic content;
- objective logical normativity;
- reason-responsive/purposive action.

If all three thicken independently and remain irreducible, mind becomes a substantially stronger unification hypothesis.

If they remain thin or naturalize without remainder, H_S remains viable and may be preferable.

## 11. Dependency firewall

Prohibited:
- differentiability -> meaning;
- syntax -> semantics;
- correlation -> representation;
- function -> purpose;
- error -> conscious judgment;
- normativity -> mind;
- semantics -> consciousness;
- mind -> infinite/perfect mind.

Each requires separate warrant.

## 12. Disposition

- I-thin -> I-semantic: **FAILED / HIGH**.
- Syntax alone -> intrinsic semantics: **NOT ESTABLISHED / HIGH confidence in underdetermination**.
- Naturalized semantics impossible: **NOT ESTABLISHED**.
- Human mind as clear paradigm of intrinsic intentional content: **MEDIUM-HIGH descriptive confidence**, reduction question open.
- I alone supports H_M∞: **WEAK**.
- Joint thick L/I/A as abductive discriminator: **PROMISING / MEDIUM**.
- TTH: **OPEN / UNCERTAIN**.

## 13. Next target

Work L-thick next:

> Is logical consequence merely structural/descriptive, or is there irreducible normativity in the distinction between valid and invalid inference?

Then work A-thick:

> Is actuality-conferring action merely transition, or does the full TRT programme require selection responsive to reasons/goals?

Only after all three should the H_S vs H_M∞ comparison be scored.


## 14. Correction: derivative-system counterexamples and ontological level discipline

The first pass used cellular automata and related formal/physical systems too aggressively as countermodels to the ultimate semantic/mind hypothesis.

That move is corrected.

A cellular automaton already presupposes:
- distinguishable states: I-role;
- identity, consistency, and transition constraints: L-role;
- if physically instantiated/executed, actualization within chi: A-role.

Likewise computers, AI systems, organisms, and physical dynamical systems are derivative structures within actualized reality:

T = <L,I,A> -> chi -> derivative systems.

They therefore cannot serve as **independent ontological counterexamples** to a hypothesis concerning the ground of T itself. Both H_S and H_M∞ predict that derivative L/I/A-bearing structures may occur within chi.

The earlier cellular-automaton example remains valid only for the narrower conceptual point:

I-thin does not contain I-semantic by definition.

It does **not** establish:

Diamond(I-thin AND not-Mind)

at the ultimate ontological level.

### New level-discipline rule

**Derivative-System Independence Rule (DSIR):**

No system whose existence already depends on the co-primitives under investigation may be treated as independent evidence that those co-primitives are ontologically self-sufficient or mind-independent.

Derivative systems may:
- illuminate how L/I/A manifest;
- test whether thick semantic/normative/agentive features appear downstream;
- supply analogies and local reduction models.

They may not:
- establish that T is self-grounding;
- establish that ultimate I is non-semantic;
- establish that ultimate L is non-rational;
- establish that ultimate A is non-agentive;
- defeat H_M∞ merely by instantiating thin derivative analogues.

### Correct hypothesis comparison

The relevant same-level comparison is:

H_S: T = <L,I,A> is primitively self-sufficient impersonal structure.

H_M∞: L/I/A are intrinsic unified aspects of ultimate mind M∞.

Evidence from within chi must be interpreted as downstream evidence under both hypotheses unless it discriminates between them.

### Revised status

- I-thin -> I-semantic: still NOT deductively established.
- Cellular automata/computers as ultimate counterexamples: WITHDRAWN.
- Derivative naturalized-semantics models as proofs of ultimate mind-independence: NOT LICENSED.
- Such models as local demonstrations that semantic-like function may be implemented downstream: RETAINED.
- Ultimate H_S vs H_M∞ comparison must be conducted at the ontological-ground level.

This correction increases the importance of identifying evidence that discriminates between the two ultimate hypotheses rather than pointing to derivative implementations both can accommodate.
