# Positive Sourcehood Model: Agent-Originating Determination

**WP:** WP-TRT-LRT-ONTO-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Track:** convergence / sourcehood  
**Target:** positive explanatory content of AOD  
**Status:** first-pass formalization complete

## Question

Can agent-originating determination (AOD) add genuine explanatory content beyond the statement "selection S occurred"?

The burden is to specify what changes when a unified rational subject is the source of an act, without using "agent" as a gap-accounting label.

## 1. Event-only model

Let:

E0 := S(p) occurs.

This gives:
- an event/outcome identity;
- actuality of S;
- selected content p.

It does not by itself specify:
- who/what apprehends p;
- whether alternatives were represented;
- whether reasons bear on p;
- whether S is intentional;
- whether S is attributable to a subject;
- whether S expresses a stable power/capacity.

E0 is therefore explanatorily sparse.

## 2. AOD model

Let M be a unified rational informational active subject.

AOD(M,p | D,R) includes:

1. **Alternative apprehension:** M actively represents a permissible set D containing p and alternatives.
2. **Reason apprehension:** M apprehends relevant reasons/ends R.
3. **Rational constraint:** R constrains D to a rationally permissible subset D_R.
4. **Intentional directedness:** p is selected as an intended content/end, not merely correlated with S.
5. **Source integration:** the selecting act is an act of the same M that apprehends D and R.
6. **Non-external origination:** no ontologically prior external selector is sufficient independently of M's act.
7. **Determination:** M's act resolves the permissible plurality to p for the relevant domain.
8. **Counterfactual ownership:** variations in M's reasons/ends/intentional act would be relevant to selection in a way that mere event occurrence does not encode.

This is positive relational structure, not merely the label "agent."

## 3. Formal sketch

Let:

App_M(D): M apprehends alternatives D.
Reason_M(R): M apprehends reasons R.
Perm(R,D) = D_R: rational constraint yields permissible subset.
Intend_M(p): p is intentional object/end.
Act_M(S_p): M performs selecting act S_p.
Source(M,S_p): S_p is attributable to M as source.

Then:

AOD(M,p) :=
App_M(D)
AND Reason_M(R)
AND p in Perm(R,D)
AND Intend_M(p)
AND Act_M(S_p)
AND Source(M,S_p)
AND not exists E [PriorExternal(E,M) AND Sufficient(E,S_p)].

The definition does not include "uncaused." It excludes only an ontologically prior external sufficient selector that would displace M's source role.

## 4. What sourcehood explains

AOD supplies answers unavailable in E0:

### Why is S intelligible as this kind of act?
Because it is directed toward apprehended p under reasons R.

### Why is p selected from D?
Not necessarily because R uniquely necessitates p, but because p is within the reason-constrained permissible set and M determines p through intentional act.

### Why attribute S to M?
Because apprehension, reasons, intention, and selection are integrated in one subject and the selection is not independently sourced in an external sufficient selector.

### Why is S not merely stochastic description?
Because the explanatory structure includes intentional content, reasons, and subject-source integration, whether or not a probabilistic model also describes outcomes.

Thus AOD adds explanatory structure even when it does not provide a uniquely differentiating reason for p over every q.

## 5. Does "M determines p" merely restate S?

It would if Source(M,S) had no independent criteria.

The independent criteria are:
- same-subject integration;
- intentional directedness;
- reason responsiveness;
- absence of displaced sourcehood by prior external sufficient determination;
- stable capacity/power for such acts;
- counterfactual sensitivity to the subject's reasons/ends.

Therefore Source is not merely a grammatical rewrite if those relations can be independently identified.

## 6. Stable power/capacity

One isolated source-attributed act may be accidental description.

Will_min requires a stable capacity:

Power_S(M): M is capable, as the same subject, of intentional reason-responsive selection across relevant admissible choice situations.

Then:

AOD-pattern + Power_S(M) -> Will_min(M).

This adds dispositional structure without requiring temporal repetition in an ultimate timeless subject. "Stable" means grounded in nature/capacity, not temporally persistent behavior.

## 7. Counterfactual structure

AOD should support counterfactuals such as:

If M had different sufficient reasons/ends while the alternatives remained available, selection could appropriately differ.

If M intentionally selected q instead of p within the permissible set, q rather than p would be designated for the relevant instantiation bridge.

These counterfactuals distinguish sourcehood from an event whose outcome is invariant under every fact about the alleged agent.

For an ultimate necessary Mind, counterfactuals involving a different nature may be impossible; the relevant variation concerns permissible intentional act, not defective rational nature.

## 8. AOD and explanation termination

If M is ultimate, asking for an ontologically prior source of M's sourcehood would contradict the hypothesis of ultimacy.

But this cannot be used to declare sourcehood brute.

The positive explanatory content is the integrated subject structure above. The termination claim, if eventually warranted, would be:

selection is grounded in the act of the non-derivative subject,

not:

selection has no explanation.

Whether a non-derivative subject is independently established remains a separate convergence question.

## 9. AOD and co-primitives

AOD itself presupposes/instantiates:
- I: alternatives, reasons, intentional content;
- L: rational constraints and coherent relations;
- A: actual apprehension and selection;
- C: unity in one subject.

This is expected under H_M∞. AOD is not offered as an upstream explanation of the co-primitives. It is a candidate expression of their unified operation in Mind.

This avoids the error made with derivative physical systems: AOD is evaluated as a same-level aspect of the Mind hypothesis, not as an independent generator of L/I/A.

## 10. H_S comparison

An impersonal structural account can mimic parts:
- D: alternatives;
- R: rule/constraint;
- transition to p.

But to match AOD it must additionally model:
- intrinsic intentional directedness;
- reasons as reasons rather than merely state variables;
- source integration;
- act attribution.

If those thick features reduce without remainder to structure, H_S can absorb AOD and H_M∞ loses explanatory advantage.

If they are irreducibly subject-involving, H_M∞ gains.

Thus the discriminator is no longer "does selection happen?" but whether intentional/reason-responsive sourcehood is reducible to impersonal structure.

## 11. Non-circularity check

AOD does not prove Mind exists. It specifies what Mind-sourcehood would amount to if instantiated.

The argument must not become:

selection requires AOD;
AOD contains Mind;
therefore Mind.

Instead:
- selection requires some discriminator S;
- candidate S models are compared;
- AOD is one model with positive explanatory structure;
- independent evidence for intentionality/reasons/sourcehood is required to prefer it.

## 12. Disposition

- AOD adds structure beyond event occurrence: **YES / MEDIUM-HIGH**.
- Sourcehood reducible to grammar alone: **NO if independent criteria hold**.
- Stable selection capacity supports Will_min: **YES as category analysis**.
- AOD explains co-primitives upstream: **NO; not claimed**.
- AOD establishes M∞: **NO**.
- AOD distinct from chance by positive structure, not elimination: **YES**.
- Reducibility of intentional/reason-responsive sourcehood to H_S: **OPEN**.
- AOD as coherent model of non-derivative rational sourcehood: **MEDIUM, pending deeper agency literature/formalization**.

## 13. Next target

The next same-level discriminator is **intentionality**:

> Can "aboutness" and reasons-as-reasons be fully represented as impersonal structural relations, or do they require a subject?

This reconnects the sourcehood work to the semantic bridge without using derivative systems as independent ground-level counterexamples.
