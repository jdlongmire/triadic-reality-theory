## Dependency rule

No active canonical artifact may infer:

1. representable ⇒ coherent;
2. coherent/L₃-admissible ⇒ actualizable;
3. actualizable ⇒ actual;
4. A ⇒ physical time;
5. L₃ alone exhausts the conditions of D.

Historical reviews and completed work packages are evidence of programme development and are not silently rewritten. Where their terminology conflicts with the canonical vocabulary, this matrix governs interpretation.
