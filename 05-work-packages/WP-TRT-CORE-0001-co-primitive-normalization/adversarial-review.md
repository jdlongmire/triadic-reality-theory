# Adversarial Review: Irreducible L-I-A Normalization

**WP:** WP-TRT-CORE-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-29  
**Disposition:** PASS WITH OPEN OBLIGATIONS

## Circularity: A

**Attack:** If A is defined as “that which actualizes,” the claim that actuality requires A risks becoming analytic.

**Disposition:** Accepted. Canonical A is fundamental state transition/change. Actualization is a role/consequence associated with A and X, not the definiens of A.

**Residual burden:** Show how primitive transition connects D to obtaining X without reintroducing actuality by definition.

## Modal smuggling: I∞ and D

**Attack:** “Possible distinctions” and D = I∞ filtered only by L₃ can import metaphysical possibility into representability and make logical coherence sufficient for realization without argument.

**Disposition:** Accepted. I∞ is unbounded representability/informational differentiation. L₃-admissibility is necessary for D. Sufficiency remains unproved.

**Residual burden:** Identify whether conditions beyond L₃ are required for actualizability and state them without circular modal vocabulary.

## Temporal smuggling: A

**Attack:** Defining A as temporal change would presuppose time while TRT elsewhere seeks to derive temporal ordering from transitions.

**Disposition:** Accepted. Primitive A has no temporal parameter. Ordered A-relations may ground temporal succession downstream.

**Residual burden:** Formalize ordering without assuming the target temporal structure.

## Reducibility: L₃

**Attack:** Logical constraint might be redescribed as regularity in information or transition.

**Disposition:** Rejected provisionally. Regularity presupposes stable identity and non-contradictory predication. A reduction must recover normative admissibility rather than merely observed pattern.

**Residual burden:** Continue the LRT proof programme and distinguish transcendental warrant from empirical FLL exposure.

## Reducibility: I∞

**Attack:** Information may be reducible to logical structure.

**Disposition:** Rejected provisionally. Constraint does not supply differentiated relata. A purely logical vocabulary requires something over which predicates range.

**Residual burden:** Specify the weakest non-semantic notion of informational differentiation needed by TRT.

## Reducibility: A

**Attack:** Given a complete state-space and laws, transition may be derivable and need no primitive A.

**Disposition:** Open challenge. A mathematical transition rule represents a relation; TRT claims representation and constraint do not themselves entail that a transition obtains.

**Residual burden:** Make the representation/efficacy distinction rigorous and test whether A can be eliminated without explanatory loss.

## Accidental strengthening

**Attack:** The normalization could turn a research programme into a claimed theorem of metaphysical necessity.

**Disposition:** Controlled. The hard core states the necessity argument and its assumptions; formal artifacts mark axioms and open directions. Physics-facing claims retain separate status.

## Overall disposition

The normalization removes two substantive defects: circular definition of A and unproved identification of L₃-coherence with actualizability. It also makes irreducibility explicit. The programme remains exposed at the representation-to-efficacy bridge, the sufficiency conditions for D, and the formal grounding of non-temporal transition order. These are open obligations, not hidden auxiliaries.
