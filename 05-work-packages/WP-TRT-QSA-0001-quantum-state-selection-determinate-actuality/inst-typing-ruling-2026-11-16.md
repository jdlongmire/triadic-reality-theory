# Inst typing ruling (2026-11-16)

**Authority:** JD Longmire, programme lead, ruling 9 on the 2026-11 reconciliation map (`0-program-methods/sessions/2026-11-16-reconciliation-rulings.md`).

**Ruling.** `Inst(sigma)`, the status of a physically instantiated quantum state, is a status inside D.

**Reason.** The charter places the quantum state in D (structure of the admissible). If instantiation were a status outside D, this package and the charter would disagree about where the state sits, and the programme would carry a second locus of actuality alongside A-events. Under this ruling, instantiation language in this package denotes a distinction within the actualizable domain; anything in this package that speaks of a state being actual is to be read as, and where necessary rewritten to route through, A-events in the sense of the accepted typing (reconciliation map section 4, ruling 4) and the occurrent reading of A (ruling 11).

**Scope.** This ruling settles the typing question the map flagged. It does not change this package's bounded claims, its chi-type analysis, or its operational criteria, except in how `Inst` is to be read.
