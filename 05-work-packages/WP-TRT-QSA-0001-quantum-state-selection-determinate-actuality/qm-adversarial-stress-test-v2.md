# Quantum Adversarial Stress Test v2

**WP:** WP-TRT-QSA-0001  
**Date:** 2026-09-30  
**Status:** adversarial pass v2  
**Revision:** specification-relative determinacy

## Core test

For a physically instantiated quantum state ψ define:

$$q_\psi := \text{“the system is in state }\psi\text{.”}$$

Then QSA permits:

$$\operatorname{Inst}(\psi)\Rightarrow\chi_{q_\psi}.$$

For a distinct observable/value claim q, the same state may satisfy:

$$U_{\mathrm{inst}}(\psi,q).$$

Thus ψ can be determinate actuality as state while under-determinate relative to q.

The test asks whether this indexed distinction survives major interpretation families.

## 1. Collapse family

Before outcome settlement, ψ may be determinate under q_ψ while an observable q remains unsettled.

A measurement/collapse model may then warrant:

$$\operatorname{Settled}(\alpha,q,\chi_q).$$

Nothing requires ψ itself to have been unreal or logically indeterminate.

**PASS.** Collapse becomes one candidate physical route from q-relative under-determinacy to χ_q, not the source of state actuality.

## 2. Decoherence

A decohering interaction can transform the physically instantiated state while the resulting reduced/pointer structure remains determinate under an appropriate state specification.

Where warranted, the interaction may also establish:

$$\operatorname{Spec}(\alpha,\psi,q).$$

But decoherence alone does not definitionally imply a unique:

$$\chi_q.$$

**PASS.** Decoherence can alter/specify state structure without being equated to settlement.

## 3. Everett / Many-Worlds

The global state may remain determinate under q_ψ:

$$\chi_{q_\psi}.$$

Decoherent branch-relative records can be represented with branch-indexed specifications q_b.

Then different branches may contain different:

$$\chi_{q_b}$$

without contradiction because q_b differs by branch/context.

For a non-branch-indexed unique-outcome q, Everett need not supply one global χ_q.

**PASS AS RIVAL TYPING.** QSA neither forces nor refutes Everett at this stage.

The key result is that state actuality χ_{q_ψ} does not imply that every branch alternative is one global determinate χ_q.

## 4. Bohmian mechanics

The wavefunction may be determinate as ψ:

$$\chi_{q_\psi}.$$

The actual configuration supplies additional determinate claims under configuration/position q.

Other operator-associated values need not be globally pre-assigned independently of context.

**PASS.** State determinacy and observable/value determinacy remain distinct.

## 5. Kochen-Specker contextuality

Kochen-Specker blocks broad noncontextual global value assignments.

QSA requires no such assignment.

A state claim q_ψ may be determinate while multiple incompatible observable claims q_i remain unsettled or context-dependent.

**PASS.** Specification-relative determinacy is compatible with the prohibition on indiscriminate global value assignment.

## 6. Non-bivalent / paraconsistent representations

A non-classical formal representation of ψ may still support a determinate state claim q_ψ.

The relevant falsifier remains a determinate bounded claim requiring both P and not-P under exactly the same q.

Representational non-bivalence does not automatically become contradictory χ_q.

**PASS PROVISIONALLY.**

## 7. Unitary evolution without measurement

At each modeled stage, ψ(t) may be determinate under its state specification q_{ψ(t)}.

An arbitrary observable q need not be settled.

**PASS.** This is the clearest demonstration that “under-determinate” must always be q-relative rather than a global property of ψ.

## 8. L3 under the revised typing

L3 applies to q_ψ:

- ψ is ψ;
- the system is not both in ψ and not in ψ under the same state claim;
- under fixed q_ψ, the system is in ψ or it is not.

L3 also applies to settled observable q:

- χ_q is self-identical;
- χ_q does not require P and not-P as the same determinate fact;
- under fixed settled q, P or not-P obtains.

No inference is licensed from:

$$L_3(\chi_{q_\psi})$$

to:

$$\chi_q(P)\land\chi_q(\neg P).$$

## 9. Result

**PASS v2.**

Specification-relative determinacy improves the original QSA split:

- it preserves physical and logical reality of ψ;
- it avoids treating amplitudes as already-settled facts;
- it avoids global definite-value realism;
- it preserves L3 across state and outcome claims;
- it remains interpretation-neutral across the tested families.

## 10. Remaining falsifier

QSA would face a direct logical counterexample if a physically warranted determinate claim required:

> the same subject, under the same predicate, conditions, respect, and context, to instantiate P and not-P as the same settled fact.

Different states, branches, contexts, observables, times, or specifications do not satisfy that falsifier.
