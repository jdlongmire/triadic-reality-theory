# Level 1 Notation Decision

**WP:** WP-TRT-QSA-0001
**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Status:** notation freeze v1

## 1. Level 0 remains canonical

The canonical compact expression remains:

$$\boxed{\chi \equiv \mathcal{A}(I \mid L)}$$

Read: **Logic constrains. Information is the representable content. Action actualizes. χ is the determinate result.**

The bar denotes constraint. It does not denote chronology and does not imply that information is unreal prior to χ. Bare χ remains licensed at Level 0.

## 2. Primitive ontology

TRT retains exactly the three canonical co-primitives:

$$\langle L_3,I_\infty,A\rangle.$$

No QSA notation introduces a fourth co-primitive.

Use:

$$\alpha : \mathbf{Act}$$

for an occurrence of primitive Action. A names the primitive role/category; α ranges over action occurrences.

## 3. Information and state notation

Use:

$$\sigma \in I_\infty$$

for a particular representable informational structure. Representability does not entail coherence, actualizability, physical instantiation, or determinate fact actuality.

Use the predicate:

$$\operatorname{Inst}(\sigma)$$

to mean that σ is physically instantiated as a state. This is a predicate, not an independent instantiation engine.

A physically instantiated state is determinate actuality under its state specification q_σ. It need not be a determinate value/fact under a different observable specification q.

## 4. Bounded claim / respect

Use provisionally:

$$q=\langle x,P,c,r\rangle$$

where x is the subject/system, P the predicate/property/observable, c the relevant conditions including temporal/contextual conditions where applicable, and r the respect under which P is asserted.

The tuple is analytical structure, not four new primitives. A contradiction test must hold q fixed. Changing x, P, c, or r produces a different bounded claim.

## 5. Specification is a derived relation

Freeze:

$$\operatorname{Spec}(\alpha,\sigma,q)$$

meaning that action occurrence α, with respect to available or instantiated σ, fixes q as the bounded claim/respect in force.

Do not canonically write S(α,σ)=q yet. QSA has not established that specification is single-valued or functional.

Specification is downstream, is a role some action occurrences acquire, does not imply conscious choice, and does not settle a value.

## 6. Settlement is a derived relation

Freeze:

$$\operatorname{Settled}(\alpha,q,\chi_q)$$

meaning that action occurrence α settles bounded claim q as determinate fact χ_q.

Do not use A(q) for settlement. A is reserved for primitive Action. Do not assume settlement is functional until uniqueness is established.

## 7. Determinate fact actuality

Use:

$$\chi_q$$

for determinate actuality under fixed q.

χ_q is narrower than bare Level 0 χ. At Level 1, for the state claim q_σ, warranted physical instantiation supports χ_{q_σ}. For a distinct observable/value claim q, Inst(σ) does not entail χ_q. A physically instantiated state may therefore be determinate as state while under-determinate relative to another q.

## 8. Actual domain

Use X for the typed actual domain, consistent with:

$$R \xrightarrow{L_3\;\mathrm{necessary\ filter}} D \xrightarrow{A} X.$$

In Lean, preserve the existing Actual type pending formal refactor. Bare χ remains the compact Level 0 result; X prevents domain actuality from being conflated with χ_q.

## 9. L3 typing

Preserve the hard-core claim:

$$L_3=\{\mathrm{Identity},\mathrm{NonContradiction},\mathrm{ExcludedMiddle}\}.$$

QSA makes explicit that L3 constrains determinate χ_q under fixed q. It does not claim this is L3's only role; the broader canonical admissibility role remains in force.

Candidate direction:

$$\operatorname{Settled}(\alpha,q,\chi_q)\Rightarrow\operatorname{L3Admissible}(\chi_q).$$

The converse is not asserted.

## 10. Typed action grades

These are roles/statuses, not new primitives.

**Grade 0: non-specifying action.** α : Act with no established Spec relation. The action may change or sustain instantiated structure without fixing a bounded claim.

**Grade 1: specifying action.** Spec(α,σ,q). The action fixes the relevant bounded claim. No value is thereby settled.

**Grade 2: settling action.** Settled(α,q,χ_q). The action settles a determinate fact under the bounded claim.

“Grade” denotes analytical role depth, not temporal order, causal rank, or separate ontological species.

## 11. Quantum stress-case notation

For a prepared quantum state, Inst(ψ) may hold.

If ψ represents alternatives relative to a basis/observable, those alternatives are not thereby each χ_q.

A downstream physical interaction may satisfy Spec(α,ψ,q) without yet warranting Settled(α,q,χ_q).

A determinate recorded fact is represented only when the settlement relation is warranted.

This notation is interpretation-neutral. It does not by itself assert collapse, branching, hidden variables, relational actualization, or observer-caused measurement.

## 12. Decoherence discipline

Do not canonically define decoherence as Spec.

Permitted provisional statement:

> A decohering environmental interaction may instantiate the derived specification role when it physically fixes or stabilizes the relevant pointer respect represented by q.

Whether a given decoherence model warrants Spec(α,σ,q) is a physics-facing claim requiring independent support. Decoherence does not by definition entail Settled(α,q,χ_q).

## 13. Mapping to Level 0

| Level 0 | Level 1 expansion |
|---|---|
| L | constraint structure; explicitly L3 where the three fundamental logical constraints are at issue |
| I | I∞, particular σ, and typed representational content |
| 𝒜 | primitive Action A; occurrences α : Act; Spec and Settled are derived roles |
| χ | compact determinate result; X is actual domain and χ_q is bounded determinate fact at Level 1 |

Level 1 unpacks χ ≡ 𝒜(I | L) without replacing it.

## 14. Prohibited notation / readings

Until separately demonstrated, do not canonize:

- S as a co-primitive;
- A(q) as an actualization operator;
- S(α,σ)=q as a function;
- Settled(α,q)=χ_q as a function;
- Inst(σ)=χ_q;
- χ as both total actual domain and bounded determinate fact in the same Level 1 argument;
- “pre-χ = unreal”;
- “superposition = P and not-P as simultaneously settled classical facts”;
- “decoherence = determinate outcome” by definition;
- “L3 applies only to χ_q”;
- temporal ordering among Inst, Spec, and Settled merely from the notation.

## 15. Frozen governing sentence

> **Action is fundamental. Specification is a derived role of an action when it bounds a respect. Settlement is a derived role/result of an action when that bounded claim obtains as determinate actuality. χ remains the compact result; χ_q is its Level 1 determinate-fact type.**

## Disposition

**FROZEN v1.** This notation is sufficient to proceed to the QSA quantum adversarial stress test and Level 0 ↔ Level 1 mapping proof. Any change during those steps must be recorded as a notation amendment rather than silently introduced.
