## Primary-source findings

### Wigner (1960)

Primary article verified: Eugene P. Wigner, "The Unreasonable Effectiveness of
Mathematics in the Natural Sciences," Communications on Pure and Applied Mathematics
13(1):1-14, DOI 10.1002/cpa.3160130102.

Relevance: establishes the classic source-side puzzle concerning the unexpectedly
powerful fit between mathematical structures and physical law. It does not provide
IIA's typed information/semantic architecture.

Confidence: HIGH.

### Polkinghorne (1991)

Primary article verified in Perspectives on Science and Christian Faith. Polkinghorne
explicitly presents the mathematical intelligibility of the physical world as a
meta-question and proposes that "reason within" and "reason without" have a common
origin in the Rationality of the Creator.

Relevance: direct antecedent for the common-rational-ground explanation of the
world/mind correspondence.

Confidence: HIGH.

### Floridi (2003/2005/2009/2011)

Primary publications verified. Floridi develops semantic information beyond merely
quantitative/statistical information and, in his veridical account, requires truth
for semantic information relevant to epistemic purposes. His correctness theory
connects semantic information, adequate models, verification/validation, and access
to the represented system.

Relevance: materially overlaps IIA's C -> T -> K region. IIA must not claim novelty
for linking semantic information and truth.

Confidence: HIGH.
