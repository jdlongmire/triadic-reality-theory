## Material antecedent discovered

Dretske is decisive for novelty control. His information-theoretic epistemology
already maps source and receiver onto known and knower and treats a reliable channel
as necessary to knowledge. Therefore the source/channel/receiver epistemic framing
cannot be claimed as original to IIA.

Polkinghorne is decisive for the theological fit argument. He explicitly argues that
the reason within the human mind and reason without in the mathematically intelligible
world have a coherent explanation in common origin in divine Rationality. Therefore
the two-ended rational correspondence cannot be claimed as original to IIA.

Bar-Hillel/Carnap and later semantic-information work decisively predate IIA's
Shannon/semantic distinction.
