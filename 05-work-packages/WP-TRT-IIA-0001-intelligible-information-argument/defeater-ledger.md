## D1 — Illicit transmitter

**Objection:** Source/channel/receiver vocabulary imports an intentional sender and
therefore begs the question.

**Assessment:** VALID against careless formulations.

**Control:** "Source" means the mind-independent state/domain about which information
is recovered. "TX" is heuristic only. No intentional transmitter appears in the
premises. Prior Mind may appear only as an abductive explanatory hypothesis.

## D2 — Equivocation on information

**Objection:** IIA slides from Shannon information to semantic information.

**Assessment:** CRITICAL.

**Control:** Maintain typed distinctions:
- I_S: Shannon/statistical information;
- I_sem: semantic content;
- K: truth-apt knowledge.
No inference I_S -> I_sem is permitted without an explicit bridge account.

## D3 — Dretske naturalization

**Objection:** Lawful covariance between source and receiver can support objective
information and cognition without antecedent Mind.

**Assessment:** SERIOUS. This directly attacks any claim that source/receiver
correspondence itself favors theism.

**Required response:** IIA must show that its explanandum includes something not
exhausted by lawful covariance: truth-aptness, intentional aboutness, rational
normativity, or the global intelligibility enabling successful theoretical
representation. Merely restating these does not establish that naturalization fails.

## D4 — Evolutionary reliabilism

**Objection:** Receivers track environmental structure because organisms with
reliable-enough representations are selected.

**Assessment:** SERIOUS for receiver adequacy; incomplete as an account of why the
source domain is mathematically intelligible, but IIA must not assume that source
intelligibility requires further explanation.

**Required test:** Separate survival-relevant tracking from abstract mathematical
and theoretical cognition without relying on incredulity.

## D5 — Structural realism

**Objection:** The world has structure and successful theories recover that
structure. No semantic transmitter is required.

**Assessment:** SERIOUS. Potentially explains much of the source-model fit.

**Required test:** Ask whether structural realism explains the existence of
rationally accessible structure and semantic/rational agents or treats one or both
as primitives. Explanatory stopping points must be compared symmetrically.

## D6 — Teleosemantics

**Objection:** Biological function grounds representational content naturally.

**Assessment:** SERIOUS for semantic content. IIA cannot assert that semantics is
non-naturalizable without engaging function-based accounts.

## D7 — Computational functionalism / emergentism

**Objection:** Semantic cognition supervenes on or emerges from sufficiently
organized physical computation.

**Assessment:** OPEN. "Emergence" is not sufficient merely as a label, but a detailed
functional or mechanistic account cannot be dismissed by demanding that lower-level
parts individually possess the higher-level property.

**Control:** Evaluate causal and explanatory bridges, multiple realization,
intentionality, truth conditions, and normativity. Avoid composition fallacies in
either direction.

## D8 — Selection / anthropic conditioning

**Objection:** Only agents in cognitively tractable environments can ask why their
environment is tractable.

**Assessment:** PARTIAL. May reduce surprise of observer-world fit, but does not by
itself establish an ontology of intelligibility or semantics.

## D9 — Brute fact

**Objection:** The world-mind correspondence has no deeper explanation.

**Assessment:** LOGICALLY AVAILABLE. IIA is abductive, so brute fact cannot be
deductively excluded. It can only be compared on explanatory cost and stopping
principles.

## D10 — Polkinghorne collapse

**Objection:** IIA is Polkinghorne's reason-within/reason-without argument expressed
in information-theoretic language.

**Assessment:** CURRENTLY THE PRINCIPAL NOVELTY DEFEATER.

**Required test:** Determine whether Shannon/semantic typing plus Dretske's
source-channel-receiver epistemology changes the inferential content or merely the
vocabulary. Unless it produces a new discriminating premise, objection, prediction,
or explanatory comparison, final disposition should be REFORMULATION or
DISTINCT_SYNTHESIS rather than DISTINCT_ARGUMENT.
