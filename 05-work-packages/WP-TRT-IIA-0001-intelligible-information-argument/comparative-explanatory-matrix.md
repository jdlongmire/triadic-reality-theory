This matrix is descriptive, not a scorecard. It records explanatory commitments,
stopping points, and research burdens symmetrically.

## Comparison rules

1. Do not penalize an account merely for having multiple mechanisms.
2. Do not reward H_RG merely for verbal unification.
3. Count a "common cause" as explanatory only if it constrains or makes expected
   features of both source and receiver.
4. Treat primitives symmetrically. A rational ground can be a stopping point just as
   physical law or structure can.
5. Distinguish ontological economy from explanatory scope.
6. Compare actual developed theories, not slogans ("emergence", "design", "evolution").
7. Record empirical discriminators where available; where none exist, mark the
   comparison metaphysical/abductive rather than empirical.

## Current finding

H_RG has a potential unification advantage because it targets both ends of the
correspondence with one provenance claim. That advantage is presently only
promissory. Unless the rational-ground hypothesis supplies constraints or bridges
that alternatives lack, "common rational source" risks redescribing the explanandum.

Naturalistic alternatives have mature mechanisms for covariance, learning,
biological function, and computation. Their principal pressure points for IIA are
semantic aboutness, truth conditions, rational normativity, abstract generalization,
and whether source-side mathematical tractability is explained or accepted as a
background feature.

No comparative winner is declared at this stage.
