# Gates 1-4 Research Note: Flexible Determinism and Constrained Actualization

**Work package:** WP-TRT-FD-0001  
**Status:** Research finding, pre-canonical  
**Date:** 2026-10-03

## 1. Definitions gate

### Determinism
For a complete state S(t) and governing dynamics D, determinism in the strict dynamical sense requires a unique successor history:

    (S(t), D) -> exactly one S(t+dt).

On this standard sense, "flexible determinism" is potentially misleading if TRT permits several physically admissible successors from the same complete physical antecedent state.

### Constrained actualization
TRT's candidate thesis is more precisely:

    Adm_L(S,M) = {I_1, ..., I_n}

    mu_L,S,M : Adm_L(S,M) -> [0,1]

    sum_k mu(I_k) = 1

    chi_k = A(I_k | L,S,M)

where one admissible outcome becomes actual.

The theory therefore distinguishes:
- **admissibility:** what may actualize under L;
- **probability:** the lawful measure over admissible outcomes;
- **actuality:** which outcome obtains;
- **actualization:** the ontological transition represented by A.

### Randomness
At least three senses must remain separate:
1. epistemic randomness: outcome unknown to an observer;
2. stochastic-law randomness: theory assigns a non-degenerate probability distribution;
3. ontological randomness: no further fact, cause, or determinant selects the individual outcome.

A demonstration of (2) does not by itself establish (3).

### Gate 1 disposition
**RENAME recommended at canonicalization unless a narrower technical definition is defended.**
Retain "flexible determinism" as the originating heuristic. Use **constrained actualization** as the technically preferred thesis.

## 2. Bell/Born gate

### Bell boundary
Bell (1964) considers completion of quantum mechanics by additional variables intended to restore causality and locality. Bell explicitly identifies locality as the essential difficulty and derives statistical consequences incompatible with quantum predictions for the relevant class of local completions.

CHSH (1969) operationalizes the issue for realizable experiments and describes its target as the family of **local hidden-variable theories**.

Loophole-free Bell tests such as Hensen et al. (2015) reject the tested local-realist null under the experiment's assumptions and statistical analysis.

Therefore the unrestricted statement:

    "Bell proved there are no hidden variables"

is false or materially incomplete.

Bell-type results do not exclude every hidden-variable ontology. Nonlocal hidden-variable theories are outside the simple unrestricted inference.

### Born boundary
The Born rule supplies probabilities for measurement outcomes. A probability assignment is part of the predictive formalism. The formal assignment alone does not adjudicate among all competing ontologies of the measurement event.

For the polarization example:

    P(pass | vertical preparation, 45-degree analyzer) = cos^2(45 degrees) = 1/2.

This establishes a lawful probability distribution. It does not, without an additional interpretive premise, entail:

    the individual event is metaphysically causeless,
    or
    no fact beyond the probability distribution bears on its actualization.

### Gate 2 disposition
**PASS with qualification.**
TRT may state that Bell/Born results do not establish unrestricted ontological randomness. TRT may not claim that Bell experiments establish constrained actualization, primitive Action, or a deterministic completion.

## 3. Interpretation stress test

| Interpretation | Admissible alternatives? | Individual outcome account | Deterministic at fundamental level? | Relation to constrained actualization |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bohmian mechanics | Effective alternatives relative to configuration | Determined by wavefunction + configuration via nonlocal dynamics | Yes | Shows Bell does not entail unrestricted indeterminism; may make primitive A unnecessary |
| Everettian | Branch structure contains outcomes | No unique global collapse outcome; branching/decoherence | Typically unitary/deterministic | Challenges TRT's assumption that exactly one chi_k becomes actual |
| Objective collapse | Multiple pre-collapse possibilities | Stochastic physical collapse selects outcome | No, ordinarily | Close structural competitor; threatens TRT novelty if A merely renames collapse |
| Operational/Copenhagen family | Measurement alternatives represented probabilistically | Formalism predicts outcomes; ontology varies by version | Not uniformly characterized | Demonstrates that predictive adequacy need not answer the actualization ontology |
| TRT constrained actualization | Yes, under Adm_L | A maps an admissible informational state to determinate actuality chi | Open | Adds content only if A is irreducible and more than a label for outcome occurrence |

### Gate 3 finding
TRT cannot infer constrained actualization merely because standard quantum mechanics is probabilistic. The strongest current role of quantum theory is **compatibility and pressure testing**, not confirmation.

Everett is the sharpest challenge to the "exactly one outcome actualizes" premise. Bohmian mechanics is the sharpest challenge to any inference from Bell to fundamental indeterminism. Objective collapse is the sharpest competitor to TRT's A if A is construed as stochastic selection.

### Gate 3 disposition
**QUALIFY.**
Constrained actualization remains a viable metaphysical model, but no quantum-interpretive discriminator has yet been established.

## 4. Action non-redundancy test

TRT's hard-core equation is:

    chi = A(I | L).

The adversarial question is whether A can be eliminated.

### Elimination attempt A: actuality as information
Suppose I is expanded to include the complete actual state. Then:

    chi = I_actual.

A appears redundant.

**Failure diagnosis:** this succeeds only by building actuality into the definition of I. TRT defines I as distinguishable, representable content. Representability permits specification of mutually incompatible possibilities. Encoding "actual" as another informational predicate does not by itself account for why that predicate is instantiated rather than merely represented.

This is a semantic relocation of the actuality problem, not yet an elimination.

### Elimination attempt B: actuality as logical entailment
Suppose L plus complete I entails chi:

    L + I_complete |- chi.

If the entailment is sufficient for physical actuality, A is redundant.

**Failure diagnosis:** logical entailment preserves consequence relations among propositions/states. A valid description of an admissible or even uniquely specified state does not entail that the state physically obtains unless a bridge premise is added:

    unique representability/entailment -> actuality.

That bridge is precisely what requires justification. Calling it a law does not remove the bridge.

### Elimination attempt C: actuality as physical dynamics
Let D be a complete physical transition law:

    S_t --D--> S_t+1.

Then A may appear to be nothing beyond D.

**Serious challenge:** if D is taken as an ontologically primitive fact that genuinely produces/constitutes the successor state, D is performing the role TRT assigns to Action. TRT can rename D as an instance of A, but that alone does not demonstrate a distinct primitive. The irreducibility claim requires showing that every adequate D already presupposes an actualizing relation rather than reducing it.

This is currently the strongest pressure point.

### Elimination attempt D: block ontology
If the complete history simply exists tenselessly, no transition from possibility to actuality is fundamental. A may be dispensable.

**Serious challenge:** TRT must either show that a block ontology still presupposes instantiation/actuality of the block, or concede that temporal "actualization" language is non-fundamental. The stronger TRT formulation should treat A as **instantiation/obtaining**, of which temporal transition is one manifestation, rather than defining A solely as temporal becoming.

### Non-redundancy result
A provisional transcendental-style distinction survives:

    L constrains.
    I differentiates/specifies.
    Neither, merely as constraint or specification, is identical to obtaining.

If there is a real distinction between a representable state and a state that obtains, an adequacy condition remains:

    representation + constraint != actuality

unless an additional actualizing/instantiating relation is supplied.

TRT names that irreducible role A.

This establishes a **candidate necessity argument**, not yet a proof that A is a numerically distinct metaphysical substance, force, or physical process.

### Gate 4 disposition
**PASS provisionally, with a major open burden.**
The elimination attempts show that removing A tends either to (a) smuggle actuality into I, (b) add an unargued entailment-to-existence bridge to L, or (c) assign the actualizing role to physical dynamics under another name. However, option (c) prevents a completed irreducibility proof. TRT must show that Action is a category required by any adequate dynamics, not merely TRT's preferred label for dynamics.

## Consolidated disposition

1. **Flexible determinism:** RENAME at canonicalization unless further analysis rescues the term.
2. **Constrained actualization:** PROMOTE from heuristic to qualified candidate thesis.
3. **Bell claim:** Bell constrains local hidden-variable/local-realist completions; it does not prove "no hidden variables."
4. **Quantum randomness claim:** probabilistic prediction does not alone prove ontological bruteness.
5. **Primitive Action:** survives the first elimination audit, but remains under a serious dynamics-reduction challenge.
6. **Empirical status:** no claim of quantum confirmation of TRT is warranted.

## Next research gate

The next package activity should formalize the **Actuality Gap Argument (AGA)**:

    P1. L determines relations of admissibility/consequence.
    P2. I determines distinguishable/representable content.
    P3. A complete specification of what is admissible or representable is not identical to the fact that some state obtains.
    P4. Any adequate ontology must account for obtaining/instantiation.
    C. Therefore an adequate ontology requires an actualizing/instantiating category not reducible merely to L or I.

The critical work is P3. It must be attacked from necessitarianism, mathematical structuralism, block-universe ontology, Humean laws, dispositional essentialism, Bohmian dynamics, Everettian unitary evolution, and ordinary physicalism.
