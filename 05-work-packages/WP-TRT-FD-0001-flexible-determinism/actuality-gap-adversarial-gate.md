# Actuality Gap Argument (AGA): Adversarial Gate

**Work package:** WP-TRT-FD-0001  
**Status:** Foundational candidate, pre-canonical  
**Date:** 2026-10-03

## 1. Target claim

TRT requires a third primitive only if actuality cannot be reduced without remainder to logical constraint (L) plus distinguishable/representable information (I).

Candidate AGA:

1. **L-role:** Logic determines consistency, consequence, and admissibility relations.
2. **I-role:** Information determines distinguishable, representable content.
3. **Actuality distinction:** A complete specification of what is representable, admissible, or entailed is not identical to the fact that a state obtains.
4. **Adequacy:** An ontology of reality must account for obtaining/instantiation.
5. **Conclusion:** An adequate ontology requires an actualizing/instantiating category not reducible merely to L or I.

TRT denotes this category A and the actualized result chi:

    chi = A(I | L)

The argument is categorical. It does not require temporal becoming, quantum collapse, libertarian agency, or indeterministic physics.

## 2. Clarification of P3

P3 must not be defended by merely asserting that "possible is not actual." The stronger claim is:

    Specification(x) does not entail Instantiation(x)

unless the ontology contains a bridge principle B such that:

    B: specified/admissible x -> x obtains.

If B is logical, the challenge is to show why existence follows from syntax/semantics.
If B is informational, the challenge is to distinguish instantiated information from merely representable information without presupposing actuality.
If B is physical dynamics, the challenge is to state what ontological role makes the dynamics productive/instantiating rather than merely descriptive.

The AGA succeeds only if proposed reductions relocate rather than eliminate this role.

## 3. Rival ontology attacks

### 3.1 Humean mosaic / Best System

**Attack:** Start with the complete mosaic of particular facts. Laws supervene on that total history as the best systematization. There is no additional governing or actualizing entity.

**Result:** This defeats any claim that A must be a separate governing mechanism. It does not defeat the categorical AGA, because the Humean begins with the actual mosaic. "Particular fact" already means something obtains. Actuality is primitive in the base rather than derived from law.

**TRT consequence:** A must not be defined as a supernatural or extra-physical governor. At minimum it denotes the irreducible actuality/instantiation role that Humeanism itself places in its base facts.

### 3.2 Dispositional essentialism

**Attack:** Fundamental properties are powers/potencies whose essences ground laws and manifestations. No separate actualizer is required.

**Result:** Stronger challenge. A potency account can claim that manifestation is internal to the dispositional nature of properties. But a disposition that never manifests remains intelligible; the identity of a potency does not by itself identify which token manifestation obtains at a given occasion without stimulus/conditions and an instantiated bearer. If the full disposition-plus-condition complex necessitates manifestation, the account supplies an ontic production/manifestation relation. That relation performs A's categorical role even if it is internal to the property's essence.

**TRT consequence:** A need not be numerically separate from every bearer/property. Co-primitivity is role irreducibility, not necessarily substance multiplication.

### 3.3 Necessitarianism

**Attack:** Given complete essences and necessary laws, the actual history follows necessarily. There is no actuality gap.

**Result:** Necessitation can close a modal gap between antecedent and consequent states conditional on an instantiated antecedent. It does not explain why the necessitating structure and antecedent obtain rather than remain a complete possible structure. A necessitarian can declare the entire structure necessary. That moves the dispute to whether necessary existence/instantiation is reducible to logical necessity.

**TRT consequence:** AGA does not refute necessitarianism. It forces it to identify its existence bridge. If "necessary" means necessarily instantiated, actuality has been included in the primitive modal claim.

### 3.4 Maudlin-style law primitivism / dynamics

**Attack:** Laws are ontologically primitive and govern temporal evolution. A complete fundamental law of temporal evolution plus state is enough.

**Result:** This is the strongest reduction candidate. If primitive lawhood includes genuine production/governance of successor states, then the law has absorbed the A-role. This challenges TRT only if TRT claims A must be an entity additional to primitive law. It does not eliminate the role distinction: constraint as such and actual production remain conceptually different jobs, even if one primitive law performs both.

**TRT consequence:** Revise the irreducibility claim to functional/categorical irreducibility. TRT should not infer three separate substances from three irreducible explanatory roles.

### 3.5 Block universe / eternalism

**Attack:** The four-dimensional history simply exists. Nothing transitions from possible to actual. Therefore A is an artifact of presentist/becoming language.

**Result:** This defeats a temporal-transition definition of A. It does not defeat an instantiation definition. The block itself either obtains or is merely a representable four-dimensional structure. A is therefore better defined as **actuality/instantiation/obtaining**, with temporal actualization as a derivative case.

**TRT consequence:** Canonical language should broaden A from "primitive action/actualization" to "primitive action/instantiation (actualization)" where context requires. Do not commit TRT to a theory of temporal passage through this argument.

### 3.6 Bohmian dynamics

Bohm's 1952 hidden-variable interpretation explicitly claims that hidden variables determine precise individual measurement results in principle. A deterministic completion can therefore supply a unique trajectory.

**Attack:** Wavefunction + configuration + guiding dynamics determine the outcome. No primitive stochastic actualization is needed.

**Result:** This defeats any identification of A with stochastic selection. It does not eliminate instantiated configuration, instantiated wavefunction/state, or the obtaining trajectory. Bohmian dynamics can occupy the A-role if construed as genuinely productive; if construed mathematically/descriptively, the instantiation question remains.

**TRT consequence:** A is neutral between deterministic and stochastic physics.

### 3.7 Everettian unitary evolution

Everett's relative-state formulation removes collapse as a fundamental dynamical postulate and treats the universal quantum state through unitary evolution.

**Attack:** There is no unique global outcome-selection event for A to perform.

**Result:** This defeats the earlier formulation "exactly one admissible I_k becomes actual" as a universal metaphysical requirement. TRT must permit a physically instantiated structure containing multiple relative outcome records if Everett is correct. The A-role concerns whether that universal state/history obtains, not necessarily selection of one branch.

**TRT consequence:** Remove unique-outcome selection from the core constrained-actualization thesis. It may be interpretation-specific, not TRT-hard-core.

### 3.8 Reductive physicalism

**Attack:** To say that physical state P obtains just is to state the complete physical fact. Asking what further actualizes P demands an unnecessary extra entity.

**Result:** Correct against reification. AGA does not warrant a further "actualizer object." But the response concedes the categorical datum: an obtaining physical fact differs from a merely represented physical state. Physicalism may take obtaining as primitive. TRT labels that primitive role A only if the label earns systematic work elsewhere.

**TRT consequence:** Avoid hypostatizing A. The argument supports irreducible role/category, not an additional physical object.

## 4. Revised formal result

The strongest defensible result is weaker and cleaner than the initial proposal:

**Actuality Gap Principle (AGP)**

    For any representationally complete specification R of a state s,
    R(s) does not by representational completeness alone entail Obtains(s).

Or:

    CompleteRepresentability(s) != Actuality(s).

And for admissibility:

    Admissible_L(s) != Obtains(s).

An ontology can close the gap in several ways:
- primitive particular facts (Humean);
- primitive governing laws (Maudlin);
- dispositional manifestation;
- deterministic dynamics;
- stochastic collapse;
- instantiated universal wavefunction/history;
- another ontology.

TRT's claim should be:

    Any adequate ontology must contain, presuppose, or ground an actuality/instantiation role.

TRT denotes the irreducible role A. Competing theories may locate that role differently.

## 5. What AGA establishes and does not establish

### Establishes, provisionally
1. L as mere constraint/consequence is insufficient for actuality.
2. I as mere distinguishability/representability is insufficient for actuality.
3. Reductions of actuality tend to presuppose instantiated facts, productive law/dynamics, manifestation, or necessary existence.
4. Therefore the actuality role cannot simply disappear from an adequate ontology.

### Does not establish
1. A is a separate substance, field, force, or event.
2. A is stochastic.
3. A selects exactly one quantum branch/outcome.
4. Quantum mechanics confirms TRT.
5. Temporal becoming is fundamental.
6. A must be additional to every rival ontology's primitives.

## 6. Effect on constrained actualization

Revise the earlier formulation:

Old candidate:
    chi_k = A(I_k | L,S,M), exactly one k.

More interpretation-neutral:
    chi = A(I | L)

with domain-specific admissibility:
    I_actualized subseteq Adm_L(S,M)

and no hard-core assertion that |I_actualized| = 1.

For single-outcome theories:
    |I_actualized| = 1.

For Everett-type theories:
    I_actualized may be a structured universal state containing multiple relative records.

This keeps TRT upstream of quantum interpretation.

## 7. Disposition

**AGA: QUALIFIED PROMOTION recommended.**

Promote the Actuality Gap Principle as a foundational TRT constraint:

    representability/admissibility does not, by itself, constitute actuality.

Promote A as an irreducible **ontological role/category** required to account for instantiation/obtaining.

Do **not** yet promote the stronger claim that A is a numerically distinct primitive entity. Co-primitivity should be stated as irreducibility of ontological role pending further formal work.

**Flexible determinism:** RENAME.

**Preferred research term:** constrained actualization.

**Quantum unique-outcome clause:** REMOVE from TRT hard core.

## 8. Next formal gate

Formalize role irreducibility:

    L-role = constraint/consequence
    I-role = distinction/specification
    A-role = instantiation/obtaining

Then test pairwise reducibility:

    A ->? L
    A ->? I
    L ->? A + I
    I ->? L + A

The target theorem is not "three substances exist." It is:

    No two roles, under their canonical definitions, are jointly sufficient to define the third without importing a bridge premise that performs the third role.

This would be a stronger and more defensible foundation for TRT's co-primitivity thesis.
