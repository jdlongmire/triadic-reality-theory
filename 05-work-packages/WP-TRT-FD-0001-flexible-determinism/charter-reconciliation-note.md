The hard core as it stands asserts no unique-outcome clause. The charter commits the programme to a single globally consistent actualization and to A actualizing one branch. The level at which the single-outcome commitment sits is for the programme lead to state.
3. **Becoming.** The gate advises against committing TRT to temporal passage. The charter commits to sequential growth in which each A-event is a causal-set birth. No reconciliation is asserted here.
4. **Terminology.** Gate 1 recommends "constrained actualization" as the technical term in place of "flexible determinism". No canonical term has been adopted.
