# L-I-A Pairwise Irreducibility Gate

**Work package:** WP-TRT-FD-0001  
**Status:** Foundational candidate, pre-canonical  
**Date:** 2026-10-03

## 1. Purpose

Test the TRT co-primitivity thesis symmetrically. No primitive receives protected status.

Canonical roles:

    L = constraint / consistency / consequence
    I = distinction / determinate specification / representable content
    A = instantiation / obtaining / actuality-role

The target is role irreducibility, not proof of three substances:

> No two canonical roles are jointly sufficient to define the third without importing a bridge premise that performs the allegedly reduced role.

A successful reduction must be non-circular, role-preserving, and bridge-free.

## 2. Reduction criterion

Let R_X be the canonical role of X.

A reduction

    X := f(Y,Z)

succeeds only if:

1. f uses only the canonical resources of Y and Z;
2. f does not include X, a synonym of X, or an operation already performing R_X;
3. the output performs the full canonical role R_X;
4. removing X does not leave an explanatory remainder that must be supplied by a bridge premise.

If a bridge B is required and B performs R_X, the reduction relocates X rather than eliminates it.

## 3. Can A be reduced to L + I?

Candidate:

    A := f(L,I).

L supplies admissibility/consequence. I supplies determinate representable content.

### Attempt A1: unique specification
If L and I uniquely specify s, declare s actual.

Failure:

    Unique_L(I=s) -> Obtains(s)

requires a bridge from uniqueness/specification to obtaining. Uniqueness among representations or admissible states is not itself instantiation.

### Attempt A2: complete physical state
Define I to include the complete physical state, including which state obtains.

Failure: actuality is inserted into I. "Actual physical state" already performs the A-role.

### Attempt A3: deterministic law
Let L include a dynamical law and I include an instantiated antecedent state.

Qualified failure: the instantiated antecedent already presupposes A. If L is strengthened from constraint to productive governance, L has absorbed the A-role and changed its canonical definition.

### A disposition

    A not reducible to L + I

under the canonical role definitions.

Confidence: PROVISIONAL-HIGH at the conceptual level. The result is conditional on maintaining L as constraint/consequence and I as distinction/specification.

## 4. Can I be reduced to L + A?

Candidate:

    I := f(L,A).

L supplies relational constraint. A supplies obtaining/instantiation.

### Attempt I1: instantiated logic
Suppose an instantiated logical structure contains all information.

Failure: a structure can contain relational form while failing to specify which distinguishable state/token/value obtains unless distinctions are already encoded. The map from undifferentiated actuality plus relation to determinate content requires a differentiation operation.

### Attempt I2: actual differences
Define information as actual distinctions produced by A under L.

This is the strongest reduction candidate:

    I = distinctions-within-actuality.

But "distinction" is already the canonical I-role. A can make something obtain and L can constrain relations, but neither role alone supplies the determinate this-rather-than-that content unless the relata/states are specified.

### Attempt I3: structural position
A token is informationally determined entirely by its place in L-relations.

Challenge: pure structuralism may individuate nodes relationally. If every informational distinction is exhausted by relational position, I may collapse into L plus instantiated structure.

Counterpressure: L gives a relation schema; an instantiated relational structure still requires determinate occupancy/cardinality/state assignment to distinguish this realization from another isomorphic or differently valued realization. If L is expanded to include all token values and state assignments, it has absorbed specification and therefore the I-role.

### I disposition

    I not reducible to L + A

under the canonical role definitions.

Confidence: PROVISIONAL-MEDIUM. Structuralist accounts are the strongest unresolved pressure point. TRT must show that relation structure and instantiation do not exhaust determinate specification without assuming primitive haecceity.

## 5. Can L be reduced to I + A?

Candidate:

    L := f(I,A).

I supplies determinate content. A supplies what obtains.

### Attempt L1: regularity from actual information
Infer laws/logic from all actual informational states:

    {I_actual} -> L.

Failure: a collection of actual facts does not by itself supply necessity, validity, consistency, or consequence. Inferring a rule from a pattern requires a rule of inference or modal/generalizing relation.

This is especially clear for logical consequence: enumerating truths does not itself define what follows from what.

### Attempt L2: Humean best system
Let L be the optimal compression/systematization of the actual informational mosaic.

Challenge: this appears to derive lawlike structure from I + A.

Qualified failure for the canonical L-role: selecting the "best" system requires standards such as simplicity, strength, fit, consistency, and inferential consequence. More importantly, a descriptive best system may account for physical lawhood on a Humean theory but does not derive logical validity itself from the mosaic without using logic in constructing/evaluating the system.

Thus Humeanism is a serious reduction of governing physical law, but not of TRT's broader L-role.

### Attempt L3: all actual transitions
If every actual transition is informationally specified, define L as the total transition relation.

Failure: extensional enumeration gives what did occur. It does not by itself distinguish necessity from accident, permitted from forbidden, or valid consequence from mere co-occurrence.

### L disposition

    L not reducible to I + A

under the canonical role definitions.

Confidence: PROVISIONAL-HIGH for logical consequence; lower for physical-law structure specifically.

## 6. Symmetry result

The three failed reductions exhibit three distinct explanatory deficits:

    L + I -> lacks obtaining
    L + A -> lacks determinate specification
    I + A -> lacks consequence/constraint

Compactly:

    Constraint + Specification != Instantiation
    Constraint + Instantiation != Specification
    Specification + Instantiation != Constraint

where "!=" means "is not definitionally sufficient under the canonical role definitions," not numerical inequality.

## 7. Bridge-premise lemma

For X in {L,I,A}, suppose a proposed reduction of X from the other two requires bridge B.

If B performs the canonical role R_X, then:

    Reduce(X; Y,Z,B)

is not an elimination of X's role.

It is a relocation:

    R_X -> B.

Therefore a genuine reduction must show how R_X emerges from R_Y and R_Z without presupposing R_X in B.

This is the central formal guardrail against definitional smuggling.

## 8. Co-primitivity theorem candidate

**LIA Role-Irreducibility Thesis (LIA-RIT):**

Given the canonical role definitions:

    R_L = constraint/consequence
    R_I = distinction/specification
    R_A = instantiation/obtaining,

no pair is definitionally sufficient for the third without either:

1. importing a bridge premise that performs the missing role, or
2. expanding one retained role until it absorbs the missing role.

Therefore L, I, and A are candidate co-primitives at the level of ontological role.

### Proof status

This is not a theorem in a formal logical calculus yet. It is a structured philosophical argument with explicit reduction criteria and counterexample obligations.

Do not label it a proved theorem in canonical publications until the definitions are formalized in a typed system and the reduction relation is specified rigorously.

## 9. Strongest objections remaining

### O1. Structuralist reduction of I
A sufficiently rich relational structure may individuate all informational content without an independent I-role.

Required response: distinguish relation schema, token occupancy, value assignment, and identity conditions. Determine whether "rich structure" imports specification.

### O2. Nominalist/Humean reduction of L
Logical/modal structure may be treated as language, convention, or optimal description rather than ontology.

Required response: show whether the act of formulating/evaluating the nominalist reduction already depends on non-conventional consequence relations, or concede that TRT's L is a transcendental condition rather than an entity.

### O3. Deflationary reduction of A
"Obtains" may be treated as semantically redundant: 'p is actual' adds nothing to p.

Required response: distinguish semantic redundancy in assertion from ontological contrast between a represented state and an instantiated state. Avoid treating an actuality predicate as a physical property.

### O4. Role carving
The triad may be guaranteed irreducible merely because the definitions were stipulated to exclude one another.

Required response: demonstrate independent motivation for each role from explanatory adequacy, and test alternative carvings with two, four, or more primitives.

This is the most important methodological objection.

## 10. Disposition

**PAIRWISE IRREDUCIBILITY: QUALIFIED PASS.**

The symmetric reduction audit supports co-primitivity at the level of role. It does not yet prove ontological fundamentality.

**Promotion recommendation:** promote LIA-RIT as a named candidate thesis with explicit "argument, not theorem" status.

**Next gate:** anti-gerrymandering / alternative-basis test.

Ask whether the L-I-A basis is minimal and non-arbitrary:

1. Can two differently defined primitives cover all three roles without smuggling?
2. Is a fourth primitive required, such as modality, identity, relation, or value?
3. Are L, I, A orthogonal roles or merely one possible decomposition?
4. Does the triad survive reparameterization into alternative ontological bases?

Only after this gate should TRT claim that the triad is minimal rather than merely irreducible under its own definitions.
