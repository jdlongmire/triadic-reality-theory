# WP-TRT-SEO-0001 SEO Rationale and Adversarial Review

**Date:** 2026-09-29

## Search intent

The site should be discoverable for its proper name, **Triadic Reality Theory (TRT)**, and for the concepts it actually addresses: foundational ontology, ontological logic, information ontology, action ontology, Logic-Information-Action, representability versus actualizability, irreducible co-primitives, and the relation between logical admissibility and determinate actuality.

These phrases are used only where they accurately describe visible content. No page is created solely to capture a query.

## Metadata strategy

Each principal route has a unique descriptive title, description, canonical URL, H1, and visible subject statement. Shared metadata identifies the project, author, ORCID, version, repository, and licenses. JSON-LD uses WebPage/WebSite/CreativeWork rather than claiming peer-reviewed ScholarlyArticle status for the site itself.

Principal equations retain proper MathJax rendering and are surrounded by indexable prose that states their semantic content.

## Rejected tactics

- no keyword stuffing;
- no hidden or off-screen search text;
- no fabricated citation counts, institutional sponsorship, peer-review status, or scientific consensus;
- no doorway pages or near-duplicate query pages;
- no schema type that promotes a research programme into an established scientific result;
- no alteration of the TRT hard core for ranking;
- no claim that Google, Bing, or another engine has indexed or ranked the site until externally observed.

## Adversarial status review

SEO language continues to call TRT a **candidate foundational ontology and research programme**. Hard-core necessity and irreducibility remain described as argued. Formal predicates remain axiomatized where appropriate. Physics-facing work remains open/protective-belt work. The SEO layer therefore does not promote proof or empirical status.

**Disposition:** PASS for semantic integrity. External indexing remains an observed future state, not a completion claim.
