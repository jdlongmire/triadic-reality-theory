# Tx/Rx Living-System Signature: Terminology and First Severe-Test Note

**WP:** WP-TRT-TXRX-0001
**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Status:** Research note, first tranche
**Epistemic posture:** exploratory; candidate distinctions remain defeasible.

## 1. Problem

The working intuition is that living systems characteristically participate in informational reception and response, with progressively richer transmission and generative agency in animals and especially humans. The first task is to determine whether that intuition can be stated without collapsing physical correlation, biochemical signaling, cognition, semantics, and rational communication into one category.

Initial hierarchy:

    information-bearing -> information-sensitive -> information-responsive -> information-representing -> information-generating

The hierarchy survives initial review, but the first three terms require tighter typing.

## 2. Working vocabulary

### Information-bearing
A state is information-bearing relative to a discriminator when differences in that state can support distinctions about another state or history. This category alone makes no claim that the bearer senses, uses, understands, or represents the information.

### Rx (reception)
A system is an Rx system with respect to input x when a physically available difference in x is selectively coupled to an internal state transition of the system. Rx requires more than information being recoverable by an external observer. It does not by itself imply cognition, representation, semantics, or consciousness.

### Discrimination
A system discriminates x1 from x2 when the difference produces reliably differentiable internal trajectories or downstream responses under relevant conditions.

### Integration
Integration occurs when response to an input depends on that input jointly with internal state, prior inputs, or concurrent signals. Temporal comparison and memory-like dependence are special cases.

### Response / action
A response is a state transition causally downstream of reception. Biological action here is not identical to TRT primitive A. Biological actions are actualized transitions within the domain described by TRT.

### Tx (transmission)
A Tx event occurs when a system produces or modifies a physically propagating state that can alter another system. This minimal definition does not require intentional communication.

### Signal
A signal is a transmitted state whose variation participates in a receiver's discriminatory response. Further functional or semantic requirements must be stated separately.

### Communication
Communication is reserved for Tx/Rx relations in which a signal functions in coordinating, informing, or modifying another biological agent. Intentional communication is a stronger subclass and must not be inferred merely from functional signaling.

### Representation
Representation is a stronger, defeasible attribution. Covariance plus response is insufficient. A representation claim should identify what is represented, for which system, by what mechanism, and what evidence distinguishes representation from direct causal coupling.

### Semantic information
Shannon information measures statistical dependence and is insufficient for meaning. A useful comparator is Kolchinsky and Wolpert's operational proposal: environmental information is semantic for a system to the extent that the correlation is causally necessary for maintaining system viability. This provides a formal bridge between correlation and system-relative significance, but is not adopted as TRT's definition of information and is not assumed to exhaust semantics.

### Informational agency
Informational agency is a graded capacity to use received distinctions in internally conditioned action and, at richer levels, to generate signals, representations, specifications, or artifacts. Agency claims must specify the level asserted.

### Specification
Specification is an informational constraint or determinate content that can guide or delimit an actualization. Intentional specification is specification generated by an agent toward a represented outcome.

## 3. Revised hierarchy

1. **Information-bearing state**: distinctions are recoverable; no system-use claim.
2. **Rx-sensitive system**: environmental differences couple selectively to internal differences.
3. **Rx-responsive system**: received differences contribute causally to state-dependent action.
4. **Integrative biological Rx**: response depends on present input plus internal state, prior input, or multiple signals.
5. **Tx/Rx biological signaling**: the system also generates states that alter receivers.
6. **Representational agency**: evidence warrants internal representation rather than direct coupling alone.
7. **Generative informational agency**: the agent constructs novel signals/specifications from represented content.
8. **Rational-symbolic agency**: open-ended abstraction, propositional truth assessment, recursive symbolic composition, normative inference, intentional specification, and durable externalization.

This is a research hierarchy, not an evolutionary genealogy and not a claim that every level is cleanly separated in nature.

## 4. First severe tests

### 4.1 Bacteria
E. coli and related bacterial chemotaxis systems detect environmental ligand changes, transduce receptor occupancy through intracellular signaling, adapt sensitivity, compare conditions over time, and alter motility. Experiments show high sensitivity and adaptation across large concentration ranges. Fold-change detection demonstrates input-history-dependent sensory rescaling.

**Disposition:** supports Rx, discrimination, integration, and state-dependent response at the single-cell level. It does not establish representation or semantic understanding.

Primary anchors:
- Macnab & Koshland (1972), PNAS, DOI 10.1073/pnas.69.9.2509.
- Jasuja et al. (1999), PNAS, DOI 10.1073/pnas.96.20.11346.
- Lazova et al. (2011), PNAS, response rescaling/fold-change detection in E. coli chemotaxis.

### 4.2 Plants
Plant growth and development integrate light, temperature, water, hormones, peptides, ions, mechanical stresses, and other cues across intracellular, intercellular, and organismal scales.

**Disposition:** nervous systems cannot be a prerequisite for the proposed life-signature. Claims of plant cognition or representation remain separate and require stronger evidence.

Anchor:
- Duran-Nebreda & Bassel (2019), Philosophical Transactions B, DOI 10.1098/rstb.2018.0370.

### 4.3 Dormancy
Dormancy appeared capable of defeating an active Rx criterion. However, work on Bacillus subtilis spores reports integration of transient nutrient signals over time through pre-existing electrochemical potential, altering subsequent germination probability while spores remain dormant.

**Disposition:** dormancy does not entail absence of Rx/integration. More extreme dormant states still require analysis. The signature should be a capacity over the relevant life-state space, not continuous overt activity at every instant.

Anchor:
- Kikuchi et al., "Electrochemical potential enables dormant spores to integrate environmental signals", PMCID PMC10593254.

### 4.4 Viruses
Virus status remains contested and definition-sensitive. Virions can be inert outside hosts, while virus-directed processes inside infected cells reorganize host machinery and propagate viral information.

**Disposition:** do not use viruses either to define or refute the Tx/Rx signature. Ask whether the relevant informational system is the virion, infected-cell/virus system, or lineage-level replicator. This is partly a system-boundary problem.

Anchors:
- Forterre (2010), "Defining Life: The Virus Viewpoint", PMCID PMC2837877.
- Koonin & Starokadomskyy (2016), "Are viruses alive? The replicator paradigm...", PMCID PMC5406846.

### 4.5 Nonliving and synthetic systems
Artificial non-living cellular mimics can be engineered to receive chemical conditions and transmit translated chemical signals to living E. coli. Synthetic protocells can exhibit environmentally responsive behavior.

**Disposition:** Rx -> discriminate -> act is insufficient as a definition or discriminator of life. A stronger candidate must test whether received information is endogenous to system maintenance, persistence, reproduction, or autonomous organization rather than merely an externally engineered transfer function.

Anchors:
- Lentini et al. (2014), Nature Communications 5:4012, DOI 10.1038/ncomms5012.
- Kolchinsky & Wolpert (2018), Interface Focus 8:20180041, DOI 10.1098/rsfs.2018.0041.

## 5. Candidate refinement: endogenous informational relevance

Replace the weak candidate:

    Life signature = Rx -> discriminate -> act

with the stronger research conjecture:

> **Living systems characteristically maintain organized state through endogenous use of environmentally and internally discriminated differences, integrating those differences into state-dependent action across their life cycle.**

This remains a conjecture. "Endogenous use," "organized state," and "maintain" must be operationalized so that the formulation does not define the conclusion into existence.

A possible empirical bridge is viability-conditioned semantic information: scramble a system-environment correlation and ask whether system persistence or viability measurably degrades. Kolchinsky-Wolpert provides an existing formalism. TRT should use it as a comparator, not identify its viability semantics with I_infinity or semantic content generally.

## 6. Animal-to-human discontinuity: first adversarial findings

### Compositional signaling is not human-exclusive
Japanese great tits experimentally combine meaningful call units such that receivers respond to the compound and are sensitive to ordering.

**Disposition:** "compositionality" alone cannot mark the human boundary.

Anchor:
- Suzuki, Wheatcroft & Griesser (2016), Nature Communications 7:10986, DOI 10.1038/ncomms10986.

### Uncertainty monitoring/metacognition cannot simply be declared human-exclusive
A bottlenose dolphin used an uncertainty response selectively near a perceptual threshold in a pattern similar to humans. The metacognitive interpretation remains contested because associative alternatives can mimic some uncertainty behavior.

**Disposition:** retain metacognition as graded/contested, not a categorical human discriminator.

Anchors:
- Smith et al. (1995), Journal of Experimental Psychology: General 124:391-408, DOI 10.1037/0096-3445.124.4.391.
- Smith (2008), "The comparative study of metacognition: sharper paradigms, safer inferences", DOI 10.3758/PBR.15.4.679.

### Human distinctiveness should be tested conjunctively
Several single-trait boundaries fail. A stronger hypothesis is that human rational-symbolic agency is distinguished by the conjunction and open-ended integration of capacities: generative symbolic composition, unrestricted abstraction, recursive representation, explicit propositional truth/falsity, normative inference, counterfactual modeling, intentional specification, cross-domain transfer, cumulative symbolic culture, and durable external information systems.

This is testable component by component while allowing continuities in individual capacities.

## 7. TRT interface

No new TRT primitive is required.

- **L3** constrains determinate biological states and state claims.
- **I_infinity** supplies representable informational differentiation at the foundational level; it is not identical to biological signals, Shannon bits, DNA, or semantics.
- **A** supplies fundamental transition/change; biological sensing, signaling, cognition, and action are structured actual processes, not definitions of A.
- **chi** includes the determinate actual biological states in which Tx/Rx relations obtain.

Model layering:

    TRT foundational ontology
        -> actualized biological organization
        -> typed Tx/Rx processes
        -> higher-order informational agency

The arrows denote dependence/model layering, not derivation presently proved by TRT.

## 8. IIA / Logos interface

Empirical hierarchy:

    world state -> biological Rx -> discrimination/integration -> action/Tx

Human rational agency adds:

    world -> Rx -> representation -> abstraction/reason
          -> specification -> Tx/artifact -> Rx

The theological interpretation may then ask whether the fit among an intelligible world, recoverable information, rational receivers, and generative transmitters is better explained under a Logos-grounded ontology than under competitors. The biological hierarchy itself is evidence to be explained; it must not be labeled divine transmission in the empirical premise.

## 9. Current dispositions

**SURVIVES:** Tx/Rx-style informational responsiveness is a pervasive characteristic of cellular life and extends through plants and animals.

**REFINED:** continuous overt activity is unnecessary; dormant systems may retain latent or low-energy information integration.

**REJECTED AS TOO WEAK:** simple Rx/discrimination/response as a definition or sufficient discriminator of life.

**OPEN:** whether endogenous viability-relevant information use supplies a robust discriminator of life.

**REJECTED AS HUMAN-EXCLUSIVE:** compositional signaling stated generically.

**DOWNGRADED:** metacognition as a categorical human-only property.

**OPEN AND PROMISING:** human distinctiveness as an architecture-level conjunction of open-ended rational-symbolic, normative, generative, and artifact-externalizing capacities.

## 10. Next research tranche

1. Build a comparative matrix: bacteria, archaea, protists, fungi, plants, simple animals, insects, corvids/parrots, cetaceans, apes, humans.
2. Test endogenous viability-relevance against fire, crystals, thermostats, hurricanes, engineered control systems, AI agents, and synthetic protocells.
3. Decompose the proposed human architecture into independently falsifiable criteria.
4. Search primary literature for the strongest nonhuman result against each criterion.
5. Determine whether a formally defined **Informational Agency Gradient** is warranted.
6. Only after these tests decide whether this becomes a TRT protective-belt paper or a separate biology-facing research programme.
