# Tx/Rx Comparative Matrix and Nonliving Counterexample Test

**WP:** WP-TRT-TXRX-0001
**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Status:** Research note, tranche 2
**Purpose:** Stress-test the Informational Agency Gradient (IAG) across living taxa and against nonliving/synthetic systems.

## 1. Working Informational Agency Gradient

The evidence supports retaining a graded model, but the levels should be treated as capability classes rather than a phylogenetic ladder.

| Grade | Capability class | Minimal warrant |
|---|---|---|
| IAG-0 | Information-bearing | Distinctions are recoverable by an external discriminator; no endogenous use inferred. |
| IAG-1 | Rx-sensitive | External/internal differences selectively couple to system state. |
| IAG-2 | Rx-responsive | Received differences alter state-dependent action. |
| IAG-3 | Integrative Rx | Present input is integrated with internal state, prior input, multiple signals, or memory-like state. |
| IAG-4 | Biological Tx/Rx signaling | System-generated signals alter other organisms; functional communication may be present. |
| IAG-5 | Representational/anticipatory agency | Evidence warrants internal mapping, prospective planning, relational concepts, or other behavior not well captured as immediate coupling alone. |
| IAG-6 | Generative symbolic agency | Learned/recombinable signals can refer beyond immediate stimulus conditions; novel informational outputs are generated from internalized relations. |
| IAG-7 | Rational-symbolic agency | Open-ended symbolic composition integrated with abstraction, explicit propositional truth/falsity, normative inference, recursive representation, intentional specification, cross-domain transfer, cumulative symbolic culture, and durable external information systems. |

A system may display capacities associated with multiple grades. The grade is descriptive and defeasible.

## 2. Comparative organism matrix

### Bacteria: strong IAG-3, collective IAG-4
Chemotaxis demonstrates reception, discrimination, adaptation, temporal comparison, and state-dependent motility. Quorum and other signaling systems extend this into cell-to-cell Tx/Rx. These systems establish that sophisticated integration does not require neurons.

**Disposition:** IAG-3 secure; IAG-4 secure for signaling populations. Representation remains unneeded for the basic explanation.

### Archaea: IAG-3/4 expected and biologically supported
Archaea possess sensory and chemotactic systems and extensive cell signaling. They reinforce the point that informational responsiveness is fundamental across cellular life rather than peculiar to bacteria/eukaryotes.

**Disposition:** include in full evidence matrix; no higher cognition claim.

### Protists / slime molds: IAG-3 with pressure toward IAG-5
Physarum polycephalum can compare options, integrate reward frequency and magnitude across repeated sampling, and solve exploration/exploitation tasks without a nervous system. Recent mechanistic work continues to characterize active decision processes.

**Disposition:** "decision-making" cannot be reserved for nervous systems. Whether this warrants representation depends on the criterion used; keep IAG-5 attribution cautious.

Primary anchor:
- Reid et al. (2016), decision-making in Physarum, PMCID PMC4938078.

### Fungi: IAG-3/4
Fungal systems sense nutrients, stress, hosts, competitors, and partners and coordinate multicellular/mycelial responses through chemical and electrical processes. Stronger claims that fungal electrical activity constitutes language should not be imported without separate evidence.

**Disposition:** Rx/integration/signaling secure; symbolic-language claims excluded pending severe test.

### Plants: strong IAG-3/4
Plants integrate environmental and developmental signals through interacting intracellular and intercellular networks, coordinating local and long-distance responses. Field work also demonstrates integration of circadian internal state with temperature/light cues.

**Disposition:** IAG-3/4 secure. "Plant cognition" is unnecessary to the Tx/Rx thesis.

Anchors:
- Information Integration and Communication in Plant Growth Regulation, Cell (2016), DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2016.01.044.
- Extensive signal integration by the phytohormone protein network, Nature 583 (2020).
- Circadian and environmental signal integration in natural Arabidopsis, primary field study.

### Simple animals: IAG-3/4
Even animals with compact or distributed nervous systems coordinate multimodal sensory input, internal state, movement, and signaling. The animal transition increases behavioral integration without creating Tx/Rx ex nihilo.

**Disposition:** supports continuity in informational agency.

### Social insects: IAG-4 with significant IAG-5/6 pressure
Honeybee waggle dance encodes direction and distance to resources. Recruits can use communicated vectors in ways consistent with map-location inference. Correct dance performance is socially learned. 2026 PNAS work further shows dance information content is audience-sensitive rather than a rigid one-way broadcast.

**Disposition:** generic "symbolic reference," displacement, social learning, or sender-receiver feedback cannot serve as uniquely human criteria.

Anchors:
- Su et al. (2008), PLOS ONE, interspecies dance-language decoding.
- Dong et al. (2023), Science, social signal learning of waggle dance.
- Honey bees infer source location from dances, PMCID PMC10041085.
- Lin et al. (2026), PNAS 123, audience-dependent information content.

### Corvids: strong IAG-5
New Caledonian crows have experimentally selected tools for specific future tasks and solved metatool problems requiring representations of hidden goals/subgoals. Ravens have shown flexible planning for tool use and bartering with long delays.

**Disposition:** planning, future-directed tool selection, and mental representation stated generically cannot mark the human boundary.

Anchors:
- New Caledonian crows plan for specific future tool use, PMCID PMC7735258.
- New Caledonian Crows Use Mental Representations to Solve Metatool Problems, PMCID PMC6384166.
- Kabadayi & Osvath (2017), Science, PMID 28706072.

### Parrots: IAG-5/6 pressure
African Grey parrot work reports abstract same/different relations, categorical concepts, number-related capacities, and reporting absence. These results challenge any human boundary stated merely as abstraction, relational concepts, labels, or absence concepts.

**Disposition:** "abstraction" alone is too broad for human uniqueness.

Anchors:
- Pepperberg (1988), comprehension of absence, PMID 16812572.
- Pepperberg (2013), Abstract concepts: data from a Grey parrot, PMID 23089384.

### Cetaceans: IAG-5/6 pressure
Bottlenose dolphins develop learned individually distinctive signature whistles. Playback experiments show identity information survives removal of voice features, and wild dolphins respond to copies of their own learned signature whistle in a manner consistent with individual addressing.

**Disposition:** learned arbitrary-like labels and individual reference cannot by themselves define the human boundary.

Anchors:
- Janik et al. (2006), PNAS, PMID 16698937.
- King & Janik (2013), learned vocal labels used to address conspecifics, PMCID PMC3740840.

### Apes: strong IAG-5, partial IAG-6 under trained conditions
Great apes exhibit planning, tool use, social cognition, learned symbol use in experimental settings, and rich gesture/vocal communication. Current comparative work on compositionality must be treated carefully because broad claims of "syntax" vary with definitions and evidence.

**Disposition:** individual components of symbolic/intentional agency overlap substantially with humans. Do not make "tool use," "planning," "intentional communication," or trained symbol association categorical boundaries.

### Cephalopods: IAG-5 candidate
Octopuses exhibit flexible learning, problem solving, exploration, and distributed sensorimotor control. Claims of language, human-like metacognition, or symbolic culture exceed current warrant.

**Disposition:** useful test case because advanced behavioral flexibility arose in a radically different nervous-system architecture.

### Humans: IAG-7 candidate
Every lower-level capability is shared somewhere in nonhuman life. The surviving human hypothesis is architectural and conjunctive:

1. open-ended generative symbolic composition;
2. unrestricted cross-domain abstraction;
3. explicit propositional assertion and truth/falsity evaluation;
4. explicit normative inference, including reasons about reasons;
5. recursively embedded representation with no demonstrated small fixed ceiling;
6. intentional specification of counterfactual artifacts before construction;
7. cumulative, ratcheting symbolic culture across generations;
8. durable external information systems such as writing, mathematics, diagrams, scores, software, archives, and formal proofs;
9. recovery and extension of abstract content generated by minds remote in space and time.

**Disposition:** IAG-7 is a candidate architecture, not yet a proven categorical boundary. Each component and the conjunction require explicit severe tests.

## 3. Nonliving counterexample attack

### Thermostats and engineered controllers
A thermostat clearly satisfies weak Rx/discriminate/respond language. Advanced controllers integrate multiple sensors, memory, goals, and feedback. Therefore IAG-1 through portions of IAG-3 are substrate-neutral functional descriptions.

**Result:** these grades cannot define life.

### AI and autonomous agents
Artificial agents can receive inputs, maintain internal state, select actions, generate messages, use symbols, and produce novel artifacts. Their objective functions, architecture, training corpus, energy provisioning, repair, and persistence are externally supplied in ordinary systems.

**Result:** behavior alone is insufficient to identify biological life or intrinsic semantic agency. The model needs explicit system-boundary and provenance variables.

### Synthetic protocells / artificial cells
Engineered nonliving cellular mimics can receive chemical messages and translate them into signals readable by living cells. Such systems directly instantiate Tx/Rx without being straightforward examples of autonomous life.

**Result:** Tx/Rx is a cross-cutting architecture, not a life discriminator.

### Fire, hurricanes, crystals, convection and dissipative structures
These systems can maintain patterned nonequilibrium states, respond causally to environmental changes, propagate effects, and in some cases exhibit feedback. Self-organization and persistence therefore cannot alone establish informational agency.

**Result:** "maintains organized state" is also too weak unless the causal role of discriminated information is specified.

### Viability-semantic information
Kolchinsky and Wolpert define semantic information as system-environment correlations causally necessary for a system to maintain its own existence under a viability function. Crucially, their framework is explicitly applicable to living **or nonliving** physical systems.

**Result:** viability-conditioned semantic information improves the distinction between mere correlation and system-relevant information, but it cannot by itself define life. This is an important correction to tranche 1.

Primary anchor:
- Kolchinsky & Wolpert (2018), Interface Focus 8:20180041, DOI 10.1098/rsfs.2018.0041, PMID 30443338.

## 4. Revised life-signature conjecture

The phrase "signature of life" should now mean a characteristic architecture whose conjunction is strongly associated with living organization, not a necessary-and-sufficient definition.

Candidate:

> **Living systems characteristically form bounded, historically continuous organizations in which internally generated and environmentally received distinctions are selectively integrated into actions that contribute to the persistence, repair, reproduction, regulation, or development of the system and/or its lineage.**

This adds four dimensions absent from simple Tx/Rx:

- **boundary:** identify the system whose information use is being assessed;
- **historical continuity:** current organization is linked to prior system organization rather than freshly imposed each cycle by an external designer/operator;
- **endogenous integration:** the mapping from distinctions to action is substantially instantiated within the system;
- **organizational consequence:** information use participates in persistence, repair, reproduction, regulation, or development.

These remain candidate discriminators. Artificial-life systems may eventually satisfy all four. If so, the correct conclusion may be that they instantiate relevant life-like organization, rather than that the criteria must be protected ad hoc.

## 5. Important conceptual result: Tx/Rx is substrate-neutral

The strongest result of tranche 2 is that Tx/Rx should not be treated as a biological primitive.

Tx/Rx is a **relational informational architecture** that can be instantiated in:
- cells,
- organisms,
- colonies,
- nervous systems,
- human communities,
- engineered communication systems,
- computers and AI,
- synthetic chemical systems.

Life instantiates Tx/Rx in a distinctive self-organizing, historically continuous, endogenously regulated architecture. Rational human agency instantiates it again at a much richer semantic and generative level.

This yields a cleaner ontology:

    information-bearing actuality
          |
          v
    Tx/Rx-capable causal systems
          |
          +-- engineered / nonliving instantiations
          |
          +-- living informational organization
                    |
                    +-- minimal cellular integration
                    +-- organismal signaling
                    +-- representational/anticipatory agency
                    +-- generative symbolic agency
                    +-- rational-symbolic agency

The branches are classifications, not asserted evolutionary derivations.

## 6. Human discontinuity after adversarial comparison

The following proposed human-exclusive properties are now rejected when stated alone:

- sensing and state-dependent response;
- learning;
- communication;
- symbolic/referential signaling in broad usage;
- displacement;
- social learning;
- planning;
- tool use;
- abstraction in broad usage;
- same/different concepts;
- individual learned labels;
- compositionality in broad usage;
- uncertainty monitoring/metacognitive-like behavior.

The surviving research target is:

> **Does any nonhuman system demonstrate the open-ended, recursively extensible integration of symbolic reference, explicit propositional truth evaluation, normative inference, counterfactual specification, cross-domain abstraction, and cumulative durable externalization that characterizes ordinary human rational agency?**

This is considerably narrower and more defensible than "animals cannot reason" or "animals cannot communicate symbolically."

## 7. Relationship to the theological model

Nothing in this comparative matrix establishes the Logos hypothesis by itself. It does, however, sharpen the explanandum.

The created order exhibits information-bearing structure. Living systems exhibit increasingly rich capacities to receive and use distinctions. Some animals generate and interpret signals with reference-like, learned, prospective, or compositional properties. Humans instantiate an unusually expansive Tx/Rx architecture capable of recovering abstract structure from the world, reasoning about it normatively, specifying states that do not yet obtain, actualizing some of those specifications, and transmitting the resulting content across minds and generations.

The theological proposal can therefore be stated downstream:

    archetypal rational source / Logos
             ->
    intelligible, information-bearing creation
             ->
    living Rx/Tx systems
             ->
    image-bearing rational Tx/Rx agents

The empirical work establishes the middle structure. The identification of its ultimate ground remains a metaphysical/theological inference and must carry its own burden.

## 8. Dispositions

**SUPPORTED:** Informational agency is meaningfully graded.

**SUPPORTED:** Tx/Rx is pervasive across living organization.

**CORRECTED:** Tx/Rx is substrate-neutral and therefore cannot itself define life.

**CORRECTED:** viability-relevant semantic information can in principle apply to nonliving systems.

**SUPPORTED:** system boundary, endogenous integration, historical continuity, and organizational consequence are promising additional axes for characterizing living informational organization.

**SUPPORTED:** many capacities historically treated as uniquely human occur individually in nonhuman animals.

**OPEN:** whether IAG-7 describes a genuine architecture-level discontinuity.

**OPEN:** whether "Informational Agency Gradient" should become canonical TRT terminology.

## 9. Next severe tests

1. Formalize IAG as a multidimensional vector rather than a scalar ladder.
2. Separate autonomy, semantics, representation, generativity, normativity, recursion, and externalization as independent axes.
3. Score strongest biological and artificial counterexamples without assuming biological privilege.
4. Define the minimum evidence required to move a system upward on each axis.
5. Test human IAG-7 against the strongest ape, cetacean, corvid, parrot, and AI cases.
6. Determine whether the empirical findings warrant a paper provisionally titled **"Informational Agency Across Living and Artificial Systems: A Tx/Rx Framework."**
