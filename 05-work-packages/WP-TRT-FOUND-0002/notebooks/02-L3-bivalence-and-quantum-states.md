# Notebook 02: L3, bivalence, and quantum predicates

**Status:** Open.

## Hypothesis
A complete fundamental state either obtains or does not obtain, without implying every observable has a predetermined value.

## Distinctions
- Law of excluded middle: P or not-P in classical propositional logic.
- Semantic bivalence: each relevant proposition has exactly one truth value.
- Value definiteness: an observable possesses a definite value before measurement.
These are not interchangeable.

## Cases to test
Qubit prepared in z-up expressed in x basis; singlet and reduced maximally mixed states; GHZ/W multipartite states; Kochen-Specker contextuality; Bell constraints; stationary pure state.

## Obligations
Specify proposition language, state/context/time indexing, domain of 'fundamental', and whether quantum logic or intuitionistic alternatives defeat necessity.

## Failure condition
Any proof of fundamental bivalence that covertly assumes noncontextual predetermined values, or equates logical alternatives with measurement outcomes, fails.
