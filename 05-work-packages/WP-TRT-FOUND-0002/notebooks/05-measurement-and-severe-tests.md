# Notebook 05: Measurement, decoherence, and severe tests

**Status:** Open.

## Separate phenomena
Entanglement creates joint correlations; decoherence suppresses accessible interference in reduced states; definite single outcomes require an interpretation or added dynamics.

## Candidate TRT questions
Does Action select a unique realized branch? Is selection deterministic, stochastic, or interpretation-only? What is the status of other branches? How does the model recover Born frequencies and avoid signaling?

## Comparison baselines
Everett/unitary-only, Bohmian mechanics, objective collapse, operational/information-theoretic interpretations. State what each predicts and which ontological commitments differ.

## Severe-test register
T1: a new quantitative probability deviation, if derived.
T2: a specified collapse/decoherence distinction, if physical dynamics added.
T3: internal consistency test for contextuality, relativistic no-signaling, and conservation.
No empirical novelty is claimed until a numerical prediction and test protocol exist.

## Failure condition
If TRT merely renames measurement without new constraints or a worked mechanism, classify as interpretation, not solved measurement physics.
