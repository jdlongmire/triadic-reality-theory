# WP-TRT-FOUND-0002: Ontological Necessity and Dynamic Stability

**Status:** Proposed research package. **Tier:** foundational hard-core audit with protective-belt notebooks.

## Motivation
The supplied four-page *Triadic Reality Theory* manuscript claims derivations of the variational principle, Schrodinger equation, and Born rule that are not established by the displayed steps. It also defines Action as transition while recent discussion proposes that physically stable configurations remain sustained by fundamental Action. These are distinct research obligations.

## Governing constraints
Canonical TRT distinguishes representable (R), L3-coherent, actualizable (D), and actual (X/chi). L3 coherence is necessary, not proven sufficient for D. A is primitive non-temporal state transition/change; actualization is its role, not its circular definition. See WP-TRT-CORE-0001 and METHODOLOGY.md.

## Work sequence
1. Test necessity, irreducibility, and countermodels.
2. Specify bivalence domain and contextual quantum predicates.
3. Test Dynamic Stability Principle against stationary states and non-temporal Action.
4. Reconstruct mathematical claims with explicit premises and missing lemmas.
5. Specify measurement models, empirical discriminators, and adversarial dispositions.

## Research protocol
Each notebook records claim, assumptions, derivation attempt, counterexample, epistemic status, failure condition, and next experiment or proof. No source is promoted from secondary to primary without verification.
