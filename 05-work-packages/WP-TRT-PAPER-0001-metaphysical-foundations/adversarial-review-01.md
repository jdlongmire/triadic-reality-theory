## Overall decision
**Major revision required.** The thesis remains philosophically discussable, but the irreducibility of *ontic* A and the infinity of I∞ have not been established by the functional-role argument. The strongest current publication route is a transparent, conditional metaphysical proposal with countermodels and explicit underdetermination. Do not mark WP complete or manuscript publication-ready.

## Next formal tasks
1. Build a countermodel in which determinate actuality is static and no primitive transition occurs.
2. Test whether the premise 'actuality requires action' is independent, question-begging, or defensible through a further argument.
3. Separate finite observed information from unbounded *representability*; test whether the latter is necessary.
4. Give a typed formal semantics for L3-admissibility, D and X without assuming plenitude.
5. Map quantum reconstructions to required supplementary principles; identify where TRT has no derivation.
