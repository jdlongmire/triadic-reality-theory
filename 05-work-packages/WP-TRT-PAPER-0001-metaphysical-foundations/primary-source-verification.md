## Findings relevant to TRT

- **Bell:** Constraints on a locality-based hidden-variable framework do not prove that physical reality violates identity or non-contradiction. Bell's original 1964 argument is about a particular set of physical assumptions, not a direct experiment on classical logic.
- **Zurek:** Environment-induced monitoring and pointer-state robustness supply an established physics-facing research framework. TRT must explain what it adds to this mechanism.
- **Schlosshauer:** The relation between decoherence and definite measurement outcomes is contested. An interpretation cannot be silently elevated into a derivation.
- **Chiribella et al.:** A genuine reconstruction of quantum formalism from explicit operational axioms exists. TRT must identify additional axioms and prove its bridge to those principles rather than claiming that three ontological labels alone uniquely select Hilbert-space quantum mechanics.
