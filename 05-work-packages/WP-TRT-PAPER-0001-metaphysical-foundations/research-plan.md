# Research and argument plan
Status: working, 2026-10-30. Parent: WP-TRT-PAPER-0001.

## Thesis and boundaries
TRT proposes L3, I∞ and A as jointly necessary irreducible conditions of determinate actuality. The paper tests the arguments for this ontological proposal and analyzes classical and quantum theories as possible instantiations. Instantiation is not derivation. Physical action integrals are not identified with primitive A. L3-admissibility is necessary for actualizability but sufficiency is open.
