## Points of difference, recorded and not resolved

1. **Scope of Excluded Middle.** The manuscript and CM-01 treat the physical scope of Excluded Middle as a separate open burden. The 2026-11-12 ruling adopts the scoping: Excluded Middle binds over propositions over actualized states. A revised manuscript should state the adopted scope and may still report the burden of defending it (review item ADV-03).
2. **Universality of A.** The manuscript lists, as one available response to the static-world challenge, narrowing the thesis so that A is necessary only for dynamic actuality. The hard core states the necessity of all three co-primitives for any determinate actuality. The narrowing is not adopted. The contested premise (P3 in CM-01) remains open, and WP-TRT-CORE-0002 owns the A-reduction challenge on main.
3. **Becoming and the block universe.** CM-01 concludes that eternalism does not by itself exclude A and leaves TRT uncommitted on temporal becoming. The charter commits to sequential growth in which each A-event is a causal-set birth. No reconciliation is asserted here.
4. **Order and the non-temporal definition of A.** Review item ADV-05 asks for an atemporal relation from which order arises as a theorem, or an admission of primitive order. Charter obligation (e), a formal definition of "condition of actualization" sufficient to prove acyclicity, is the governing form of that task.
5. **Unboundedness of I∞.** Review item ADV-04 is not addressed by the charter and remains open.
