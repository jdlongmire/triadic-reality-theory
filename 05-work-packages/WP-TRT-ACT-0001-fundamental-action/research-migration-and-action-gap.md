## Rationale and authoritative constraints

TRT's canonical README and WP-TRT-CORE-0001 already define A as primitive fundamental state transition/change without presupposing physical time, force, energy or actuality. Actualization is a **role/consequence** of A, not A's definition. The canonical architecture distinguishes representable R, logically constrained candidates, actualizable D and actual X. Coherence is not sufficient for actualizability without proof.

The owner further clarifies: without A nothing obtains, and A necessarily entails genuine ontic non-identity change. This is an explicit owner research premise whose compatibility and explanatory adequacy must be tested. Do not replace TRT's existing definition or silently equate A with actuality.

## TRT research obligations

**TRT-ACT-1, ontic difference.** Give noncircular identity/equivalence criteria for fundamental informational aspects. Exclude mere syntactic relabeling and gauge-equivalent descriptions. An independently justified invariant could witness difference; it must not be introduced solely to make A nontrivial.

**TRT-ACT-2, change constitutes obtaining.** Distinguish A being necessary whenever X obtains from A explaining why X obtains. Do not presuppose two independently actual states, a first event, physical time, or a separately existing I∞.

**TRT-ACT-3, directionality.** Test whether non-identity implies any ordering or asymmetric relation. It does not by itself; extra premises must be identified.

**TRT-ACT-4, R/D/X typing.** Determine where the ontic difference criterion belongs in the representable → admissible → actualizable → actual sequence without defining actualizability as already actual or inferring D from L₃-coherence.

**TRT-ACT-5, severe-test bridge.** Seek at least one constrained physical consequence of the strengthened A thesis. If no possible observation distinguishes it from static-fundamental alternatives, classify its status as metaphysical rather than verified physical theory.

**TRT-ACT-6, LRT interface.** Expose explicitly conditional assumptions and results that LRT may use to investigate identity-through-change, dynamics and measurement. LRT must not infer Born probabilities or Hilbert structure merely from A-NIC.

## Audit of prior models

The CC-1..5 minimal A-C semantics allowed an actual singleton with no change. That model is excluded **conditional on the owner's stronger A-NIC premise**. A macroscopically stationary but fundamentally changing model may remain compatible; the proposed hidden change must survive a physical equivalence test. Finite models with internal nonidentity demonstrate relative logical satisfiability, not the actualization mechanism. p-versus-q selection models are conditional critiques, not TRT's definition of Action.

## Success, failure and epistemic discipline

- Success: an independently justified ontic equivalence criterion and a noncircular account of A's constitutive contribution, with explicit dependencies and at least one discriminatory physical constraint.
- Negative research result: demonstrate that candidate criteria collapse into circularity, gauge artifacts, or an untestable label; record the failure without patching in an unsupported axiom.
- HIGH, conditional: A-NIC excludes actual structures devoid of fundamental non-identity change by stipulation.
- UNCERTAIN: adequacy of ontic difference and constitutive grounding.
- NOT ESTABLISHED: nontrivial dynamics, initial-state account, physical time, measurement solution, Born rule, novel prediction.
