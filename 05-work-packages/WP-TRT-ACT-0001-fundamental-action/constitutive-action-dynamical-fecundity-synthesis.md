## Fixed starting commitments and their limits

TRT's co-constitutive triad is χ = [L₃ : I∞ : A]. L₃ is a necessary constraint on actualizability, not an established sufficient filter. R (representable), D (actualizable), and X (actual) are distinct. A is primitive fundamental state transition/change, defined without primitive physical time, force, motion, energy, or actuality. Actualization is a role/consequence of A, not its defining synonym. The owner's **A-NIC** research commitment states that A entails genuine fundamental ontic non-identity change. This is an additional explicit premise, not a theorem from L₃ and I∞.

Do not say that absolute ontic inertness is allowed under the *strengthened* A-NIC position. A structure that obtains while admitting no fundamental ontic non-identity change contradicts A-NIC together with constitutive necessity of A. A macroscopically stationary configuration, or a structure with no *further* transition, need not be absolutely inert. Equivalence under gauge and relabeling must be checked before counting differences as ontic.

## Three predicates, three proof obligations

1. **Constitutive change:** `FundamentalChange(x)` expresses the primitive non-identity requirement. Its noncircular truth conditions and ontic equivalence criterion are open.
2. **Licensed capacity:** `LicensedTransition(F,s,t;E)` expresses a possible further transition in an explicitly admissible context E. Licensing is not occurrence; the state-space typing, ontic distinction, and admissibility of E must be specified.
3. **Occurrence:** `Occurs(F,s,t;E)` asserts that a licensed transition obtains. No inference from capacity to occurrence is licensed without an independent principle.

These are schematic research predicates, not formalized TRT definitions. The arrows from constitutive change to further capacity and from capacity to occurrence are **open dependency questions**, not established entailments.

## DF and DF-R: optional, strictly downstream hypotheses

The original DF formula quantified over arbitrary `s` without a state-space membership condition. Its DF-R formula allowed a change entirely in environment E, with no guaranteed participation by F. Neither displayed formula proves DF implies DF-R: a composite-context embedding principle would be required.

A possible *typed research schema*, deliberately not adopted as an axiom:

- DF*: for each actual F and each eligible s in S(F), there exists t in S(F) such that s and t are ontically distinct and a **further** transition s→t is A-licensed.
- DF-R*: for each actual F there exists an admissible composite context (F,E) and a licensed transition between ontically distinct composite configurations **in which F participates nontrivially**. Define participation without merely relabeling E.
- To claim DF* ⇒ DF-R*, independently justify that every relevant intrinsic transition embeds in at least one admissible context (possibly a neutral context). To claim strictness, provide a model satisfying DF-R* and refuting DF*.

These concern additional transition capacity beyond the change required for constitution. Neither entails occurrence. The supplied C0–C4 table is not accepted as verified: notably, C1's failure of a capacity-only DF conflicts with its label “capacity, no occurrence” unless further model assumptions are supplied. Reconstruct each model's domain, valuation, eligibility, and licensing before assigning truth values.

## Time and downstream physics

Fundamental A is not parameterized by physical time. Any derived succession requires an independently argued asymmetric/order relation; non-identity alone does not entail direction. Page–Wootters is a **comparison target** for relational time, conditional on composite Hilbert structure, an internal clock, correlations, and global constraint. It is not derived from the triad. Likewise, Wheeler–DeWitt-style constraints remain exploratory comparisons.

The linear-versus-quadratic actualization-resolution question is a protective-belt mathematical keystone, not yet demonstrated to be constitutively equivalent to A. Complex Hilbert structure, local tomography, Born measure, unitary evolution, measurement, gravity, cosmology, and novel empirical predictions are NOT ESTABLISHED as pure consequences of the triad.
