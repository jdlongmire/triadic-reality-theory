## Programme charter

Filed 2026-11-09, the charter and objective are the programme's governing documents. Their stated status is the charter's own: programme objective; not yet demonstrated. Beyond obligation (a), L3-sufficiency for D, which stands behind the hard boundary below, the charter records four further obligations:

- **Selection rule (b).** A rule fixing the actualized algebra. The gravitational order-difference refinement has been falsified. The working channel is redundancy selection: a distinction is a candidate for actualization if it is recorded in at least two disjoint, causally independent regions of D.
- **Actualization model (c).** A model whose events constitute spacetime: sequential growth in which each A-event is a causal-set birth.
- **Tsirelson bound (d).** Derive the Tsirelson bound from A or I-infinity.
- **Persistence of Zero (d-prime).** Derive Persistence of Zero from L3 and A.
- **Condition of actualization (e).** Formally define "condition of actualization," sufficient to prove acyclicity and to serve as the premise of (d-prime).

**Discreteness commitment.** Actuality is constituted by individuated A-events forming a partial order that is irreflexive, acyclic, and locally finite. Time and geometry are recovered from order and number.

**Failure conditions.** TRT in its present form fails if the Co-Admissibility Conjecture is falsified (named target: Feller et al. 2020); if the channel that actualizes matter distinctions does not leave electron and molecular interference intact while actualizing macroscopic records, the test the gravitational channel already failed; or if the Tsirelson bound is shown to be unobtainable from any constraint on A or I-infinity.
