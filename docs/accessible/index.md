# Triadic Reality Theory explained: three requirements for determinate reality

Start with a concrete state: a coin lying heads-up.

This introduction presents the same Logic-Information-Action ontology used in the technical TRT corpus, using ordinary examples before formal notation.

## Logic constrains

The coin is the coin. It cannot be both heads-up and not-heads-up at the same time and in the same respect. TRT calls the relevant constraint $L_3$: Identity, Non-Contradiction, and Excluded Middle.

## Information differentiates

“Heads” differs from “tails.” A reality with no distinctions contains no state specifiable as this rather than that. TRT uses $I_\infty$ for the unbounded domain of representable distinctions and relations.

“Square circle” can be represented as words. Representation alone does not make its object coherent, actualizable, or actual.

## Action transitions

Descriptions of two states do not themselves supply a transition between them. TRT uses $A$ for fundamental state transition/change. This primitive does not assume physical time.

## Putting the three together

$$\boxed{\chi \equiv \mathsf{A}(I_{\infty}\mid L_3)}$$

This compact identity states TRT's ontology. The actualizability question is typed separately:

$$R \xrightarrow{\;L_3\;\mathrm{necessary}\;} D \xrightarrow{\;A\;} X\,(\chi)$$

A representable state can fail the logical filter. A logically coherent state may still fail whatever complete conditions actualizability requires. An actualizable state need not become actual.

## Why irreducible?

<div class="arg"><strong>Logic cannot supply content or transition.</strong> Constraint does not create the distinctions it constrains or perform a transition.</div>
<div class="arg"><strong>Information cannot supply its own admissibility or transition.</strong> A specification can encode contradiction, and a specification does not perform itself.</div>
<div class="arg"><strong>Action cannot supply its own relata or constraints.</strong> Transition requires distinguishable states and admissibility conditions.</div>

## What TRT does not claim

TRT does not currently claim a completed physical theory, a derivation of the Born rule, a derivation of general relativity, or a proof that $L_3$ exhausts metaphysical actualizability.
