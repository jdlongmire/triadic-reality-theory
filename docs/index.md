# What must be present for a determinate reality to obtain?

TRT proposes three **irreducible co-primitives**: logical constraint, informational differentiation, and action.

<div class="math-hero">

$$\boxed{\chi \equiv \mathsf{A}(I_{\infty}\mid L_3)}$$

</div>

<div class="grid"><div class="card"><h3>L₃ · Logic</h3><p>Identity, Non-Contradiction, and Excluded Middle function as ontological constraints on determinate actuality.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>I∞ · Information</h3><p>The unbounded domain of representable distinctions, relations, states, and configurations. Representation does not entail actuality.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>A · Action</h3><p>Fundamental state transition or change, defined without presupposing physical time, motion, force, or energy.</p></div></div>

The stronger modal question is separate from the compact identity:

<div class="math-hero">

$$R \xrightarrow{\;L_3\;\mathrm{necessary\ filter}\;} D \xrightarrow{\;A\;} X\,(\chi)$$

</div>

Here $R$ is representable content, $D$ is the actualizable domain, and $X/\chi$ is what obtains. $L_3$-admissibility is necessary for $D$; sufficiency remains open.
