# Terminology concordance

| Accessible wording | Canonical technical term | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| what can be described distinctly | representable domain $R$ / $I_\infty$ | representation does not entail coherence or actuality |
| what cannot violate basic logic | $L_3$-admissible / coherent under the necessary logical filter | necessary for $D$, sufficiency open |
| what could actually be realized | actualizable domain $D$ | stronger than bare representation |
| fundamental change | primitive action $A$ | no physical time presupposed |
| what actually obtains | $X/\chi$ | actuality |
| the three together | $\chi\equiv\mathsf{A}(I_\infty\mid L_3)$ | compact ontology, not a proof that $D=I_\infty\mid L_3$ |

The accessible path may simplify vocabulary. It may not change these semantic boundaries.
