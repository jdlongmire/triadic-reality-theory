# Gödel, general relativity, and the gap between possibility and actuality

Gödel's 1949 rotating cosmological model is an exact solution of Einstein's field equations and permits closed timelike curves. For TRT, its main value is methodological: it shows why several different senses of "possible" must not be collapsed.

Let $g_G$ denote the Gödel spacetime.

## Five different questions

| Question | TRT treatment |
|---|---|
| Can $g_G$ be specified? | $g_G\in R$ |
| Is it logically admissible? | $\mathrm{Adm}_{L_3}(g_G)$ |
| Does it satisfy GR under its stipulated model conditions? | $\mathrm{Adm}_{GR}(g_G)$ |
| Is it genuinely actualizable? | $g_G\in D$ remains a separate question |
| Does it obtain? | $g_G\in X$ is not entailed by solutionhood |

The central inference constraint is:

$$\boxed{\mathrm{Adm}_{T}(x)\nRightarrow\mathrm{Obtains}(x)}$$

A theory can admit a state in its solution space without thereby asserting that the state is instantiated.

## Why TRT does not add a new ontological tier

It is tempting to write a ladder from representable, to coherent, to law-admissible, to actualizable, to actual. That is useful pedagogically, but the arrows can smuggle in an unwarranted implication.

TRT instead keeps its canonical architecture:

$$R\xrightarrow{\;L_3\;\mathrm{necessary\ filter}\;}D\xrightarrow{\;A\;}X(\chi)$$

and treats physical-theory solutionhood as an additional predicate:

$$x\in R,\qquad \mathrm{Adm}_{L_3}(x),\qquad \mathrm{Adm}_{T}(x).$$

For the Gödel solution, GR-admissibility does not by itself establish membership in $D$, and neither establishes actuality.

## Connection to the compact ontology

TRT's compact identity remains:

$$\boxed{\chi\equiv\mathsf{A}(I_\infty\mid L_3)}$$

Gödel's case does not prove primitive $A$. It clarifies the burden that $A$ addresses. Formal specification and constraint satisfaction do not, without an additional bridge principle, entail obtainment.

That distinction can be stated compactly:

**Constraint does not entail instantiation.**

## What Gödel established

Gödel established an exact relativistic cosmological solution with the relevant unusual causal structure. The result concerns what the Einstein equations permit under the model's conditions. It is not empirical evidence that our universe is a Gödel universe, nor by itself evidence that travel into one's past is physically realizable in our universe.
