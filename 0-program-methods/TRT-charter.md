# Triadic Reality Theory: Programme Charter

*Working notes, derivations, antecedents, and references: see TRT-working-notes.md.*

## Objective

Triadic Reality Theory aims to establish that the co-primitives of the created order ⟨L₃, I∞, A⟩ are each necessary, and jointly sufficient up to contingent specification, for determinate actuality, and that the constitutive structures of physics (time, geometry, dynamics, mathematical structure) are derivable from or tightly constrained by them.

## Definitions

- **I∞**: unbounded informational representability; the representable domain R of distinctions, relations, states, and configurations.
- **L₃**: ontological logical constraint: Identity, Non-Contradiction, and Excluded Middle (the last scoped to propositions over actualized states).
- **A**: fundamental state change; the primitive transition from admissible to actual, presupposing no physical time, motion, force, energy, or prior actuality. Physical dynamics is ordered A. Unitary evolution is internal structure of D, not an instance of A.

## Architecture

R --L₃ (necessary filter)--> D --A--> X,  with χ ≡ A(I∞ | L₃)

Three tiers: representable (R), admissible (D), actual (X).

## Commitments

**Source.** The co-primitives are irreducible to one another and to anything in the order they constitute, but not self-existent. They have a common source (the Great Selector), which is the ground of the system and not a constituent of it, and so not a fourth principle. The source grounds each by a distinct mode: L₃ by the divine nature (necessary, not selected); I∞ by the divine intellect (necessary); the specification of D and each actualization by the will (contingent: creation and providence). Selection occurs only in the will-relation: once as the initial specification of D, and continuously as grounded actualization within D. The source's act is not an instance of A and is related to it analogically. Secular presentations state the triad as primitive and the specification as boundary data.

**Discreteness.** The actual is constituted by individuated A-events forming a partial order that is irreflexive, acyclic, and locally finite. Individuation follows from L₃ Identity; acyclicity from Non-Contradiction together with A's exclusion of prior actuality; local finiteness is an intrinsic property of A. Time and geometry are recovered from order and number (causal-set reconstruction). Time between A-events is relational.

**Logical realism.** Reality is not locally real but is logically real: every actualized proposition is determinate and jointly consistent with every other across the whole of X, irrespective of spatial separation. Correlations between separated A-events are global consistency constraints on a single actualization and transmit no influence. TRT surrenders value-definiteness for unactualized properties and locality at the level of A's selection; it preserves no-signaling and realism about D and X.

**Order selection.** A actualizes order. The actualized event algebra is the algebra generated by the order-invariant (stem) events of the realized causal set. Preclusion applies only to measure-zero events within the actualized algebra. How matter distinctions enter the actualized algebra is open: the gravitational order-difference criterion has been falsified (working notes §4.5).

**Distinctive claim.** Scoped definiteness, Persistence of Zero, and strong positivity of the quantal measure are not independent postulates (as in the histories programme) but consequences of the triad. TRT keeps classical logic and restricts its domain; it does not revise logic.

## Obligations

1. Establish the necessity and irreducibility of the triad, including resistance to collapse into a single primitive under three aspects.
2. Discharge the open internal obligations:
   - (a) L₃-sufficiency for D, or explicit identification of the contingent specification D carries (Hilbert-space and Hamiltonian structure).
   - (b) The selection rule fixing the actualized algebra. Constraints: must not license preclusion of generic measure-zero events; intrinsic to the growing causal set; uniquely determined. Working rule: order selection. Pointer test: records are stem-expressible; the unrefined rule over-actualizes which-slit distinctions. The gravitational order-difference refinement is falsified: at Planck density G cancels from the signal-to-noise ratio, and the criterion predicts collapse of electron and molecular superpositions more than nineteen orders of magnitude too fast. Working channel: redundancy selection. A distinction is a candidate for actualization if it is recorded in at least two disjoint, causally independent regions of D; A actualizes one branch of the resulting decomposition. Open: a covariant, foliation-free definition of records on the causal set, and the record length scale (derive or declare). Fallback: matter A-events with a declared rate. Covariance: no prior covariant formulation found; proposed repair defines records on the algebras of domains of dependence of mutually spacelike regions, so that by primitive causality the condition is slice-independent. Remaining: minimum-separation and maximum-record-length scales (derive or declare); a covariant notion of recoverability. Causal sets lack a time-slice property; the preferred causal-set route defines records on spacetime regions (Sorkin–Johnston style) and needs no slices, but is conditional on interacting QFT on causal sets, an open problem of causal set theory itself. Working formulation: continuum algebraic QFT.
   - (c) An actualization model whose events constitute spacetime: sequential growth with each A-event a causal-set birth, Bell causality replaced by Persistence of Zero plus strong positivity.
   - (d) Derive the Tsirelson bound from A or I∞ (via strong positivity; alternative: information causality).
   - (d′) Derive Persistence of Zero from L₃ and A. Sketch in working notes.
   - (e) Formally define "condition of actualization," sufficient to prove acyclicity and to serve as the premise of (d′).
3. Derive or tightly constrain core physical structure from the co-primitives without redefining them.

## Failure conditions

TRT in its present form fails if any of the following obtains:

1. **Empirical.** The Co-Admissibility Conjecture is falsified: pointer-state selection under incompatible decoherence channels proceeds in a manner inconsistent with A selecting a single globally L₃-consistent actualization from D (named target: Feller et al. 2020).
2. **Empirical.** Whatever channel actualizes matter distinctions must leave electron and molecular interference intact and actualize macroscopic records. The gravitational order-difference channel fails this test (working notes §4.5); any replacement is subject to the same test and to bounds on collapse models (cf. Donadi et al. 2021).
3. **Structural.** The Tsirelson bound is shown to be unobtainable from any constraint on A or I∞. Then either a fourth principle is required, or quantum structure is wholly contingent specification and the derivational ambition reduces to constraint.

Weakening of claims follows the escalation criteria: logical impossibility proven, empirical falsification clear, or all approaches exhausted and documented.
