# Triadic Reality Theory: Programme Objective

## Objective

Triadic Reality Theory aims to establish that the co-primitives ⟨L₃, I∞, A⟩ are each necessary, and jointly sufficient up to contingent specification, for determinate actuality, and that the constitutive structures of physics (time, geometry, dynamics, mathematical structure) are derivable from or tightly constrained by them.

## Definitions

- **I∞**: unbounded informational representability; the representable domain R of distinctions, relations, states, and configurations.
- **L₃**: ontological logical constraint: Identity, Non-Contradiction, and Excluded Middle (the last scoped to propositions over actualized states).
- **A**: fundamental state change; the primitive transition from admissible to actual, presupposing no physical time, motion, force, energy, or prior actuality. Physical dynamics is ordered A. Unitary evolution is internal structure of D, not an instance of A.

## Architecture

R --L₃ (necessary filter)--> D --A--> X,  with χ ≡ A(I∞ | L₃)

Three tiers: representable (R), admissible (D), actual (X).

## Discreteness commitment

The actual is constituted by individuated A-events (from L₃ Identity) forming a partial order that is irreflexive and acyclic (from L₃ Non-Contradiction together with A's exclusion of prior actuality) and locally finite (an intrinsic structural property of A, not derived from L₃). Continuous dynamics describes the structure of D. Physical time and geometry are to be recovered from the A-order (order) and A-event counts (number), following the causal-set reconstruction programme (Bombelli et al. 1987; Malament 1977), with Lorentz invariance secured as in Bombelli, Henson & Sorkin (2009). Time between A-events is relational, as in Page & Wootters (1983).

## Success conditions

1. Establish the necessity and irreducibility of the triad, including resistance to collapse into a single primitive under three aspects.
2. Discharge the open internal obligations:
   - (a) L₃-sufficiency for D, or explicit identification of the contingent specification D carries beyond logical consistency (Hilbert-space and Hamiltonian structure);
   - (b) the selection rule governing A's resolution from D to X;
   - (c) an actualization model whose events constitute, rather than occupy, spacetime.
3. Derive or tightly constrain core physical structure from the co-primitives without redefining them.
