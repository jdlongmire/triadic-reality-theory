## The ruling

The reconciliation is asymmetric: on each point, the document carrying the precise statement governs.

1. On A, the invariant wording governs. A is fundamental non-temporal state transition/change, not defined as actualization. The charter's "transition from admissible to actual" is read and recorded as a statement of A's architectural role (D --A--> X, within R --L3--> D --A--> X), not its definition. Obtains remains a distinct notion from A.
2. On Excluded Middle, the charter's scoping governs. Excluded Middle (bivalence) binds over propositions over actualized states. The scoping coheres with the programme's typed distinctions (representable, admissible, actualizable, actual) and with the FLL hypothesis, which predicates bivalence of physical informational actualizations; it is adopted as the precise statement and is recorded where the hard core states L3 unscoped.
