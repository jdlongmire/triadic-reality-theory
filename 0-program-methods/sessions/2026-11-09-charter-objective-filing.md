## External item assessed (analogy, recorded as such)

- Item: U.S. Bureau of Industry and Security final rule (Federal Register
  Doc. 2026-22793, 2026-11-09) extending the suspension of the Affiliates
  Rule through 2027-01-10. The rule extends Entity List and Military
  End-User List restrictions to foreign firms 50 percent or more owned by
  listed parties.
- Assessment: the rule has no evidential bearing on this programme or on
  LRT. It touches no obligation and no failure condition in the charter.
  Three structural analogies were noted for teaching use only:
  (1) scoped Excluded Middle: the proposition that a given firm will be
  restricted on 2027-01-11 concerns an unactualized state, and the rule's
  automatic-reinstatement clause supplies determinacy by stipulation
  rather than by actualization; (2) the 50 percent ownership threshold is
  contingent specification of the admissible set, not a deliverance of
  logical necessity, and its identity attribution is conventional where
  L3 Identity is ontological; (3) the list regime operates as an
  admissibility filter over representable transactions, with the
  suspension changing the filter's extension rule while the underlying
  list is unchanged.
- Caveat recorded with the analogies: a regulator's act is not an instance
  of the primitive A. A is the transition from admissible to actual and
  presupposes no prior actuality; a Federal Register publication is an
  event within the actual. The mapping is analogy of structure, never
  instantiation.
