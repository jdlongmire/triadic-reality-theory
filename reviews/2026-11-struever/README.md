## Why this paper, in the paper's own terms

- It argues a monoprimitive ontology: a methodological reduction (irreducibility, deductive sufficiency, realizability) is claimed to force exactly one primitive, named **Capacity**, from which distinction, binding, propagation, and relation are derived.
- Its **Methodological Proposition 01** argues that two independent primitives cannot constitute a complete connected foundation, because their mutual relation would require an additional independent premise. That argument bears directly on TRT's co-primitive triad and on WP-TRT-CORE-0001 (co-primitive normalization): whether the triad's members are "independent" in the sense the proposition attacks, and what work the charter's common-Source commitment does against it.
- It claims a completed **Ontological Closure Condition**: all conditions necessary for configurational realizability in one deductively connected structure, with ontology as a finished task and domain mapping as the open-ended remainder.
- Stated limit, in the author's own words: the article does **not** reproduce the mathematical closure expression or its full derivation ("protected mathematical kernel"). The derivation claim is therefore not examinable from this text; the methodology and the claimed structure are.
