## What is declined, and why

**1. The ontology recast — adds nothing past v0.9.** The triad is TRT's own L₃ / I∞ / A. The document's two-tier engine is coarser than TRT's four-tier one and reintroduces circularity- and Non-Contradiction-misuse hazards TRT already closed. Accepting it would be a step *down* the rigor ladder.

**2. The categorical-quantum formalism (dagger SMC, idempotent-monad decoherence, ZX/ZH) — wrong corner, wrong horn.** Its centerpiece (decoherence as a structure-preserving idempotent) is the re-described einselection that PRD-001 already recorded NEGATIVE. The dagger-compact toolkit bakes in the inner product and so tilts toward the quadratic horn — the side the appraisal log already shows under degeneration pressure. Declined per the negative heuristic.

**3. The new scope (emitter-recycler, black-hole recyclers, generative cosmology, consciousness) — belt-inflation.** New conjectures with no failure conditions, proposed when the discipline is to *close* routes and formalize the root. Declined per the ROADMAP's standing position that exposition is not the bottleneck.

**One graft: a sheaf-cohomological probe of the keystone (OPN-002).** Logged at [`../../2-theory/00-foundational/actualization-resolution-sheaf-probe.md`](../../2-theory/00-foundational/actualization-resolution-sheaf-probe.md). Scoped as an *optional, lower-priority* formal vehicle for sub-route 1 of the keystone, below the live commutant/gravity work, with its own failure condition and a cheap first step (verify the primary; confirm the possibilistic obstruction is well-defined on the Feller structure).

## One correction this triage records (ZX → sheaf)

An interim suggestion (in the relay conversation) to use **ZX-calculus** as the keystone probe was **withdrawn** and corrected to **sheaf cohomology**. ZX is dagger-compact and carries the inner product, so it can only express the quadratic horn; it cannot neutrally represent the metric-free linear horn. The correction is recorded in the sub-route doc and is the reason the graft is sheaf-theoretic, not diagrammatic-quantum. (Bayesian self-correction: the prior was revised the moment the metric-dependence of the two horns was traced through.)
