What would strengthen TRT is a formalized, discriminating criterion that either recovers a known piece of physics non-trivially or generates a novel prediction that can be (and ideally is) corroborated. Ontology alone, however coherent, does not move the program past “Unappraised.”
Given the shared premise that QM already deploys the three co-primitives, the leverage lies in showing that the triad does more than redescribe existing theory.
Highest-leverage near-term moves
	1	Resolve the co-admissibility formalization (the original Task 0.1 root)
The decoherence/pointer-selection route has already recorded a negative (the candidates re-described einselection). The remaining live path is the gravity arm of the same keystone (OPN-002): whether the natural measure of co-admissibility / cycle-closure failure on a relational structure is linear (raw obstruction → exact GR via Regge + Lovelock) or quadratic (best-fit residual → Bianconi-class higher-curvature corrections).
A definite answer—linear or quadratic—derived from the structure of L₃ rather than imposed by hand would be the first theoretically progressive step that carries observational content (presence or absence of higher-curvature corrections at high curvature).
	2	Make the FLL physical hypothesis operational
The program has already elevated “physical actualization is bivalent” (no irreducible both/neither outcomes) to a governing empirical hypothesis with an explicit falsifier: one reproducible irreducible B or N actualization.
Strengthening requires a contrastive, apparatus-complete protocol (or a clean negative disposition) that can be run against existing data or a proposed experiment. Local Friendliness-type inequalities are flagged as a candidate frontier; turning that into a concrete, TRT-specific likelihood ratio would raise the status from “ontological schema” to “proposed physical principle under test.”
	3	Produce one non-trivial recovery or derivation
Even before novel predictions, a formal criterion that recovers, from the triad alone:
	◦	the Born measure (or a precise reason why it must be postulated),
	◦	the Einstein–Hilbert action (conditional on the linearity result), or
	◦	Hilbert-space structure / unitary evolution in a limit
would convert the claim “QM relies on the primitives” into “the primitives generate (part of) QM.”
	4	Tighten the Lean core and close open sufficiency obligations
The existing Lean formalization already refuses to encode L₃-sufficiency. Extending it so that proposed bridges (co-admissibility measure, actualization-rate scalar, etc.) are either proved or explicitly marked as open assumptions would make the protective belt machine-checkable and reduce the risk of silent redefinition of the hard core.
What would not strengthen it
	•	Additional philosophical elaboration of the triad (the roadmap already notes that three independent reviews converged on this being past the bottleneck).
	•	Categorical or diagrammatic recasts that re-describe decoherence or measurement without new discriminating content (one such proposal was already declined for this reason).
	•	Inflating confidence labels or removing failure conditions to protect existing claims.
Bottom line
TRT is strengthened by turning the three co-primitives into a computable filter or measure that makes a definite, potentially false claim about either (a) the linear-vs-quadratic gravity fork or (b) the empirical exclusion of non-bivalent actualizations. Until one of those (or an equivalent discriminating descendant) is formalized and either corroborated or cleanly falsified, the program remains a disciplined candidate ontology that correctly observes that QM presupposes the triad, but has not yet shown that the triad does independent physical work.
