## Answers required by WP-TRT-LCC-0001

### 1. What observable raw-data pattern counts as $\chi_\varnothing$?

None is specified. Every possible retained record falls into `0`, `1`, known/unknown physical
anomaly, invalid trial, or unclassified record. No record falls outside physical actuality merely
because the classifier lacks a model for it.

### 2. Why is that pattern not another physical outcome or equipment/data failure?

The submission supplies no positive criterion establishing this. It supplies exclusions. An
observed residual must be added to the physical outcome/anomaly model; an absent or corrupted
record is a record-chain failure. The required distinction is therefore not achieved.

### 3. Why is $C_L$ certified independently of the alleged outcome?

It is not. Temporal ordering of classifiers is insufficient when $C_P$ and $C_R$ use the same
outcome-bearing analog records. More strongly, outcome-informative redundancy contradicts the
candidate criteria, while outcome-neutral redundancy cannot establish $I_{\mathrm{irr}}$.

### 4. What result differs from standard complete quantum measurement?

None. A complete POVM already assigns unit total probability to its exhaustive outcome space. A
new observed residual becomes another POVM effect or shows that the old model was incomplete. The
submitted zero-probability claim therefore does not discriminate LCC/TRT from standard theory.

## Why reclassification, not deletion

The proposal exposed a real debt in the LRM: TRT names the outcome-actual boundary but has no
instrument-level criterion for observing failure to cross it. Keeping that debt explicit prevents
future drafts from turning POVM normalization or unexplained readout residuals into claimed
evidence for actualization. The conceptual probe earns tier 2; the proposed experiment does not
earn tier 3.
