# Stage-3 Red-Team — Non-Bivalent Rival Model Attempt

- **Date:** 2026-08-28
- **Target:** [`non-bivalent-rival-model-attempt.md`](../../2-theory/02-variational/non-bivalent-rival-model-attempt.md)
- **Verdict:** **conditional no-go survives; unrestricted no-go and tier-3 promotion do not**

## Attack 1 — labels are not semantics

Calling two instrument outcomes `both` and `neither` does not make them truth-value gluts or gaps.
The construction now makes this its central failure rather than presenting a four-outcome POVM as
a non-bivalent physical theory.

## Attack 2 — trace loss can be physical

A trace-decreasing operation is legitimate as one branch of an instrument. It becomes a gap only
if the complete trial has no other branch. At the complete-instrument level its complement is an
outcome, whether observed directly or carried by an environment. The dichotomy is therefore about
complete trials, not a denial of postselection or lossy subchannels.

## Attack 3 — the argument may smuggle in classical logic

The conclusion is explicitly conditional on normalized operational probabilities and complete
tests, requirements needed to compare predicted frequencies. It does not infer the FLL from those
requirements. A non-Kolmogorov or non-bivalent event algebra remains open if it provides its own
normalization, composition, and record semantics.

## Attack 4 — generalized probabilistic theories escape Hilbert space

They do, but operational-probabilistic frameworks still organize physical experiments as tests
with outcome probabilities. Adding `gap` and `glut` to such a test recreates the semantic bridge;
omitting them leaves no likelihood for their records. This is an identified research constraint,
not a theorem over every GPT.

## Attack 5 — paraconsistent quantum mechanics is a rival

It is a logical/metaphysical rival. The cited programme locates contradiction in potential powers
and does not derive a different actual record likelihood. It cannot yet power this discriminator.

## Final calibration

The strongest minimal physical construction failed constructively: when made predictive it becomes
an expanded outcome theory; when kept irreducibly gap/glut-like it ceases to be a complete
likelihood model. This is a useful negative result and narrows the model-building target. It does
not close `OPN-009`, prove universal bivalence, or authorize tier 3.
