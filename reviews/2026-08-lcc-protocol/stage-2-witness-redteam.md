# Stage-2 Red-Team — Global FLL Closure Witness

- **Date:** 2026-08-28
- **Target:** [`global FLL closure witness`](../../2-theory/02-variational/fll-closure-witness.md)
- **Verdict:** **candidate inequality survives as an auxiliary-model witness; direct-FLL and
  tier-3 readings do not yet survive**

## Attack 1 — the witness tests a conjunction, not FLL alone

An excess rejects global bivalence only jointly with context identity, closure, response
calibration, and the causal independence/error model. Any one can fail. The document now states
this explicitly, so the inequality survives; calling a violation direct evidence against FLL does
not.

## Attack 2 — redundancy is correlated by construction

Every fragment shares the target system and the interaction that creates its record. Conditional
independence after fixing a latent outcome removes ordinary downstream errors but not leakage,
mapping-pulse failure, drift, or a common environment. Those can produce all-gap/all-glut patterns
at first order rather than $\epsilon^R$.

**Required change:** treat $2\epsilon^R$ as a reference special case. A real protocol must bound the
joint forbidden-event probability from a causal calibration model that includes common modes.
Applied.

## Attack 3 — independent affirmation of a proposition and its negation is nontrivial

In classical logic $\neg P$ is the complement of $P$, not a second unrelated observable. Two
physical detectors can both fire or both remain silent without the proposition being both true and
false or neither. The inferential bridge depends on operational equivalence between the report
channels and the propositions, including matched efficiencies and exclusivity controls.

**Required change:** supply a one-hot ancilla mapping and label the remaining operational-equivalence
debt. Applied at candidate level; hardware proof remains open.

## Attack 4 — contextuality is an attractive but invalid shortcut

Kochen–Specker violations reject joint noncontextual valuation assumptions across measurement
contexts. They do not demonstrate a glut or gap for one proposition in one completed context. The
witness document draws this boundary correctly.

**Outcome:** survives, provided no contextuality result is cited as prior FLL violation.

## Attack 5 — the rival logics are not rival physical models

Paraconsistency is a property of consequence; dialetheism and paracompleteness are semantic views;
the cited paraconsistent quantum-superposition programme is interpretive and does not provide a
distinct probability for coherent post-closure gaps/gluts. Without a contrastive likelihood, the
witness predicts the same no-excess result as ordinary quantum measurement.

**Outcome:** blocks tier 3. The witness is a valid severe-test *form*, not yet a discriminating
physics prediction.

## Attack 6 — finite records do not certify unrestricted globality

Any experiment samples a finite causal record architecture. It can test the claim that FLL governs
that completed context and can pressure a universal by counterexample. It cannot verify global FLL
from repeated confirmations. The asymmetry is Popperian and acceptable if stated: one sound
violation refutes the bridge; zero violations only tighten an implementation-relative bound.

## Attack 7 — identity is partly built into the experiment

Event IDs, clock windows, and wiring define which reports are treated as concerning “the same
thing.” A failure of that bookkeeping is not an ontological violation of Identity. Identity must be
a separately audited antecedent, not inferred from report agreement.

**Outcome:** identity functions primarily as a closure/typing gate in this witness. The first
empirical targets are glut and gap structures (Non-Contradiction and Excluded Middle), not a direct
test of $x=x$.

## Final calibration

The principal challenge is upheld: a positive discriminator can be constructed, and the earlier
impossibility-style conclusion is withdrawn. The current artifact earns tier 2 because its
inequality is explicit and reproducible. It does not earn `PRD-*` status until the causal joint
bound and a rival physical likelihood are supplied.
