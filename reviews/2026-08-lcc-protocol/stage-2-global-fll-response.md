# Stage-2 Response — Global Effect of the Three FLL

## Challenge

JD challenged the first-pass terminal assessment: sustained alternatives to classical logic make
violations conceivable, so there should be some discriminator available for globally operative
Identity, Non-Contradiction, and Excluded Middle.

## Disposition of the challenge

**Accepted.** The first pass made a category mistake in reverse. It correctly denied that literal
nonactualization could simply be inserted as an ordinary third POVM outcome, but then treated an
ordinary detector label as the only possible positive evidence. A structured actual meta-record
can witness a gap or glut in the target outcome valuation.

## What survives from the first review

- The submitted draft did not specify such a witness.
- Its closure and outcome classifiers reused outcome-bearing records without a sufficient
  independence account.
- Complete-POVM normalization alone is not a TRT-specific prediction.
- Unstructured missing data remain apparatus/data events, not nonactualization evidence.

## What is overturned

- It is false that every observable residual must be interpreted only as another target outcome.
  A record may be evidence *about the valuation status* of the target proposition.
- Redundancy need not mean multiple copies of `0` or `1`; it can mean independent agreement on a
  gap (`00`) or glut (`11`) status.
- The absence of a discriminator in the submitted draft does not establish the impossibility of a
  discriminator.

## New result

The Stage-2 analysis derives an error-bounded forbidden-correlation inequality for dual affirmative
reports of $P$ and $\neg P$. Under a closed global-bivalent model and conditionally independent
calibrated fragments,

$$
p_{\mathrm{gap}}+p_{\mathrm{glut}}\leq B_{\mathrm{FLL}}.
$$

The exact bound and reproducible reference calculation live in
[`global-bivalent-closure-witness-under-fll.md`](../../2-theory/02-variational/global-bivalent-closure-witness-under-fll.md).

This establishes that a discriminator **can** be called on. It does not yet establish a physical
implementation or a rival theory that predicts violation. The WP is therefore reopened and remains
tier 2 rather than returning immediately to tier 3.

## Red-team boundary

Contextuality is precedent for the witness method, not evidence that FLL has already failed.
Kochen–Specker inequalities reject specified context-independent joint valuations across multiple
contexts. The proposed GBC witness restricts itself to one completed physical context and tests
whether its actual records admit a coherent bivalent valuation. Conflating those claims would
trivialize both contextuality and the global-bivalence-under-FLL thesis.
