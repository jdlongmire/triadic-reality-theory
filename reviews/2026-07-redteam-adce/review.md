## Steelman

ADCE's strongest form: causal set theory already commits to "Number = Geometry" at Planckian density, and already hosts one dark-energy mechanism (everpresent Λ) driven by the *fluctuation* δN ~ √N. It is a legitimate, well-posed dynamical question whether the *mean* growth rate can instead carry the signal — the two mechanisms have different failure conditions, and the mean-growth branch is genuinely unoccupied territory on a substrate with forty years of credibility. v3 is unusually honest for a speculative note: it names its axiom (A-SAT), names its coincidence problem, names its rival's derived advantages, pre-registers seventeen failure criteria, and repeatedly warns against mistaking dictionary identities for results (§6). The triadic ontology supplies what CST deliberately omits: an account of what an element-birth *is*. If a CSG-based growth law were derived that produced matter-era deceleration, late acceleration, the observed magnitude, and a distinguishable w(z), this would be a major result. The paper claims none of that yet, and says so.

The steelman was attacked in that form. Several attacks land anyway, because they are internal: the paper's own v3 equations refute the paper's own v3 prose.

## Vector-by-vector

**1 — The static-universe hole: 4-volume counting destroys the central geometric hypothesis (§8). REFUTED (as stated). HIGH.**

The abstract and §8 assert: "continued irreversible actualization necessarily changes the geometry of realized spacetime." Under v3's own adopted resolution (§9.2–9.3, N = ρ₄V₄ with V₄ = V_c∫a³dt′), this is false, and provably so from the paper's own equations. Verified symbolically: Ṅ = ρ₄V_c a³ > 0 for **any** a(t) > 0. A static universe (a = const) accumulates 4-volume, hence N, linearly and forever, at saturated density, with zero metric evolution. A *contracting* universe also has Ṅ > 0. Constant-rate actualization ⟺ exactly a static universe. The v2 forcing argument ("bounded density forces geometric enlargement") had content only under 3-volume counting — the very counting §9.2 discards to escape Bousso. Under the fix, the capacity bound plus continued actualization constrain a(t) not at all; expansion maps to *acceleration* of the count (H = N̈/3Ṅ), and cosmic acceleration to a jerk condition (3ṄN⃛ > 2N̈², verified). The Bousso escape was purchased at the price of the central hypothesis, and the paper does not notice the transaction. **Consequence: falsification criterion 3 ("realized causal growth does not imply any necessary metric evolution") has arguably already fired against the v3 formulation** — a criterion firing without the document registering it is itself a discipline finding (see vector 9). Change-the-verdict: rewrite §8 as a hypothesis about *why the actualization rate accelerates* (third-derivative structure), or supply a back-reaction argument that a static geometry cannot sustain actualization. Neither is present.

**2 — A-SAT: coherent as an axiom, but it relocates the smuggle rather than fixing it, and inverts the direction of explanation. Coherence SURVIVES; "naming fixes the smuggle" REFUTED. MED-HIGH.**

At saturation, N ≡ ρ₄,max V₄ exactly. Fork: either (i) geometry determines Ṅ via the dictionary — then §10's "what determines the growth law?" is answered trivially by geometry, and ADCE is re-labeled CST kinematics (its own criteria 11–12); or (ii) a microphysical Ṅ determines geometry — but then A-SAT quietly asserts that geometry *responds* so as to hold ρ₄ at its bound while N grows, which **is** the ungrounded growth-to-geometry content of §8/Target 4. The conclusion is in the premise: A-SAT presupposes the theorem it was introduced to enable. This is the same smuggle §9.1 says v2 committed, moved one derivative up and given a name. Naming an axiom is progress in honesty (criterion 4 correctly gates it), but §9.1's framing — that stating A-SAT repairs the argument — overclaims: even *granting* A-SAT, vector 1 shows nothing forces expansion. A-SAT is simultaneously still-smuggling (direction ii) and insufficient (vector 1). Change-the-verdict: a derivation of A-SAT from an extremal principle *that also derives the geometric response*, i.e. Target 3 and Target 4 jointly. The paper's candidate (Krishna & Mathew-style entropy maximization) is a fair lead, unexecuted.

**3 — Equivocation on N: Planck-sprinkling elements vs. physical actualization events. REFUTED (the dual use is incoherent as written). HIGH on structure; MEDIUM on the numeric gap.**

The paper needs N to be two things. For the Bousso escape and A-SAT's "Planckian sprinkling density" (§9.2), N is CST's element count: one per Planck 4-volume ≈ 2.2×10⁻¹⁴⁸ m³·s, i.e. ~10¹⁴⁷ elements per m³ per second. For the ontology, §4's working definition counts decays, molecular transitions, decohered records, biological records — physical record-forming events whose 4-density is generously ~10⁴⁵ /m³·s (decoherence-rate scale) and for most named instances vastly lower: **at least ~100 orders of magnitude below saturation.** So: if N = CST elements, A-SAT is just the CST dictionary, and §4's event taxonomy, §13's microphysics, and Target 1's actualization criterion are dynamically irrelevant to N — the "ontological grounding" (§2.5 novelty item 1) is an interpretive gloss on unchanged kinematics, which is CONTRIBUTING's canonical degenerating move (interpretation ≠ derivation). If N = physical actualization events, A-SAT is empirically false by ~100 orders and the Planckian-density Bousso escape needs re-arguing at the actual event density. The two readings cannot both be operative, and the paper oscillates between them — the safety claims borrow one reading, the novelty claims the other. Change-the-verdict: an explicit two-level structure (Planck-scale substrate count vs. coarse-grained record count) with a stated bridge law; nothing of the kind is in the text.

**4 — Bousso escape incomplete: persistent records re-import spatial entropy accounting. UNCERTAIN, leaning against. MEDIUM.**

§4 *requires* each actualization to leave a persistent causal relation or record. Persistent records exist at later instants in space; the record content of a spatial region at time t then scales with the 4-volume of its causal past, which outgrows boundary area for large regions — the area-bound tension reappears at the record layer even though the *event counting* dwells in 4-volume. The escape is clean only for CST's reading of N (elements *are* spacetime, carrying no entropy in it) — precisely the reading under which ADCE adds nothing (vector 3). Criterion 17 is the right failure condition, but the paper presents it as hypothetical; it is already visibly live from §4's own wording. Change-the-verdict: show records may merge/coarse-grain so that retrievable information per 3-region respects area bounds while the event count remains irreversible (record ≠ standing bit) — a real possibility, unargued.

**5 — "Monotone advantage" (§11) collides with the DESI history the paper anchors to (§14). REFUTED under the magnitude reading; UNCERTAIN under the sign-only reading; the paper equivocates between them. HIGH on the math.**

Computed independently: for any CPL history in the paper's cited quadrant (w₀ > −1, w_a < 0, crossing present — e.g. w₀ = −0.75, w_a = −0.86), ρ_DE(a) **rises to a maximum at the crossing (a* ≈ 0.71, z* ≈ 0.41) and has been *decreasing* since** (d ln ρ/d ln a = −3(1+w) flips sign at the crossing; verified symbolically and numerically). Consequences: (a) a Λ_eff monotone in magnitude cannot cross w = −1 at all — so if §11's "monotone rather than sign-fluctuating... potential advantage" means monotone Λ_eff, the mechanism is structurally *unable* to produce the DESI-preferred history, and §14's "phantom crossing is an opportunity" directly contradicts §11's advantage row. (b) If "monotone" means only "does not fluctuate in sign," the advantage claim survives but weakens, and the paper must then explain why the geometric signature of *growing* realized structure is a dark-energy density that peaked at z ≈ 0.4 and is now declining — prima facie the opposite of a growth signature. Either way, DESI DR2 is prima facie **pressure against** mean-growth mechanisms, not the neutral "live target" §14 presents; and since the ρ_A(N) map is entirely underived, "monotone" is an unearned characterization of a nonexistent quantity — the mild form of exactly the identity-as-result sin §6 warns against. Change-the-verdict: define "monotone" precisely; exhibit any toy growth law whose effective ρ_A is non-monotone with fixed sign and state why that is natural for mean growth.

**6 — Magnitude asymmetry understated: the mean-coupled scale is naturally O(1), not 10⁻¹²⁰. SURVIVES as honestly-open, but the table understates. MEDIUM.**

Everpresent Λ derives smallness for free: the signal is the fluctuation, suppressed by 1/√N ~ 10⁻⁶⁰. A mechanism coupled to the *mean* has natural scale ρ₄,max — Planckian, the original cosmological-constant problem — since the mean exceeds the fluctuation by √N ~ 10⁶⁰. "Open (Target 3/5)" in the §11 table is technically honest but conceals that criterion 14's failure mode is the *default* outcome absent a derived suppression mechanism the rival gets by construction. The paper should say: starts ~120 orders wrong, suppression mandatory. Not a refutation — an honesty-of-framing finding.

## Overall verdict

v3 is a genuine progressive step over v2 in Lakatosian terms — it names its rival, its axiom, its coincidence problem, and adds failure criteria; none of that is retracted here, and this is **not** a false refutation of the note as a whole. The determination question OPN-008 poses remains well-posed and worth keeping open. But the repositioning has an unnoticed structural cost: **the 4-volume fork resolution, adopted to escape Bousso, falsifies the paper's own central geometric hypothesis as stated** (vector 1, checkable in three lines from §9.3's equations), the saturation axiom relocates rather than removes the smuggle (vector 2), the paper equivocates on what N counts (vector 3), and the advertised monotone advantage is in unacknowledged tension with the DESI history the paper anchors to (vector 5). Vectors 1, 3, and 5 are internal-consistency failures, not external objections.
